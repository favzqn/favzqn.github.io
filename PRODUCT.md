# Product: Fauzan Fathurrahman Portfolio

## What is this?
Personal portfolio website showcasing full-stack engineering and automation work. Built with Astro, serves as a hub for project case studies, blog posts, and professional experience.

## Who is it for?
- Potential clients (startup founders, business owners looking to hire)
- Recruiters and hiring managers
- Other developers (blog readers)

## Goals
- Showcase real projects with case studies (Problem → Solution → Result)
- Establish credibility as a full-stack engineer and automation specialist
- Convert visitors into inquiries or job opportunities

## Tone
- Professional but not corporate
- Clean and minimal, let the work speak
- No unnecessary decoration

## Tech Stack
- Astro 5 with TypeScript
- Custom CSS (CSS variables for theming)
- Netlify deployment

## Key Pages
- `/` — Bio and introduction
- `/projects` — Grid of case study cards
- `/projects/[slug]` — Individual project detail pages
- `/experience` — Work history
- `/blog` — Technical articles
