## 🎧 Podcasts I Listen To

### Tech & Engineering

**Syntax.fm**  
Web development, hosted by Wes Bos & Scott Tolinski. Great for staying current with web tech.

**Software Engineering Daily**  
In-depth technical interviews. Wide range of topics from databases to distributed systems.

**The Changelog**  
Open source conversations. Interviews with maintainers and contributors.

**Engineering Enablement**  
Developer productivity and platform engineering. Newer podcast but high quality.

---

### Business & Startups

**Acquired**  
Deep dives into company histories. 2-3 hour episodes that feel like 30 minutes.

**My First Million**  
Business ideas and entrepreneurship. Entertaining and educational.

**Lenny's Podcast**  
Product management and growth. Great guests, actionable insights.

---

### General Learning

**Lex Fridman Podcast**  
Long-form conversations with interesting people. AI, philosophy, science, and more.

**Huberman Lab**  
Science-based health and performance. Dense but practical.

**The Tim Ferriss Show**  
World-class performers. Hit or miss depending on the guest, but the hits are incredible.
