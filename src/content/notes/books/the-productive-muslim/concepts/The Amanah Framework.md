# The Amanah Framework

Amanah means "trust" — your body, time, and mind are trusts (amanah) from Allah. Productivity is the responsible stewardship of these trusts.

---

## The Three Trusts

1. **Your Body** — take care of it through sleep, nutrition, and exercise
2. **Your Time** — manage it wisely through planning and prayer
3. **Your Mind** — develop it through learning, reflection, and dhikr

---

## Why It Matters

- You will be asked about how you spent your time, body, and mind
- Productivity is not optional — it is a form of worship
- Neglecting your trusts is a form of ingratitude
- Honoring your trusts brings barakah and inner peace

---

## How to Apply

- **Body**: sleep well, eat halal, exercise regularly
- **Time**: plan your day around salah, use a planner, avoid waste
- **Mind**: read Quran, learn new skills, reflect on your life
- **Balance**: don't over-invest in one trust at the expense of others
