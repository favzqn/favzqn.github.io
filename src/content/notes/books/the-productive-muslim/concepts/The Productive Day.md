# The Productive Day

The Islamic prayer schedule (salah) provides a natural structure for the day — five anchors that divide your time into productive blocks.

---

## The Five Anchors

1. **Fajr** (pre-dawn) — wake, pray, deep work
2. **Dhuhr** (noon) — break, reflect, recharge
3. **Asr** (afternoon) — continue working, wrap up tasks
4. **Maghrib** (sunset) — stop, eat, connect with family
5. **Isha** (night) — wind down, plan, sleep

---

## Why It Works

- Prayer breaks the day into manageable blocks
- Each prayer is a reset — a chance to refocus
- The schedule aligns with natural energy rhythms
- It prevents burnout by building in rest
- It creates consistency — the same schedule every day
