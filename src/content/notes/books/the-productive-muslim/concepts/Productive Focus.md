# Productive Focus

Focus is a form of worship. When you give your full attention to a task, you are honoring Allah's trust in your mind.

---

## Key Ideas

- Distraction is the enemy of productivity and worship
- Deep focus on your work is an act of ibadah (worship)
- Multitasking is a myth — it splits your attention and reduces quality
- The best work comes from single-pointed focus
- "Whatever you do, do it wholeheartedly for Allah"

---

## How to Focus Productively

- **Set intention** — begin with Bismillah
- **Eliminate distractions** — phone on silent, close tabs
- **Work in blocks** — aligned with prayer times
- **Take breaks at prayer** — natural reset points
- **End with shukr** — gratitude for the ability to focus
