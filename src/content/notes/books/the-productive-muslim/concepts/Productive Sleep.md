# Productive Sleep

Sleep is not wasted time — it is an act of worship when done with the right intention. The Productive Muslim sleeps to recharge, not to escape.

---

## Key Ideas

- Sleep is a mercy from Allah — honor it
- The Prophet (peace be upon him) slept early and woke for Fajr
- Quality of sleep matters more than quantity
- Sleep with the intention of waking for worship
- "When you sleep, it is as if you are receiving death"

---

## How to Sleep Productively

- **Sleep early** — after Isha, not after midnight
- **Wake for Fajr** — the most productive sleep cycle
- **Avoid screens before bed** — protect your mind
- **Make dua before sleep** — protect yourself with Quran
- **Sleep with intention** — wake up with purpose
