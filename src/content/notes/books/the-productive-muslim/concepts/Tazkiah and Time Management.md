# Tazkiah and Time Management

Tazkiah is self-purification — the process of cleansing the heart from spiritual diseases. It is the deepest form of productivity because it removes the inner barriers to effective action.

---

## Key Ideas

- The biggest productivity problem is not time — it is the heart
- Spiritual diseases (envy, anger, greed, laziness) destroy productivity
- Tazkiah removes these diseases, freeing you to act effectively
- "He who knows himself knows his Lord"
- Self-purification is the foundation of all other productivity

---

## The Spiritual Diseases and Their Cures

- **Laziness** → cures: tawakkul (trust in Allah), breaking tasks into small steps
- **Anger** → cures: wudu (ablution), patience, remembrance of Allah
- **Envy** → cures: gratitude (shukr), making dua for the one you envy
- **Greed** → cures: sadaqah (charity), contentment (qana'ah)
- **Anxiety** → cures: salat (prayer), dhikr (remembrance), tawakkul
