# Fasting and Productivity

Ramadan is the ultimate productivity training ground. Fasting trains discipline, empathy, and spiritual focus — skills that transfer to the rest of the year.

---

## Key Ideas

- Fasting trains self-control — the foundation of all productivity
- Ramadan is a spiritual reset — a chance to rebuild habits
- The structure of Ramadan (suhoor, iftar, taraweeh) creates natural rhythm
- Fasting purifies the heart — removing distractions and desires
- "Ramadan is not a month of rest — it is a month of training"

---

## The Ramadan Productivity System

1. **Suhoor** — early morning fuel and prayer
2. **Fajr** — the most productive hour of the day
3. **Morning adhkar** — spiritual preparation
4. **Work blocks** — deep work during fasting hours
5. **Iftar** — breaking fast with gratitude
6. **Taraweeh** — evening worship and community
7. **Reflection** — journal and plan for tomorrow
