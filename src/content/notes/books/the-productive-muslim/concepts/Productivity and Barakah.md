# Productivity and Barakah

Barakah is divine blessing that multiplies the benefit of your efforts. The Productive Muslim doesn't just work hard — they seek barakah in their work.

---

## Key Ideas

- Barakah is not just luck — it is a blessing that amplifies results
- You can't earn barakah through effort alone — it comes from Allah
- The conditions for barakah: sincere intention, halal means, consistency
- Productivity without barakah leads to burnout; productivity with barakah leads to sustainability
- "Seek the life of the world through that which Allah has given you, but do not forget your share of the Hereafter"

---

## How to Seek Barakah

- **Start with Bismillah** — begin every task in Allah's name
- **Pray on time** — salah creates barakah in your day
- **Give charity** — wealth increases through giving
- **Be consistent** — small consistent deeds are more beloved than large inconsistent ones
- **Make dua** — ask Allah for barakah in your time, health, and work
