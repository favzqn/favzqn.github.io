# Mohammed Faris Abu

Productivity coach and author known as "Abu Productive." Founder of ProductiveMuslim.com. Pioneer of integrating Islamic spirituality with modern productivity science.

---

## Key Ideas

- Productivity is not about doing more — it's about doing what matters with barakah
- Your body, time, and mind are trusts (amanah) from Allah
- Fasting, prayer, and dhikr are productivity tools, not just religious rituals
- The Islamic prayer schedule is the original time management system
- Self-purification (tazkiah) is the deepest form of productivity

---

## Background

- Grew up in the UK, studied at Islamic University of Madinah
- Worked in corporate productivity before founding ProductiveMuslim.com
- Trains organizations, companies, and individuals in Islamic productivity
- Has helped millions of Muslims integrate faith and productivity
