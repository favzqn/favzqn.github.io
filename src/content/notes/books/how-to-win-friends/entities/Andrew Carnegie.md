# Andrew Carnegie

Scottish-American industrialist and philanthropist (1835–1919). Built a steel empire and gave away most of his fortune. Dale Carnegie was not related to him but took his last name professionally.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Put all your eggs in one basket — and watch that basket"
- "Do not look for approval except for the consciousness of doing your best"
- "The man who acquires the ability to take full possession of his own mind may take possession of anything else to which he is justly entitled"
- Leadership is about people, not products

---

## Legacy

- Built Carnegie Steel Company, sold to J.P. Morgan for $480 million
- Funded over 2,500 public libraries worldwide
- Established Carnegie Mellon University, Carnegie Hall, Carnegie Corporation
- His life is the "rags to riches" story that inspired Dale Carnegie
- His philosophy: "The man who dies rich dies disgraced"
