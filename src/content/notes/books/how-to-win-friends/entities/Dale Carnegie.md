# Dale Carnegie

American author and self-help guru (1888–1955). Author of *How to Win Friends and Influence People* (1936), one of the best-selling books of all time.

---

## Key Contributions

- Founded the Dale Carnegie Training organization
- Pioneered the self-help genre with practical, actionable advice
- Taught courses on public speaking, sales, and interpersonal skills
- His methods are used by Fortune 500 companies worldwide
- *How to Win Friends and Influence People* has sold over 30 million copies

---

## Core Ideas

- Don't criticize, condemn, or complain
- Give honest and sincere appreciation
- Arouse in the other person an eager want
- Become genuinely interested in other people
- Make the other person feel important
- Avoid arguments and show respect for opinions

---

## Background

- Born in Maryville, Missouri
- Worked as a traveling salesman before becoming a teacher
- Started teaching public speaking at a YMCA in New York
- Developed his course into a worldwide training organization
- Wrote several books, but *How to Win Friends* is his masterpiece
