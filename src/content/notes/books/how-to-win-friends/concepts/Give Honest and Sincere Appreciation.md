# Give Honest and Sincere Appreciation

People crave appreciation more than food, warmth, or shelter. The deepest human desire is to be appreciated and valued.

---

## Key Ideas

- "The deepest principle in human nature is the craving to be appreciated"
- "Be hearty in your approbation and lavish in your praise"
- Appreciation must be honest and sincere — flattery is transparent and cheap
- People will do remarkable things for recognition
- "Talk to people about themselves — and they will listen for hours"

---

## Appreciation vs Flattery

- **Appreciation** is genuine, specific, and from the heart
- **Flattery** is vague, self-serving, and transparent
- "Flattery is telling the other person precisely what he thinks about himself"
- "Everyone appreciates appreciation — nobody appreciates flattery"
- The key difference: sincerity

---

## How to Practice

- **Be specific** — "I love how you handled that client" vs "good job"
- **Be timely** — praise immediately, don't wait
- **Be sincere** — only say what you mean
- **Be public** — praise in front of others when appropriate
- **Be generous** — don't hold back appreciation
