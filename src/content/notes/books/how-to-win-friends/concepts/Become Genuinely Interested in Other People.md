# Become Genuinely Interested in Other People

"You can make more friends in two months by becoming interested in other people than you can in two years by trying to get other people interested in you."

---

## Key Ideas

- Interest begets interest — people like those who like them
- Curiosity about others is the foundation of charisma
- Ask questions, listen to answers, remember details
- People are fascinating if you bother to look
- "You can make more friends in two months by becoming interested in other people than you can in two years by trying to get other people interested in you"

---

## How to Practice

- **Ask open-ended questions** — "What do you enjoy about your work?"
- **Listen without interrupting** — give them space to speak
- **Remember details** — follow up on what they told you
- **Be curious** — every person has a story
- **Show genuine interest** — not polite curiosity, but real fascination

---

## The Paradox

- When you stop trying to be interesting, you become interesting
- When you stop trying to be liked, you become likable
- Interest in others is the most attractive quality
- Curiosity is the opposite of self-consciousness
