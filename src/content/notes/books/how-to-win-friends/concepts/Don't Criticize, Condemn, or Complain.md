# Don't Criticize, Condemn, or Complain

Carnegie's first and most fundamental principle: criticism is futile because it puts a person on the defensive and wounds their precious pride.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Criticism is futile because it puts a person on the defensive and usually wounds his or her pride"
- "Any fool can criticize, condemn and complain — and most fools do"
- "Of all animals, man is the only one that is cruel — he is the only one that inflicts pain for the pleasure of doing it"
- Criticism rarely changes behavior — it just creates resentment
- People don't criticize themselves, even when they deserve it

---

## Why Criticism Fails

- It puts people on the defensive
- It wounds their pride and sense of importance
- It arouses resentment and kills motivation
- It never produces lasting change
- It makes the critic feel superior, which makes the recipient feel inferior

---

## What to Do Instead

- **Understand** why people do what they do — empathy first
- **Tolerate** ignorance — people may not know better
- **Practice patience** — criticism is impatience
- **Look for the good** — and mention it
- **Be generous** — generous praise, not stingy criticism
