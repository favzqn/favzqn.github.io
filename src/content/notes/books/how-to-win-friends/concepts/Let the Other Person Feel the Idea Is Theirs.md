# Let the Other Person Feel the Idea Is Theirs

People love their own ideas. If you want to persuade someone, guide them to your conclusion and let them believe they thought of it.

---

## Key Ideas

- "People are more enthusiastic about things they have thought of than things suggested by others"
- "People are more loyal to their own ideas than to ideas imposed on them"
- "Let the other person feel the idea is theirs — and they will embrace it"
- The best leaders guide without directing
- "A suggestion is not a suggestion unless the person to whom it is made feels it is his own"

---

## How It Works

- People defend their own ideas fiercely
- Ideas imposed from outside meet resistance
- Ideas that feel like one's own are embraced enthusiastically
- The best persuasion is invisible — the other person feels free
- "Suggestions should be made in a way that they come from the other person's mind, not yours"

---

## How to Practice

- **Ask leading questions** — "Have you considered...?"
- **Plant seeds** — let ideas grow in their mind
- **Give credit** — "That's a great idea"
- **Guide, don't direct** — steer without controlling
- **Be patient** — let them arrive at the conclusion
