# Avoid Arguments

The only way to win an argument is to avoid it. Arguments are unwinnable — even if you win, you lose.

---

## Key Ideas

- "The only way to get the best of an argument is to avoid it"
- You cannot win an argument — you can only make the other person feel defeated
- Arguments create resentment and kill relationships
- "Nine times out of ten, an argument ends with each party more firmly convinced than ever that he is absolutely right"
- "A man convinced against his opinion is of the same opinion still"

---

## Why Arguments Fail

- People don't argue with logic — they argue with emotion
- Winning an argument makes the other person feel inferior
- Losing an argument makes them resentful
- Both outcomes damage the relationship
- "You can't win an argument. You can't win because if you lose it, you lose it; and if you win it, you lose it"

---

## What to Do Instead

- **Avoid arguments entirely** — walk away before you start
- **Show respect for their opinion** — even when you disagree
- **Listen** — understand before trying to be understood
- **Find common ground** — agree where you can
- **Let them save face** — dignity matters more than being right
