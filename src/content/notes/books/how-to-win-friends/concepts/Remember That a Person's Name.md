# Remember That a Person's Name

A person's name is the sweetest sound in any language. Remembering and using someone's name is a powerful act of recognition.

---

## Key Ideas

- "A person's name is to that person the sweetest and most important sound in any language"
- "Remember that a name is the sweetest sound that can come to a person's ear"
- "The average person is more interested in their own name than in all the other names on earth put together"
- Remembering names shows respect and attention
- People feel valued when you remember their name

---

## Why It Works

- A name is identity — remembering it honors the person
- It shows you were paying attention
- It distinguishes you from everyone who forgot
- It creates an immediate bond
- "People are so proud of their name that they strive to perpetuate it"

---

## How to Practice

- **Repeat the name** when you first hear it: "Nice to meet you, Sarah"
- **Use it in conversation** — but not excessively
- **Write it down** after meeting someone
- **Ask about spelling** if unsure — shows care
- **Connect the name to something** — a mental image or story
