# Show Respect for the Other Person's Opinion

"Never say 'you're wrong' to anyone." People have a right to their own opinions, even if they're mistaken.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Never tell a person he is wrong — you only succeed in stripping that person of self-esteem"
- "If you are going to prove anything, don't let anybody know it"
- "Show respect for the other person's opinion. Never say 'you're wrong'"
- People are not rational beings — they are emotional beings with rationalizations
- Respecting their opinion doesn't mean agreeing with it

---

## The Principle

- People hold their opinions as part of their identity
- Saying "you're wrong" attacks their identity
- Even if you win the argument, you lose the relationship
- "Any fool can criticize, condemn and complain"
- Respect opens doors; criticism closes them

---

## How to Practice

- **Listen fully** before responding
- **Acknowledge** their point: "I see why you think that"
- **Share your view** as an alternative, not a correction
- **Be humble** — your opinion might be wrong
- **Preserve dignity** — let them save face
