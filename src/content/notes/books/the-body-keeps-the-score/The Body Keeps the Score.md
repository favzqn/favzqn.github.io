# The Body Keeps the Score

*The Body Keeps the Score: Brain, Mind, and Body in the Healing of Trauma* by Bessel van der Kolk (2014).

---

## Part I: The Rediscovery of Trauma

Trauma fundamentally reshapes the brain and body. Van der Kolk argues that clinicians must listen to survivors' stories rather than relying solely on diagnostic labels. Childhood abuse and neglect fragment normal development, creating lasting imprints on mind and physiology.

## Part II: This Is Your Brain on Trauma

Trauma shatters the integrated sense of self. Traumatic memories are stored differently from normal memories — they are imprinted as sensory fragments rather than coherent narratives. The amygdala becomes hyperactive (the "watchdog"), while the prefrontal cortex goes offline. [[Dissociation]] and depersonalization are survival responses that allow the mind to escape unbearable experience.

## Part III: The Minds of Children

Secure attachment is the foundation of resilience. Children need consistent, caring relationships to develop normally. When those are absent, the brain develops around survival rather than growth. Van der Kolk advocates for a diagnosis of developmental trauma to better serve abused children.

## Part IV: The Embodiment of Trauma

The body speaks the language of trauma through chronic pain, digestive issues, and autoimmune conditions. Olfactory memories can trigger flashbacks instantly. Body-based therapies — including [[Yoga for Trauma Recovery]], [[EMDR and Trauma Processing]], and [[Neurofeedback for Trauma]] — help survivors reclaim ownership of their bodies.

## Part V: Paths to Recovery

- [[EMDR and Trauma Processing]] — bilateral stimulation to reprocess traumatic memories
- [[Yoga for Trauma Recovery]] — restoring interoception and body ownership
- [[Theater and Trauma]] — dramatherapy and reclaiming narrative
- [[Neurofeedback for Trauma]] — retraining brain electrical patterns
- [[Safe Enough to Feel]] — creating conditions for healing

## Part VI: Finding Your Voice

Telling your story — [[The Power of Disclosure]] — is a critical step in recovery. Secrets keep trauma alive; disclosure transforms it. Recovery means [[Reclaiming Agency]]: moving from victim to survivor to thriver.
