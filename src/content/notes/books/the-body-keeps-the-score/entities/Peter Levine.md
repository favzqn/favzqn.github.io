# Peter Levine

American psychologist and developer of Somatic Experiencing (SE), a body-oriented approach to trauma healing. Author of *Waking the Tiger* (1997).

---

## Key Contributions

- Developed Somatic Experiencing, a body-based trauma therapy
- Observed that animals in the wild discharge survival energy naturally
- Humans "lock" survival energy in the body due to social conditioning
- SE works by gradually releasing trapped survival energy

---

## Core Ideas

- Trauma is uncompleted survival energy trapped in the body
- Animals shake and tremble after a threat; humans suppress this
- The body must discharge fight-or-flight energy to complete the trauma cycle
- Titration — approach traumatic sensations slowly, in small doses
