# Bessel van der Kolk

Dutch psychiatrist and one of the world's leading researchers on trauma. Professor at Boston University School of Medicine. Author of *The Body Keeps the Score* (2014).

---

## Key Contributions

- Pioneered neuroimaging studies of trauma (PET scans of PTSD patients)
- Coined the concept of "developmental trauma disorder"
- Advocated for body-based approaches to trauma recovery
- Demonstrated that trauma is encoded in the body, not just the mind
- Championed EMDR, yoga, neurofeedback, and theater as healing tools

---

## Core Ideas

- Trauma is a body-based condition, not just a psychological one
- The "talking cure" alone is insufficient for trauma
- Safety and connection are prerequisites for healing
- The brain can change at any age (neuroplasticity)
