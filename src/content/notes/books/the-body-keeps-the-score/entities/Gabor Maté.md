# Gabor Maté

Hungarian-Canadian physician and author specializing in trauma, addiction, and stress. Author of *In the Realm of Hungry Ghosts* (2008) and *The Myth of Normal* (2022).

---

## Key Contributions

- Connected childhood trauma to addiction and chronic illness
- Argued that addiction is not a disease but a response to unresolved pain
- Developed a compassionate, trauma-informed approach to treating addicts
- Linked modern society's "normal" to widespread trauma

---

## Core Ideas

- All addiction originates in childhood pain and unmet needs
- "The question is not 'Why the addiction?' but 'Why the pain?'"
- Trauma is not what happens to you but what happens inside you
- Society normalizes conditions that are actually trauma responses
