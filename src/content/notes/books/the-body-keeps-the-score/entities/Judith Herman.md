# Judith Herman

American psychiatrist and researcher at Harvard Medical School. Author of *Trauma and Recovery* (1992), a foundational text in trauma studies.

---

## Key Contributions

- Defined "complex PTSD" as distinct from single-incident PTSD
- Identified the three stages of trauma recovery: safety, remembrance, reconnection
- Pioneered the concept of "complex trauma" from prolonged interpersonal abuse
- Advocated for a feminist perspective on trauma and power

---

## Core Ideas

- Trauma fundamentally disrupts the victim's relationship with self and others
- Recovery requires a safe therapeutic relationship
- The three stages: (1) establish safety, (2) remembrance and mourning, (3) reconnection
- Tyranny of the inner critic — survivors internalize the abuser's voice
