# Bruce Perry

American psychiatrist and neuroscientist specializing in childhood trauma. Senior Fellow at the ChildTrauma Academy. Developed the Neurosequential Model of Therapeutics (NMT).

---

## Key Contributions

- Developed the Neurosequential Model — mapping brain development to trauma impact
- Showed that childhood trauma disrupts brain development from the bottom up
- Brainstem (survival) → limbic (emotion) → cortex (thinking)
- Trauma in early life affects the most fundamental brain structures

---

## Core Ideas

- The brain develops sequentially: brainstem first, cortex last
- Childhood trauma arrests development at the level where it occurred
- You must work from the bottom up — body first, then emotion, then thought
- "Connect before you redirect" — relationship is the foundation of healing
