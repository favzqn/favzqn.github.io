# Polyvagal Theory

Developed by Stephen Porges, polyvagal theory explains how the vagus nerve mediates three autonomic states: safety (social engagement), mobilization (fight/flight), and immobilization (freeze/collapse).

---

## The Three States

1. **Ventral vagal** (social engagement) — safe, connected, able to think and feel
2. **Sympathetic** (fight/flight) — mobilized for defense, anxious, angry
3. **Dorsal vagal** (freeze/collapse) — shutdown, numb, dissociated

---

## Why It Matters for Trauma

- Trauma traps people in sympathetic (hyperarousal) or dorsal vagal (hypoarousal) states
- Recovery means restoring ventral vagal dominance
- The social engagement system (face, voice, prosody) is key to feeling safe
- Eye contact, tone of voice, and co-regulation activate the ventral vagal system

---

## How to Activate Ventral Vagal

- Safe, warm relationships
- Singing, humming, chanting (vibrates the vagus nerve)
- Cold water exposure
- Slow, deep breathing
- Yoga and gentle movement
