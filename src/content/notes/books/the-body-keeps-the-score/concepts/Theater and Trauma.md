# Theater and Trauma

Van der Kolk found that dramatherapy helps trauma survivors reclaim their bodies, voices, and narratives. Performing gives survivors a way to re-experience and reframe their stories.

---

## How It Works

- Survivors perform scenes, monologues, or improvised pieces
- They experiment with different roles and perspectives
- The body becomes a tool for expression, not just a site of pain
- It rebuilds confidence, voice, and social connection

---

## Why It Works

- Trauma silences the voice and freezes the body
- Theater requires both voice and movement — directly countering trauma's effects
- It creates a safe container to explore difficult emotions
- It restores a sense of agency: "I choose what I perform"
- It builds community and connection with others

---

## Key Program: Trauma Center Drama Therapy

- Developed by David Read Johnson
- Combines role play, storytelling, and performance
- Participants create a "personal narrative" through drama
- Shown to reduce PTSD symptoms and increase sense of mastery
