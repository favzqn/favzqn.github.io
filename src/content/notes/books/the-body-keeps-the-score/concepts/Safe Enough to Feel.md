# Safe Enough to Feel

Healing from trauma requires feeling safe enough to experience emotions that have been suppressed. This is the delicate balance of trauma therapy: approaching pain without being overwhelmed.

---

## The Paradox

- Traumatized people desperately need to feel to heal
- But feeling is exactly what trauma taught them to avoid
- The therapy must create enough safety to approach the unspeakable
- Too much safety = avoidance; too much intensity = retraumatization

---

## What "Safe Enough" Looks Like

- A therapist who is calm, present, and nonjudgmental
- A body that is regulated (not in fight/flight/freeze)
- A pace that respects the person's limits
- The ability to stop and ground when overwhelmed
- Trust that the therapist will not push too far

---

## The Role of the Therapist

- Must tolerate their own discomfort with the patient's pain
- Must not rescue or fix — must witness and hold space
- Must regulate their own nervous system (co-regulation)
- Must be patient — healing cannot be rushed
