# Reclaiming Agency

Trauma takes away a person's sense of control. Recovery means reclaiming the ability to make choices, set boundaries, and direct one's own life.

---

## How Trauma Destroys Agency

- The traumatic event happens *to* you — you have no choice
- The body stays in survival mode — you can't choose to feel safe
- Shame and guilt make you feel undeserving of agency
- Hypervigilance means the environment controls you, not the other way around

---

## How to Reclaim Agency

- **Make choices** — even small ones restore a sense of control
- **Set boundaries** — saying "no" is an act of self-reclamation
- **Speak your story** — choosing to disclose is an act of agency
- **Move your body** — choosing to move is choosing to live
- **Choose your relationships** — surround yourself with safe people

---

## The Journey from Victim to Survivor

1. **Victim** — "This happened to me; I had no power"
2. **Survivor** — "I made it through; I am still here"
3. **Thriver** — "I am not defined by what happened; I choose my life"
