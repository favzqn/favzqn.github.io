# Language of the Body

Trauma speaks through the body. Chronic pain, digestive issues, autoimmune disorders, and other physical symptoms are often the body's way of expressing what words cannot.

---

## How Trauma Manifests Physically

- Chronic muscle tension (especially jaw, shoulders, back)
- Digestive problems (IBS, nausea, appetite changes)
- Autoimmune conditions (the body attacking itself)
- Chronic pain without medical cause (fibromyalgia)
- Headaches and migraines
- Heart palpitations and chest tightness

---

## Why the Body Holds Trauma

- The body encodes the memory of what happened
- When the mind cannot process the trauma, the body carries it
- Survivors may not remember the trauma consciously but feel it physically
- Physical symptoms are the body's attempt to complete the survival response

---

## Treating the Body's Language

- **Somatic therapies** — talk to the body, not just the mind
- **Body-based practices** — yoga, tai chi, dance
- **EMDR** — process the memory through bilateral stimulation
- **Breathwork** — release held tension and complete the stress cycle
