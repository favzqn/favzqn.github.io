# Interoception

Interoception is the ability to perceive internal body signals — heartbeat, breath, hunger, temperature, muscle tension. Trauma disrupts this fundamental self-awareness.

---

## Definition

The sense of the internal state of the body. It is the foundation of self-awareness and emotional regulation. When interoception is intact, a person can recognize what they feel and respond appropriately.

---

## How Trauma Disrupts Interoception

- Traumatized people often cannot identify what they feel physically
- They may be numb to body signals (hypo-interoception)
- Or overwhelmed by body signals (hyper-interoception)
- This disconnect makes it hard to distinguish between past danger and present safety

---

## Why It Matters

- Emotions are body-based: you feel anger in your chest, fear in your stomach
- Without interoception, you cannot name or manage your feelings
- Recovery requires relearning how to listen to the body
- Van der Kolk calls this "becoming the owner of your own body"

---

## How to Improve Interoception

- **Body scan meditation** — systematically notice each body part
- **Yoga** — cultivate awareness of sensation through movement
- **Biofeedback** — visualize body signals in real time
- **Mindful breathing** — notice the rhythm of breath
- **Gentle movement** — tai chi, qigong, dance
