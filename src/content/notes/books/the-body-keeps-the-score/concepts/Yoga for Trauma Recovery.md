# Yoga for Trauma Recovery

Van der Kolk's research showed yoga is one of the most effective treatments for trauma. It helps survivors reclaim ownership of their bodies and widen their window of tolerance.

---

## Why Yoga Works

- Restores interoception (awareness of body signals)
- Teaches self-regulation through breath and movement
- Creates a sense of agency over the body
- Activates the ventral vagal system (feeling safe)
- Discharges trapped survival energy

---

## Key Findings from Research

- Traumatized people who did yoga showed greater improvement than those who did only talk therapy
- Yoga reduced PTSD symptoms more than any medication studied
- Benefits increased over time with regular practice
- The key was not the style of yoga but the interoceptive awareness it cultivated

---

## What Matters in Trauma-Informed Yoga

- Emphasis on choice ("you can do this or not")
- Focus on internal sensation, not external appearance
- Gentle, accessible movements
- Breath awareness without forced breathing
- Creating a sense of safety and ownership
