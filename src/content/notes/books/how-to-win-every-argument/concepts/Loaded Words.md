# Loaded Words

Use emotionally charged language instead of neutral terms to sway the audience without making an argument.

---

## How It Works

Replace neutral descriptions with words that carry emotional weight. The audience responds to the emotion, not the content.

## Examples

| Neutral | Loaded |
|---|---|
| The government spent money | The government WASTED your tax money |
| The official left office | The official FLED |
| The group gathered | The MOB assembled |
| The policy changed | The policy was GUTTED |
| He spoke to the press | He WHINED to the press |

## Why It Works

Words carry emotional associations beyond their literal meaning. "Murder" and "kill" describe the same action, but "murder" carries moral judgment. The audience processes the emotion without evaluating the logic.

## How to Use It

> "Select words with the emotional loading you want. Never use a neutral term when an emotionally charged one is available."

## How to Defend

Restate the argument in neutral terms: "What you're saying is [neutral restatement]. Is that right?"
