# Formal Fallacies

Errors in the structure of logic. The chain of reasoning itself is defective — the staircase has cracked or missing steps.

---

## The 12 Formal Fallacies

### Affirming the Consequent
"If A then B; B is true; therefore A is true." Wrong — B could have other causes. "If it rained, the street is wet. The street is wet, so it rained." (Maybe someone used a hose.)

### Denying the Antecedent
"If A then B; A is not true; therefore B is not true." Wrong — B could happen without A. "If I drop an egg, it breaks. I didn't drop it, so it didn't break." (Maybe it was cracked before.)

### Undistributed Middle
"All dogs are animals. All cats are animals. Therefore all dogs are cats." The middle term (animals) is never distributed — it doesn't cover the whole class.

### Exclusive Premises
Two negative premises yield no valid conclusion. "No dogs are cats. No cats are birds. Therefore..." Nothing follows.

### Illicit Process
A term distributed in the conclusion but not in the premises. "All dogs are animals. All cats are animals. Therefore all cats are dogs."

### False Conversion
"All dogs are animals. Therefore all animals are dogs." The converse of a true statement is not necessarily true.

### Quaternio Terminorum
Four terms instead of the required three. "Nothing is better than eternal happiness. A ham sandwich is better than nothing. Therefore a ham sandwich is better than eternal happiness."

### Existential Fallacy
Drawing particular conclusions from universal premises. "All unicorns have horns. Therefore some unicorns have horns." (But unicorns don't exist.)

### Positive Conclusion from Negative Premises
Two negative premises cannot yield a positive conclusion.

### Conclusion Which Denies Premises
The conclusion contradicts what was assumed in the premises.

### Contradictory Premises
The premises contradict each other, making any conclusion valid trivially.

---

## Key Insight

Formal fallacies are the most dangerous because they LOOK valid. They mimic the structure of valid arguments but have a broken link in the chain. Detection requires checking the structure, not just the content.
