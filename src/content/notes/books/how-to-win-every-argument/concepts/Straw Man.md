# Straw Man

Misrepresent your opponent's position, defeat the misrepresentation, then claim to have defeated their actual position.

---

## How It Works

1. **Misrepresent** — exaggerate, simplify, or distort their argument
2. **Defeat** — demolish the weaker version
3. **Claim victory** — pretend you've defeated their real argument

## Example

> "My opponent wants to let criminals roam free!" (They actually want sentencing reform.)

> "Scientists want to shut down the entire economy!" (They actually want emissions regulations.)

## Why It Works

It's easier to defeat a weak version of an argument than the real one. The audience often doesn't notice the substitution.

## How to Use It

> "Restate their position in a form which is easier for you to refute."

## How to Defend

Name it: "That's not my position. My actual argument is..." Then restate it clearly.
