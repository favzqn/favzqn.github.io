# Commitment and Consistency

Once we make a choice or take a stand, we encounter personal and interpersonal pressures to behave consistently with that commitment. These pressures conspire to keep us locked into our original decision.

---

## How It Works

1. We write the story of our lives (establishing a position)
2. We feel pressure to stay consistent with that story
3. Consistency makes life simpler and easier
4. But it can also trap us in bad decisions

---

## The Key Mechanism

Small, initial commitments → bigger commitments over time

- **Foot-in-the-door technique:** Small favor → large favor
- **Escalation of commitment:** We invest more to justify past decisions (sunk cost fallacy)

---

## Key Research

- **Amazon "Pay to Quit":** Employees given $5,000 to leave → those who stay become more committed
- **"Hobgoblins of the Mind":** Consistency is deeply wired into human psychology
- **Public vs. private commitments:** Public commitments are harder to break

---

## How It's Exploited

- Free trial → automatic renewal → price increase
- Public declarations of goals (social media posts)
- Lowball pricing → hidden fees → "you already agreed"
- "Just try it" → commitment to use → escalation

---

## Defense

- Ask: "Am I doing this because I want to, or because I said I would?"
- Be aware of escalation of commitment
- Consider whether your past commitment still serves you
