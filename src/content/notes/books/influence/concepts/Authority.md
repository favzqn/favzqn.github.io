# Authority

We defer to experts and figures of authority. Authority can be real (credentials, expertise) or symbolic (titles, clothing, possessions).

---

## Two Types of Authority

1. **In authority** — positional: CEO, boss, professor, doctor
2. **Of authority** — expert knowledge: specialist, consultant, authority on a topic

---

## The Three Pillars of Authority

1. **Titles** — "Dr.", "Professor", "CEO" increase compliance
2. **Clothing** — uniforms, lab coats, expensive suits
3. **Trappings** — wealth signals (expensive cars, watches, jewelry)

---

## Key Research

- **Milgram obedience experiments:** People shocked others when told by a lab-coated authority figure
- **CEO letter experiment:** CEO's letter asking for charity donation → 12% compliance vs 5% control
- **Combined reciprocity + authority:** Sweets + CEO letter → 17% compliance

---

## How It's Exploited

- "Studies show..." (without citing which studies)
- Celebrity endorsements (authority of fame)
- White lab coats in advertising
- Professional titles used to sell products
- Expensive packaging and settings

---

## Defense

- Ask: "Is this person an actual expert on this specific topic?"
- Consider whether the authority is genuine or symbolic
- Be skeptical of authority used to override your own judgment
