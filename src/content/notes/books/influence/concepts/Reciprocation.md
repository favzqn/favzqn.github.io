# Reciprocation

The rule says we should try to repay what another person has provided us. If someone does us a favor, we feel obligated to return it.

---

## How It Works

- A favor, gift, or concession creates a feeling of indebtedness
- The obligation is powerful and cross-cultural
- "Much obliged" = "thank you" in English; Portuguese "obrigado" = "I am obligated"
- Japanese "sumimasen" = "this will not end" (the obligation)

---

## Key Research

- **Christmas card experiment:** Sending cards to strangers → cards poured back
- **Charity with sweets:** Giving a small packet of sweets before asking for donation → contributions more than doubled
- **Magna Carta:** Used reciprocity to define treatment of enemy merchants in wartime

---

## How It's Exploited

- Free samples (food, products)
- "But first" technique: Give something before asking
- Charities: Small gift (address labels, stickers) before donation request
- Denial-then-retreat: Start with large request, retreat to smaller one

---

## Defense

- Recognize when a "favor" is really a sales tactic
- Reciprocate the original favor, not the manipulation
- Judge the original intent — was it genuine or strategic?
