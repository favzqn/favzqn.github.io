## The Seven Principles

| # | Principle | Core Idea |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | [[Reciprocation]] | We feel obligated to return favors |
| 2 | [[Liking]] | We say yes to people we like |
| 3 | [[Social Proof]] | We look to others for how to behave |
| 4 | [[Authority]] | We defer to experts and figures of authority |
| 5 | [[Scarcity]] | We want what is rare or disappearing |
| 6 | [[Commitment and Consistency]] | We stay consistent with past commitments |
| 7 | [[Unity]] | We say yes to "one of us" |
