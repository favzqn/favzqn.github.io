# Solomon Asch

Social psychologist known for his conformity experiments (1951).

---

## Key Research

- Groups of 7-8 people, but only 1 was the real subject
- Others were confederates who gave obviously wrong answers
- ~75% of subjects conformed at least once
- Group pressure can override individual judgment, even when the answer is obviously wrong

---

## Relevance to Influence

Demonstrates the power of **Social Proof** — we look to others to determine correct behavior, even when we know they're wrong.
