# Baratunde Thurston

Writer, comedian, and technology commentator. Known as one of the "most connected" people.

---

## Key Facts

- 59,000 Gmail conversations, 1,500 Facebook posts/year
- Disconnected for 25 days as an experiment
- Result: less stress, more presence, conversations with strangers, bought a bike
- "The end came too soon" — but he went back to it

---

## Relevance to Influence

Featured in Deep Work (not Influence) as an example of digital overload. Demonstrates the tension between social connection and the cost of constant connectivity.
