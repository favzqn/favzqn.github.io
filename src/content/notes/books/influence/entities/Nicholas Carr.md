# Nicholas Carr

Writer and technology critic. Author of *The Shallows*.

---

## Key Works

- "Is Google Making Us Stupid?" (2008 Atlantic article)
- *The Shallows: What the Internet Is Doing to Our Brains* (2010) — Pulitzer finalist

---

## Key Quote

"What the Net seems to be doing is chipping away my capacity for concentration and contemplation."

---

## Relevance to Influence

Carr argued that internet use fragments attention and reduces capacity for deep thinking. To write *The Shallows*, he had to move to a cabin and forcibly disconnect — proving his own point about digital distraction.
