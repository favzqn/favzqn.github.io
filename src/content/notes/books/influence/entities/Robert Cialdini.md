# Robert Cialdini

Social psychologist. Author of *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion*.

---

## Background

- PhD in social psychology
- Arizona State University professor (Regent's Professor Emeritus)
- Spent 3 years undercover studying compliance professionals
- Combined laboratory experiments with real-world observation

---

## Key Ideas

- Seven universal principles of influence: reciprocation, liking, social proof, authority, scarcity, commitment and consistency, unity
- "Click, run" automaticity in human behavior
- Compliance professionals use these principles consciously; most people are vulnerable to them

---

## His Story

"I can admit it freely now. All my life I've been a patsy. For as long as I can recall, I've been an easy mark for the pitches of peddlers, fundraisers, and operators of one sort or another."

This motivated his career studying the psychology of compliance.
