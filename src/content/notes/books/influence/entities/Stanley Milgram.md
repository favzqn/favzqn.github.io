# Stanley Milgram

Social psychologist known for his obedience experiments (1963).

---

## Key Research

- Subjects told to administer electric shocks to a "learner" (actually an actor)
- Authority figure in lab coat ordered them to continue
- 65% delivered maximum voltage (450 volts)
- Authority figure overrode moral judgment
- Demonstrated that ordinary people can be led to do terrible things under authority

---

## Relevance to Influence

Demonstrates the power of **Authority** — when an authority figure gives an order, people obey even against their own moral compass.
