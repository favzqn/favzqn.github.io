# Ellen Langer

Social psychologist at Harvard, known for research on automatism and mindfulness.

---

## Key Research — The "Because" Experiment

- Asked to cut in line at a photocopier
- "Excuse me, I have 5 pages. May I use the copier?" → 60% compliance
- "Excuse me, I have 5 pages. May I use the copier BECAUSE I'm in a rush?" → 94% compliance
- "Excuse me, I have 5 pages. May I use the copier BECAUSE I have to make copies?" → 93% compliance
- The word "because" triggered automatic compliance — even when the reason was meaningless

---

## Relevance to Influence

Demonstrates how humans use **automatic triggers** — "click, run" patterns. The word "because" activates a compliance shortcut, regardless of the actual reason.
