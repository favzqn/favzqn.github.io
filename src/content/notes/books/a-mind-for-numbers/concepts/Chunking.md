# Chunking

"Chunking — mental leaps that unite separate bits of information through meaning — is one of the first steps toward gaining expertise in math and science."

---

## What Is a Chunk?

A chunk is a collection of information bound together by meaning. Once chunked, you don't need to remember all the underlying details — you have the main idea. "It's like getting dressed in the morning. Usually you just think one simple thought — I'll get dressed. But it's amazing when you realize the complex swirl of underlying activities."

---

## How to Form Chunks

1. **Focus attention** — you can't chunk something you haven't paid attention to
2. **Understand the basic idea** — don't just memorize; comprehend
3. **Practice** — work problems, test yourself, apply the chunk
4. **Connect chunks** — build a "chunk library" by seeing how chunks relate

---

## Chunking vs Choking

Choking happens when you're so focused on individual details (focused mode) that you lose sight of the whole picture. The solution: periodically step back and see the big picture (diffuse mode).
