# Memory Palace Technique

"Memory in math and science is about understanding, not rote memorization. But for formulas you must memorize, the memory palace is the most powerful technique known."

---

## The Method

Also called the "method of loci" — an ancient Greek technique:
1. Visualize a familiar place (your home, a walk through your neighborhood)
2. Place items to remember at specific locations along the route
3. Make the images vivid, exaggerated, and even bizarre
4. When you need to recall, mentally walk through your palace

---

## Why It Works

The brain is excellent at spatial and visual memory. By converting abstract information (formulas, lists, concepts) into vivid visual images placed in a spatial context, you leverage your strongest memory systems.

---

## Oakley's Tips

- Start with a simple palace (your kitchen sink)
- Make images vivid and emotional
- Practice daily — it gets faster with time
- Understanding the formulas makes memorization much easier
