# Procrastination as Zombie Habit

"The last thing you want to do in tackling procrastination is to go around spraying willpower on it like it's cheap air freshener."

---

## The Habit Loop

Oakley describes procrastination as a zombie habit — an automatic response your brain falls into:
1. **Cue**: You sit down to study difficult material
2. **Routine**: Your brain seeks something more pleasant (social media, YouTube)
3. **Reward**: Temporary relief from discomfort

---

## Why Willpower Doesn't Work

Willpower uses a lot of neural resources. It's a limited, exhaustible resource. Fighting procrastination with willpower alone is like fighting a fire with a spray bottle.

---

## The Four-Part Habit Change Plan

1. **The Cue**: Choose a consistent time and place for studying
2. **The Routine**: Use the Pomodoro technique — 25 minutes of focused work
3. **The Reward**: Allow yourself a treat after the session
4. **The Belief**: Believe you can change — hang out with "can do" people

---

## Mental Contrasting

Vividly imagine your goal (medical school, a great career) and then contrast it with your current situation (macaroni and cheese dinners, student debt). The contrast creates motivation without willpower.
