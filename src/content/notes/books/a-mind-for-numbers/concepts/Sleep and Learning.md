# Sleep and Learning

"Sleep is a critical part of learning — the brain consolidates memories and makes new connections during sleep."

---

## What Happens During Sleep

- **Memory consolidation**: The brain replays and strengthens neural patterns formed during the day
- **Toxin clearing**: The glymphatic system washes away metabolic waste
- **Creative connection**: The diffuse mode works on problems during sleep
- **Emotional processing**: REM sleep processes the emotional content of learning

---

## Practical Implications

- Don't sacrifice sleep to study more — you'll learn less
- Review material briefly before sleep to "prime" consolidation
- A nap after learning can boost retention
- Consistent sleep schedule supports learning more than occasional all-nighters

---

## Oakley's Edison Trick

Hold a ball bearing while napping over your desk. When you fall asleep, it drops and clatters, waking you — capturing the diffuse-mode insight that emerged during the micro-nap.
