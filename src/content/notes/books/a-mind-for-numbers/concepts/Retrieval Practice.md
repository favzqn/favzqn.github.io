# Retrieval Practice

"The critical importance of retrieval for learning." — Karpicke & Roediger

---

## The Principle

Pulling information OUT of your brain is far more effective than putting information IN. Retrieval practice — testing yourself, recalling from memory — produces stronger, more durable learning than passive review.

---

## Techniques

1. **Close the book and recite** — what did you just read?
2. **Self-quizzing** — write questions and answer them
3. **Flashcards** — but only if you use them for active recall, not recognition
4. **Practice problems** — solve without looking at solutions
5. **Teach someone else** — explain the concept to another person

---

## Why It Works

Retrieval strengthens the neural pathways associated with the information. Each time you pull something from memory, that memory becomes more accessible. Rereading just makes the text familiar — retrieval makes it permanent.
