# Barbara Oakley

Professor of Engineering at Oakland University. Creator of "Learning How to Learn" — the world's most popular online course (with Terrence Sejnowski). Author of *A Mind for Numbers* and *Learn Like a Pro*.

---

## Personal Story

Oakley flunked math in high school — twice. She deliberately avoided math and science, finding success in languages instead. Then she was commissioned as a second lieutenant in the U.S. Army Signal Corps and suddenly needed to learn electronics and engineering.

She developed her learning strategies out of necessity — not innate talent. She went on to earn a PhD in engineering and become a professor.

---

## Philosophy

- You don't need to be a "math person" to learn math
- Learning strategies matter more than innate talent
- The brain can be sculpted at any age
- Failure is a teacher, not a sentence
- "Perseverance is the virtue of the less brilliant" (quoting Cajal)

---

## Key Contributions

- Focused vs diffuse modes as a framework for understanding learning
- The Pomodoro technique applied to studying
- Chunking as the building block of expertise
- The illusion of competence as the primary learning trap
- Making neuroscience research accessible to general audiences
