# Poverty and Desperation

The social conditions that drive people to crime in *Crime and Punishment*.

---

## St. Petersburg in the 1860s

- Overcrowded, hot, smelly streets
- Tiny apartments shared by multiple families
- Alcohol everywhere — cheap and accessible
- No social safety net
- The poor were invisible to the wealthy

---

## How Poverty Drives Crime

- Raskolnikov can't pay rent, drops out of university
- Marmeladov drinks away his family's resources
- Sonia turns to prostitution to support her family
- Katerina Ivanovna is consumed by tuberculosis and pride
- The pawnbroker Alyona exploits the desperate

---

## Dostoevsky's Point

Poverty doesn't just cause material suffering — it degrades the soul. The environment of desperation makes terrible decisions seem rational. Raskolnikov's theory of extraordinary men was, in part, a product of his desperate circumstances.
