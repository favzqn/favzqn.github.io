# Doubles and Duality

The novel is built on pairs of characters who mirror and contrast each other.

---

## Key Doubles

| Character | Mirror | Relationship |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| [[Raskolnikov]] | [[Svidrigailov]] | Dark double — what Raskolnikov could become |
| [[Raskolnikov]] | [[Porfiry Petrovich]] | Intellectual mirror — both brilliant, but one serves law |
| [[Raskolnikov]] | [[Razumikhin]] | Healthy double — reason used well |
| [[Sonya Marmeladov]] | [[Lizaveta]] | Both gentle, both victimized |
| [[Dunya Raskolnikov]] | [[Sonya Marmeladov]] | Both sacrifice for family |

---

## Raskolnikov's Internal Duality

- Oscillates between compassion and cold logic
- Name means "schism" — he has split himself
- First page: narrator says he is "hopelessly impoverished" but also "not cowardly"
- His entire arc is the struggle between these two selves

---

## The Function of Doubles

- Show Raskolnikov's potential paths
- Create tension and moral contrast
- Illuminate the themes from different angles
- Show the consequences of different choices
