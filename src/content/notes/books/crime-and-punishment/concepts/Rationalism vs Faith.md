# Rationalism vs Faith

The central philosophical conflict: Western rationalism vs Russian spiritual values.

---

## Raskolnikov's Position

- Western rationalist: believes reason can justify anything
- His Extraordinary Man Theory is based on logic
- He murders to test his theory — reason over morality

---

## Sonya's Position

- Russian spiritual: faith and love are the answer
- She reads the Gospel, not philosophy
- Her power comes from spiritual conviction, not logic

---

## The Conflict

- Raskolnikov's name comes from "raskol" (schism) — he has split himself
- His patronymic "Romanovich" suggests Western (Roman) worldview
- The novel argues that rationalism without faith leads to destruction
- Redemption comes through spiritual awakening, not intellectual understanding

---

## Historical Context

- 1860s Russia: "new ideas" of Western rationalism, socialism, utilitarianism
- Dostoevsky was critical of these movements
- He saw them as replacing spiritual values with material ones
