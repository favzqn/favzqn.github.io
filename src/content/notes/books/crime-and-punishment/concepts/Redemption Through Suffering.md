# Redemption Through Suffering

Dostoevsky's central spiritual message: redemption comes through suffering, love, and faith.

---

## The Lazarus Story

Sonya reads to Raskolnikov from the Gospel of John — the story of Lazarus, raised from the dead after four days. This parallels Raskolnikov's spiritual death and potential rebirth.

---

## The Epilogue

- Raskolnikov remains cold and unrepentant in prison
- Sonia follows him to Siberia
- He finally breaks down, weeps at her feet
- He picks up the Gospel — begins spiritual rebirth
- "The beginning of a new story"

---

## Dostoevsky's Message

- Reason alone cannot save humanity
- Faith and love are necessary for redemption
- Suffering purifies and transforms
- The "new story" is one of spiritual awakening
