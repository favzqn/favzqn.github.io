# Extraordinary Man Theory

Raskolnikov's central philosophy: some people are "extraordinary" and have the right to transgress moral law if their ideas require it.

---

## The Two Categories

1. **Ordinary people** — must live in obedience, are conservative
2. **Extraordinary people** — have the right to step over laws for their ideas

Raskolnikov sees Napoleon as the model: a man who killed thousands to achieve greatness and was celebrated for it.

---

## Raskolnikov's Article

He wrote an article arguing that extraordinary men have the right to murder if their goal justifies it. Porfiry Petrovich uses this article to trap him.

---

## The Test

Raskolnikov murders Alyona Ivanovna to test whether he is "extraordinary." The result: he is destroyed by guilt, proving he is ordinary.

---

## The Irony

- Raskolnikov fails the test — he cannot handle the guilt
- Svidrigailov shows what a truly "extraordinary" man looks like — and he ends in suicide
- The theory is revealed as self-deception and moral bankruptcy
