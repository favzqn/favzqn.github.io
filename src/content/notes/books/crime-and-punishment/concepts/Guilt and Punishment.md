# Guilt and Punishment

The psychological torment that follows crime is inevitable, regardless of whether one is caught.

---

## Raskolnikov's Punishment

- **Before arrest:** fever, delirium, isolation, paranoia, compulsive behavior
- **After arrest:** 8 years in Siberia
- **The real punishment:** the destruction of his soul by guilt

---

## Key Insight

The "punishment" in the title refers not just to legal punishment but to the psychological and spiritual destruction that guilt causes. Raskolnikov is punished long before he is caught.

---

## The Structure

- Part One: the crime and immediate guilt
- Parts Two-Five: escalating psychological torment
- Part Six: confession as release
- Epilogue: spiritual rebirth through suffering
