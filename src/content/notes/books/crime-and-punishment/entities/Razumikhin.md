# Razumikhin

Dmitri Prokofich Razumikhin. Raskolnikov's former university friend. Loyal, practical, warm-hearted.

---

## Key Traits

- Loyal to Raskolnikov despite his coldness
- Practical, energetic, good-humored
- Name derives from "razum" (reason) — he represents healthy rationality
- Unlike Raskolnikov, he uses his mind productively

---

## Role

- Cares for Raskolnikov during his illness
- Represents the healthy alternative to Raskolnikov's path
- Eventually marries Dunya
- Provides practical help and emotional support
