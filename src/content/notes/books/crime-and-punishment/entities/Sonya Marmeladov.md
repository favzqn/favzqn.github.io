# Sonya Marmeladov

Sofia Semyonovna Marmeladova. Prostitute and spiritual savior of Raskolnikov. Her name means "divine wisdom" in Greek.

---

## Key Traits

- Gentle, devout, self-sacrificing
- prostitute to support her family (father Marmeladov, stepmother Katerina, half-siblings)
- Maintains faith despite degradation
- Reads the story of Lazarus to Raskolnikov — the resurrection of the dead

---

## Role in the Novel

- Represents suffering and redemption
- The only person Raskolnikov truly confesses to
- Follows him to Siberia despite his coldness
- Her faith persists through all hardship
- She is Raskolnikov's moral compass

---

## The Lazarus Story

Sonya reads to Raskolnikov from the Gospel of John — the story of Lazarus, raised from the dead after four days. This parallels Raskolnikov's spiritual death and potential rebirth.

---

## Key Moments

- Confesses her love for Raskolnikov
- Gives Raskolnikov the cross (Lizaveta's cross)
- Follows him to Siberia
- Waits for him despite his coldness
- Raskolnikov finally weeps at her feet — beginning of his rebirth
