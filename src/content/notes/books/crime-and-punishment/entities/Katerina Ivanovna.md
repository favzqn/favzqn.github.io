# Katerina Ivanovna

Marmeladov's second wife. Consumptive (tuberculosis) and fiercely proud.

---

## Key Traits

- Formerly educated, came from a good family
- Married Marmeladov despite his alcoholism
- Consumptive — coughs blood, knows she is dying
- Proud, refuses to beg, makes Sonia work as prostitute
- Torments her stepchildren

---

## Role

- Represents the desperation of poverty
- Her pride prevents her from accepting help
- Her death creates chaos and conflict at the funeral
- Drives the tragedy of the Marmeladov family
