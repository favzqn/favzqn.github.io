# Svidrigailov

Arkady Ivanovich Svidrigailov. Wealthy libertine who serves as Raskolnikov's dark double.

---

## Key Traits

- Wealthy, sensual, amoral
- Pursues pleasure without moral restraint
- Has committed crimes (implied murder of wife Marfa)
- Yet capable of unexpected generosity
- Represents what Raskolnikov could become

---

## Role as Double

- Svidrigailov is Raskolnikov's "shadow self"
- He has actually done what Raskolnikov only theorized about
- He represents the logical endpoint of Raskolnikov's Extraordinary Man Theory
- His amorality is complete, not tortured

---

## Key Moments

- Confesses dark secrets to Raskolnikov
- Witnesses Raskolnikov's confession to Sonia
- Proposes to Dunya (Raskolnikov's sister)
- Dunya rejects him; he releases her
- Commits suicide — shoots himself in the head

---

## Symbolism

- Represents the emptiness of a life without moral constraint
- His suicide shows the dead end of Raskolnikov's theory
- He is the "extraordinary man" who has transgressed — and found nothing
