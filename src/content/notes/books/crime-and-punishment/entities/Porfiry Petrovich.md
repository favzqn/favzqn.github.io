# Porfiry Petrovich

The examining magistrate investigating the murders of Alyona and Lizaveta. A master of psychological detection.

---

## Key Traits

- Short, stout, corpulent, clean-shaven
- Sickly yellowish face with ironical expression
- Eyes shine with "watery, sentimental light"
- Wears dressing gowns, appears casual
- Extremely intelligent and perceptive

---

## Detection Method

- Never collects physical evidence
- Uses psychology and conversation to trap suspects
- Engages Raskolnikov in intellectual discussions
- References Raskolnikov's article on crime
- Gradually tightens the psychological noose

---

## Key Confrontations

1. First meeting: discusses Raskolnikov's article on "ordinary" vs "extraordinary" men
2. Second meeting: plays cat-and-mouse, mentions "psychological crime"
3. Final confrontation: directly tells Raskolnikov he knows the truth, urges confession
4. "I am not a beast, I am a gentleman!" — Raskolnikov's response

---

## Role

- Represents the law and moral order
- The only character who fully understands Raskolnikov's psychology
- Gives Raskolnikov a chance to confess voluntarily
- Shows mercy by not arresting him immediately
