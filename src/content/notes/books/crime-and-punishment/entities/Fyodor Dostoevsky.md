# Fyodor Dostoevsky

Fyodor Mikhailovich Dostoevsky (1821-1881). Russian novelist, philosopher, journalist. Author of *Crime and Punishment*.

---

## Key Facts

- Born October 30, 1821 in Moscow
- Mother died when he was fifteen
- Graduated from St. Petersburg Academy of Military Engineers (1843)
- Arrested for involvement with socialist group (1849)
- Sentenced to death, commuted to 4 years in Siberian labor camp
- Married twice, had gambling addiction
- Died February 9, 1881 in St. Petersburg

---

## Major Works

- *Poor Folk* (1846)
- *Notes from Underground* (1864)
- *Crime and Punishment* (1866)
- *The Gambler* (1867)
- *The Idiot* (1869)
- *Demons* (1872)
- *The Brothers Karamazov* (1880)

---

## Philosophy

- Struggle between faith and reason
- Critique of Western rationalism and materialism
- Emphasis on suffering, redemption, and spiritual rebirth
- Deep interest in human psychology and moral choice
