# Marmeladov

Semyon Zakharovich Marmeladov. Sonia's father. Alcoholic former civil servant.

---

## Key Traits

- Drinks heavily, squanders family resources
- Aware of his degradation but unable to stop
- Delivers a famous confession in the tavern (Part 1, Ch 2)
- Philosophizes about suffering and God's mercy

---

## Role

- His alcoholism drives Sonia to prostitution to support the family
- Represents the lower depths of St. Petersburg society
- His death by carriage accident (Part 3) brings Raskolnikov into contact with the family
- His funeral dinner becomes a scene of conflict
