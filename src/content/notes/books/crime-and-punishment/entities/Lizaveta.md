# Lizaveta

Alyona Ivanovna's half-sister. Gentle, simple-minded, and kind. Accidentally murdered by Raskolnikov.

---

## Key Traits

- Simple, kind, submissive
- Gives her possessions away to others
- Arrives unexpectedly during the murder
- Raskolnikov kills her in panic — she was not part of the plan

---

## Role

- Her accidental murder makes the crime worse than planned
- She is innocent — unlike Alyona, she is not exploitative
- Her cross is given to Sonia, who gives it to Raskolnikov
- Her murder weighs on Raskolnikov's conscience
- Represents the unintended consequences of "extraordinary" actions
