# Dunya Raskolnikov

Avdotya Romanovna Raskolnikova. Raskolnikov's younger sister. Proud, beautiful, and strong-willed.

---

## Key Traits

- Beautiful, intelligent, proud
- Willing to sacrifice for family (agrees to marry Luzhin for money)
- Strong moral character
- Resembles Raskolnikov in pride and independence

---

## Role

- Her planned marriage to Luzhin provides Raskolnikov's initial motivation for murder (to save her)
- Luzhin attempts to frame Sonia; Dunya exposes him
- Svidrigailov falls in love with her; she rejects him
- Eventually marries Razumikhin (Raskolnikov's loyal friend)
