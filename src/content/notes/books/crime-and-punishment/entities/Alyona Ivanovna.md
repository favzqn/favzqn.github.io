# Alyona Ivanovna

The pawnbroker whom Raskolnikov murders. Old, stingy, exploitative.

---

## Key Traits

- Lends money at high interest
- Hoards wealth while others starve
- Exploits the poor
- Raskolnikov sees her as a "louse" — someone whose death would benefit society

---

## Role

- Her murder is the central crime of the novel
- Raskolnikov justifies killing her through his Extraordinary Man Theory
- Her death triggers his psychological destruction
- Her hidden wealth under the stone becomes evidence
