# Luzhin

Pyotr Petrovich Luzhin. Dunya's fiancé. Opportunistic, calculating, self-serving.

---

## Key Traits

- Plans to marry Dunya for her beauty and obedience
- Wants a wife who will be grateful and submissive
- Attempted to frame Sonia for theft (planting money)
- Self-righteous, considers himself generous

---

## Role

- Represents the "rational egoist" — similar to Raskolnikov but without the depth
- His plot to frame Sonia backfires when Raskolnikov exposes him
- Dunya breaks off the engagement
- Shows that Raskolnikov's theory, when applied without intelligence, becomes mere selfishness
