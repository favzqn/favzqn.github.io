# Magic Words Framework

Phil M. Jones's core thesis: a small change in words can produce a large change in outcomes. The difference between getting what you want and not getting it often comes down to a few carefully chosen words.

---

## The Premise

People do not make decisions based on information alone. They make decisions based on how information makes them **feel**. The right words at the right time can shift someone's emotional state from resistance to agreement, from indecision to action.

---

## The Two Foundations

1. **Loss Aversion**: People work harder to avoid loss than to achieve gain
2. **Feel Before Logic**: Something has to feel right before it makes sense

Every magic word in the book builds on these two principles.

---

## The 23 Phrases

The book provides 23 specific phrases organized by function:
- **Future visualization**: "How would you feel if...", "Just imagine..."
- **Time control**: "When would be a good time to...", "I'm guessing you haven't got around to..."
- **Choice architecture**: "You have three options", "There are two types of people"
- **Agreement building**: "I bet you're a bit like me", "If... then"
- **Emotional regulation**: "Don't worry", "The good news is..."
- **Social proof**: "Most people"
- **Conversation control**: "What happens next is...", "What makes you say that?"
- **Commitment**: "Could you do me a small favor?", "Just one more thing"
