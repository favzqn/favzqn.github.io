# Loss Aversion

"People will work far harder to avoid a potential loss than they will to achieve a potential gain."

This is the first of Jones's two foundational principles.

---

## How It Works

Every human decision involves two motivational forces:
- **Toward motivation**: moving toward a positive outcome
- **Away motivation**: moving away from a negative outcome

Loss aversion means the away motivation is roughly **twice as powerful** as the toward motivation. The pain of losing $100 is felt more intensely than the pleasure of gaining $100.

---

## The Application

Jones uses loss aversion in the magic words:

- **"How would you feel if your competition passed you?"** — away motivation
- **"How would you feel if you lost everything?"** — away motivation
- **"Just imagine what your boss would say if you missed this opportunity"** — away motivation
- **"Just imagine the look on your kids' faces when they see you achieve this"** — toward motivation

The greater the contrast between loss and gain, the more likely the person is to move.
