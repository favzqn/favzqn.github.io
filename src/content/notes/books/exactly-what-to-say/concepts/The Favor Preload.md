# The Favor Preload

"Could you do me a small favor?" — gets agreement before the person even knows what the favor is.

---

## How It Works

The request of a favor almost always gains unanimous agreement. The worst response is still a conditional yes ("Depends what it is"). By getting the yes first, you've already won the hardest part.

Jones applies this to referrals — the most common scenario where people fail to ask:
1. Wait for "Thank you" (gratitude = indebtedness)
2. "You couldn't do me a small favor, could you?"
3. "You wouldn't happen to know... just one person... someone who, just like you... would benefit from..."
4. Then shut up

---

## Why It Works

- Gratitude creates indebtedness — the best time to ask is when they feel they owe you
- "Small favor" lowers the bar
- The preface makes it hard to say no before knowing the request
