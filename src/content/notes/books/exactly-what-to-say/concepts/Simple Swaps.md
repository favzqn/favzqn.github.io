# Simple Swaps

Changing a couple of words can make all the difference in the results you get from conversations.

---

## The Two Swaps

### 1. Questions → Assumptions
**Old**: "Do you have any questions?" (invites doubt)
**New**: "What questions do you have for me?" (assumes commitment)

The old version suggests they should have questions. The new version assumes they've decided and just need details. If they have no questions, they've made a decision.

### 2. Permission → Assumption
**Old**: "Can I have your phone number?" (asks permission, invites no)
**New**: "What's the best number to contact you at?" (assumes you'll be in touch)

The old version creates permission-based resistance. The new version assumes the relationship and asks for logistics.

---

## The Principle

Both swaps work by **assuming the outcome**. When you assume commitment, the path of least resistance is agreement. When you ask permission, the path of least resistance is refusal.
