# The Columbo Method

"Just one more thing..." — named after the TV detective who would turn back at the door.

---

## How It Works

When a conversation seems over and the other person has declined, turn back with "Just one more thing..." and introduce a smaller, easier ask. Their guard is now down, and they're more likely to agree to something small.

Jones distinguishes this from upselling (asking for more at the point of transaction). The Columbo Method is a **downsell** — a lesser objective when the primary objective fails.

---

## The Application

- Instead of asking for a large contract, ask for a trial order
- Instead of asking for partnership, ask them to try the product
- Instead of asking for a referral, ask them to sample something

The key is that the smaller ask feels easy compared to what they just declined.
