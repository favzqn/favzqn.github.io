# The Rule of Three Options

"As I see it, you have three options..."

Jones uses the Rule of Three to narrow choices and guide decisions. The human brain processes three options easily; more than three creates paralysis.

---

## The Technique

1. Present three options
2. Stack them in your favor — worst first, best last
3. Finish with "What's going to be easier for you?"

The word "easier" eliminates the laborious first option. The status quo (second option) was already off the table. The only remaining choice is the one you wanted.

---

## Why It Works

- **Three is memorable**: The rhythm of three makes for easy listening
- **Stacking controls perception**: The last option benefits from primacy of recency
- **"Easier" is disarming**: It sounds helpful, not pushy
- **Eliminates overwhelm**: Fewer choices = faster decisions
