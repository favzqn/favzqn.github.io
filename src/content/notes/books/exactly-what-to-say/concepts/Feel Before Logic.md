# Feel Before Logic

"Something has to feel right before it ever makes sense."

The second of Jones's two foundational principles. People do not decide based on evidence; they decide based on emotion, then rationalize with evidence.

---

## The Evidence

Jones observes: "I am sure you have stepped away from a conversation confused about why the other person did not follow your advice and have wondered, 'I don't know why they don't do it. It just makes sense for them to do it.'"

The problem is not the logic. The problem is that you never addressed the feeling.

---

## The Application

Every magic word in Jones's system targets emotion before logic:
- "How would you feel if..." — targets the emotion of the future scenario
- "Just imagine..." — triggers emotional visualization
- "Don't worry" — addresses anxiety before content
- "The good news is..." — reframes emotional label
- "Most people" — triggers belonging (emotional safety)
