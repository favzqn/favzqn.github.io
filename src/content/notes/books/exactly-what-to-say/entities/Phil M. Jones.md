# Phil M. Jones

British sales trainer, author, and speaker. Author of *Exactly What to Say* and several other books on sales and communication.

---

## Background

- Son of a builder
- First mentor: Peter Lee at instil
- Career in sales training and speaking
- Mission: #teachingtheworldtosell — changing the perception that "sales" is a dirty word
- Defines sales professionals as "decision catalysts" — "professional mind-maker-uppers"

---

## Philosophy

- Words matter more than strategies
- Small changes in language produce large changes in outcomes
- Sales is not manipulation — it's helping people make decisions they already want to make
- The person asking questions controls the conversation
- Emotion drives decisions; logic justifies them
