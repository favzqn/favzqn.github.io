# Columbo

The fictional TV detective played by Peter Falk. Famous for his disheveled raincoat, cigar, and the phrase "Just one more thing..."

---

## The Technique

Columbo would quiz a suspect, go through the rigmarole of gathering information, then turn to leave. Just when the suspect thought they had gotten away with it, he would turn back with his finger pointed upward and say: "Oh, just one more thing."

In this moment, when the suspect's guard was down, he could ask the question that would crack the case.

---

## The Application

Phil M. Jones adapts this to sales and influence: when a conversation seems over and the other person has declined, turn back with "Just one more thing..." and introduce a smaller, easier ask. Their guard is now down.
