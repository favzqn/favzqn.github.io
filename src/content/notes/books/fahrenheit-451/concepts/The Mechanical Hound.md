# The Mechanical Hound

A robotic hunting dog that tracks targets by scent. It sleeps but does not sleep, lives but does not live. It is technology perverted from companion to enforcer.

---

## Description

- Eight insect legs
- Multi-faceted ruby-glass eyes
- A four-inch hollow steel needle in its proboscis
- Injects massive jolts of morphine or procaine
- Can remember and identify ten thousand odour-indexes
- Programmed to hunt specific targets

## The Hound and Montag

The Hound growls at Montag in the firehouse — someone has programmed it with his scent. It is the first sign that Montag is being watched. Later, it hunts him after he kills Beatty.

## Bradbury's Prediction

The Mechanical Hound is an autonomous weapon with facial recognition (by scent), algorithmic targeting, and no human judgment in the loop. Written in 1953, it predicts drone warfare, algorithmic policing, and autonomous weapons systems.

## Key Insight

The Hound is not evil. It has no malice. It is a machine that does what it is programmed to do. The evil is in the programming — in the society that chose to build hunting dogs instead of companions.
