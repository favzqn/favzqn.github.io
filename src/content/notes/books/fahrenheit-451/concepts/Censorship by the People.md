# Censorship by the People

Bradbury's central thesis: books were not banned by the government. People stopped reading them. Minorities objected to being offended, content was shortened and simplified, and television filled the void. The firemen were created to finish what the people had already started.

---

## The Sequence

1. **Minorities object** — every minority group wanted something removed that offended them. Books were shortened to avoid controversy.
2. **Simplification accelerates** — classics cut to 15-minute radio shows, then 2-minute columns, then dictionary entries.
3. **Schools shorten** — philosophy, history, languages dropped. English and spelling neglected. Life becomes immediate.
4. **Television fills the void** — the parlour walls provide sensation without thought, entertainment without effort.
5. **Firemen finish the job** — established to burn what little remains.

## Beatty's Explanation

> "Colored people don't like *Little Black Sambo*. Burn it. White people don't feel good about *Uncle Tom's Cabin*. Burn it. Someone's written a book on tobacco and cancer of the lungs? The cigarette people are weeping? Burn the book."

## Key Insight

This is not Orwell's *1984*. There is no Thought Police, no Ministry of Truth. The censorship is voluntary. The people chose comfort over complexity, sensation over thought. The government simply provided the match.
