# Guy Montag

The protagonist of *Fahrenheit 451*. A fireman whose job is to burn books. Through three encounters — Clarisse, the woman who dies with her books, and Faber — he awakens from numbness to awareness, destroys his old life, and becomes a living book.

---

## Arc

1. **Numb**: takes pleasure in burning, wears happiness like a mask
2. **Awakened**: Clarisse asks "Are you happy?" — he realizes he is not
3. **Disturbed**: the woman who dies with her books shatters his numbness
4. **Seeking**: hides books, seeks Faber, tries to read
5. **Breaking**: reads poetry to Mildred's friends, kills Beatty, flees
6. **Reborn**: crosses the river, finds the outcasts, begins to remember

## The Mask

Montag wears his happiness like a mask. Clarisse runs off with it. What's underneath is emptiness — the recognition that he has been burning the very things that could fill him.

## Key Insight

Montag's transformation is not from ignorance to knowledge. It is from numbness to feeling. He doesn't need to learn what books contain — he needs to learn that he is not happy. The books come after.
