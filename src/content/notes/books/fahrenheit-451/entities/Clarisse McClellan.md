# Clarisse McClellan

A seventeen-year-old who walks instead of driving, talks instead of watching walls, and asks questions nobody asks. She is the catalyst for Montag's awakening.

---

## Characteristics

- Walks in the rain and tastes it
- Notices old leaves smell like cinnamon
- Asks "Are you happy?" and changes everything
- Watches people instead of television
- Is considered anti-social by her school
- Disappears — probably hit by a car, possibly dead

## The Question

> "Are you happy?"

Five words that dismantle Montag's entire life. The question is not accusatory. It is genuine. Clarisse genuinely wants to know — and that genuineness is what makes it devastating.

## The Dandelion Test

She rubs a dandelion under his chin to test if he is in love. It doesn't work. "It's that dandelion," he says. "You've used it all up on yourself." Clarisse has already consumed all the wonder. There is none left for him.

## Key Insight

Clarisse is not a rebel. She is not fighting the system. She is simply living differently — and that is enough to crack the system open. You don't have to fight fire. You just have to notice the rain.
