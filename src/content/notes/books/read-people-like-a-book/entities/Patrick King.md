# Patrick King

Social interaction and conversation coach. Author of several books on social skills, conversation, and emotional intelligence. Founder of PatrickKingConsulting.com.

---

## Philosophy

King's approach is evidence-based but practical. He draws on psychology research (Ekman, Big Five, MBTI) but emphasizes that no technique is 100% reliable. His core message: people-reading is a skill that improves with practice and humility.

---

## Key Contributions

- Integration of multiple personality frameworks (Big Five, MBTI, Enneagram, Keirsey) into a single reading system
- Emphasis on baseline reading before drawing conclusions
- Voice as body language — the insight that the voice is a physiological part of the body
- Lie detection through cognitive load rather than individual cues
- Nine response frameworks (HPM, SBR, EDR) for conversation agility
- Covert persuasion techniques that map to Lao Tzu's wu wei
- Witty banter as a learnable skill, not innate talent
- Verbal mirroring: the best conversationalist talks the least
- Conversation as pattern recognition — a science, not an art
- Small talk as a filtering mechanism for trust
- Likability is psychology, not luck — 27 studies prove it
- The Pratfall effect: vulnerability increases likability
