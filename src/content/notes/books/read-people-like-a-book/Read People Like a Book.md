# Read People Like a Book

*Read People Like a Book: How to Analyze, Understand, and Predict People's Emotions, Thoughts, Intentions, and Behaviors* by Patrick King (2020).

---

## Summary

A practical handbook for analyzing people through their emotions, body language, personality, speech, environment, and values. King integrates psychology research (Big Five, MBTI, Enneagram, Ekman's microexpressions, FBI interrogation techniques) into an accessible framework for everyday people-reading.

### Chapter 1: Emotions and Motivations
- The ego and its defenses
- Maslow's hierarchy of needs
- People's motivations drive all behavior — find the motivation and you can predict the behavior
- How needs (safety, belonging, esteem, self-actualization) manifest in communication

### Chapter 2: Reading Through Sight and Observation
- **Microexpressions** (Paul Ekman): 6 universal emotions expressed in fractions of a second — fear, anger, surprise, sadness, happiness, disgust
- **Macroexpressions**: slower, conscious, fakable
- **Body language clusters**: read the whole body, not individual gestures
- Aggressive, assertive, submissive, relaxed, romantic, deceptive body patterns
- Voice as body language — pitch, timbre, speed, volume reveal posture and emotion
- Message clusters: the whole body communicates one unified intention

### Chapter 3: Personality Science and Typology
- **Big Five (OCEAN)**: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extroversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism
- **MBTI**: 16 personality types based on 4 dichotomies (E/I, S/N, T/F, J/P)
- **Keirsey Temperaments**: 4 temperaments (Guardian, Artisan, Idealist, Rational)
- **Enneagram**: 9 personality types focused on motivations and fears
- Personality as context for reading behavior — patterns override individual gestures

### Chapter 4: Lie Detection 101
- Most people are terrible at detecting lies (even professionals)
- **Establish a baseline** first — everyone's normal is different
- **Increase cognitive load** — ask unexpected questions, add details
- **Incongruence clusters** — words say one thing, body says another
- Watch for emotional leaks and timing mismatches
- The importance of clusters over individual cues

### Chapter 5: Intuition and Thin Slicing
- Trust initial gut reactions, then supplement with deliberate analysis
- Thin slicing: small amounts of data → surprisingly accurate assessments
- Avoid confirmation bias — challenge first impressions
- Judges, firefighters, military all use intuition + analysis together

### Chapter 6: Analyzing People Through Their Environment
- Home and possessions as extended body language
- Sam Gosling's research: you can guess political inclinations from bedroom decor
- What people spend money on reveals values
- Closed/open spaces mirror closed/open personalities

### Chapter 7: Values and Goals
- 3 questions to reveal core values: (1) What pain/pleasure drives you? (2) Where do you spend money? (3) What are your fears?
- Fears reveal what people think of their ability to handle adversity
- Goals reveal priorities — the emotion behind the goal matters more than the goal itself

---

## Key Themes

1. **Whole-Person Reading** — body, voice, personality, environment, values are one system
2. **Baseline First** — know someone's normal before reading deviations
3. **Clusters Over Singles** — no single cue is diagnostic; look for patterns
4. **Personality as Context** — the same gesture means different things in different personalities
5. **Ego Drives Behavior** — people protect their self-image above almost everything
6. **Intuition + Analysis** — gut feelings are data; deliberate analysis verifies them
7. **Lie Detection Is Hard** — humility is essential; even experts are only slightly better than chance
