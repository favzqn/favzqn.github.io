# Values Reading

Three questions to reveal someone's core values: (1) What pain and pleasure drives you? (2) Where do you spend money? (3) What are your fears?

---

## The Three Questions

### 1. What Pain and Pleasure Drives You?
"What really matters most to you — in both a positive and negative way?" The emotion behind the answer reveals values. Wanting to be CEO isn't about the title — it's about the feelings (security, status, purpose) that come with it.

### 2. Where Do You Spend Money?
Money follows values. What people invest in reveals what they prioritize. What they refuse to spend on reveals what they devalue.

### 3. What Are Your Fears?
Fears reveal what people think of their ability to handle adversity. A person who fears rejection is telling you that psychological harm is more serious than physical harm in their world.

---

## Practical Application

By combining these three data points, you can predict:
- How someone will react to conflict
- What will motivate them in negotiations
- What will cause them to shut down
- What kind of relationship they'll build with you
