# Message Clusters

"Rather than trying to imagine what every possible manifestation of aggression looks like, we can focus on the aggression itself, and watch for resulting clusters of behavior."

---

## The Principle

Human beings communicate as whole systems. If someone is feeling hostile, that hostility shows up in their language, actions, facial expressions, voice, AND posture simultaneously. You don't need to decode individual gestures — you read the overall message.

---

## The Six Whole-Body Patterns

1. **Crossing, closing, shutting off** — guardedness, suspicion, shyness
2. **Expanding, opening, loosening** — friendliness, comfort, trust
3. **Forward, pointed, directed** — dominance, control, persuasiveness
4. **Preening, touching, stroking** — romantic intentions
5. **Striking, abrupt, forceful, loud** — energy, violence, sometimes fear
6. **Repeating, agreeing, mirroring** — respect, friendliness, submission

---

## Voice as Body Language

King's key insight: voice is not an abstract symbol but a real, physiological part of the body. Research by Wim Pouw (2020) showed that listeners can detect hand gestures through voice alone — because gestures change muscle tension, which changes voice quality.

Listen to: speed, timbre, volume, pitch, breathing, control.
