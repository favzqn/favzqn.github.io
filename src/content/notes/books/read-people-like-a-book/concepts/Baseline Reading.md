# Baseline Reading

"Before you can identify deviations, you need to know what's normal for that person."

---

## The Principle

Everyone's "normal" behavior is different. An introvert's crossed arms may simply be their comfortable posture, not defensiveness. A loud person's volume is baseline, not aggression. Without establishing a baseline, you're comparing everyone to YOUR normal, not theirs.

---

## How to Establish a Baseline

1. Observe someone in a low-stakes, relaxed situation first
2. Note their typical posture, voice, eye contact, speech patterns
3. Record what "comfortable" looks like for them
4. THEN watch for deviations from that baseline

---

## When Baselines Shift

A baseline is not static. Someone's behavior at work differs from their behavior at home. Someone's baseline in the morning differs from late at night. Different contexts bring different personality traits to the surface.
