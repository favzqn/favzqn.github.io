# Ego Defense Mechanisms

People protect their self-image above almost everything else. Understanding ego helps you predict and interpret behavior.

---

## How the Ego Works

The ego is the mind's self-image — how we see ourselves and how we want to be seen. When this image is threatened, people deploy defense mechanisms:

- **Denial** — refusing to accept reality
- **Projection** — attributing your own unacceptable feelings to others
- **Rationalization** — creating logical excuses for emotional behavior
- **Displacement** — redirecting emotions from the real target to a safer one
- **Regression** — falling back to earlier, simpler behaviors under stress

---

## Reading Ego Defenses

When someone's ego is threatened, you'll see:
- Defensive body language (crossed arms, closed posture)
- Verbal deflection or blame-shifting
- Emotional reactions disproportionate to the situation
- Incongruence between words and body
