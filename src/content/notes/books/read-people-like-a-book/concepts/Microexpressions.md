# Microexpressions

Involuntary facial expressions lasting 1/25th to 1/5th of a second, identified by psychologist Paul Ekman. They reveal concealed emotions that the person is trying to hide.

---

## The Six Universal Emotions

1. **Fear** — eyebrows raised and pulled together, upper eyelids raised
2. **Anger** — eyebrows lowered and pulled together, lips pressed firmly
3. **Surprise** — eyebrows raised, jaw drops, eyes widen
4. **Sadness** — inner eyebrows raised, corners of lips turn down
5. **Happiness** — cheeks raised, corners of mouth pulled back and up
6. **Disgust** — nose wrinkled, upper lip raised

---

## Macro vs Micro

| Feature | Macroexpression | Microexpression |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 0.5-4 seconds | 1/25 - 1/5 second |
| Consciousness | Conscious | Unconscious |
| Fakability | Easily faked | Nearly impossible to fake |
| Reliability | Moderate | High |

---

## The Caveat

Microexpressions are real, but King emphasizes: "There are no guarantees." They are one data point, not proof. A single microexpression of disgust does not mean someone is disgusted with YOU — they may have just tasted something bad.
