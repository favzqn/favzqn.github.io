# Big Five Personality Traits

The OCEAN framework: five broad dimensions that determine personality.

---

## The Five Traits

1. **Openness to Experience** — curious/imaginative vs. practical/conventional. Linked to leadership and creativity.
2. **Conscientiousness** — careful/cautious vs. impulsive/disorganized. Linked to job success, longevity, and health.
3. **Extroversion** — outgoing/social vs. reserved/solitary. Draws energy from others vs. from solitude.
4. **Agreeableness** — kind/sympathetic vs. cold/detached. Cooperates vs. competes.
5. **Neuroticism** — anxious/insecure vs. emotionally stable. Moods shift quickly vs. steady.

---

## Practical Use

King's approach: you can't give everyone a personality test, but you can observe where they fall on these five spectrums through conversation and behavior. A person who is cold, blunt, and doesn't observe social niceties may simply be low in agreeableness — not hostile.

Understanding personality as a persistent pattern saves you from over-interpreting individual moments.
