# Beyond Organic

"We never called ourselves organic — we call ourselves 'beyond organic.' Why dumb down to a lesser level than we are?"

---

## The Concept

Joel Salatin's Polyface Farm represents "beyond organic" farming — a system that goes further than the USDA organic label by:
- Rotating animals through pastures in a symbiotic cycle (chickens follow cows, cows follow chickens)
- Using no chemicals, no confinement, no antibiotics, no external inputs
- Running entirely on solar energy (grass → animal → manure → grass)
- Operating profitably without subsidies

---

## Why Beyond Organic?

Salatin argues that the USDA organic label has been co-opted by industrial interests:
- Organic dairy cows can be confined and grain-fed
- Organic produce can be shipped 3,000 miles
- "Organic" no longer means local, small-scale, or pasture-raised
- The word has been captured by corporations like General Mills and Earthbound Farm

---

## The Grass Cycle

Salatin's system works like nature:
1. Cows eat grass, deposit manure
2. Chickens follow, eating insects stirred up by cows and scratching through manure
3. Chicken manure fertilizes the soil
4. Grass regrows, cows return
5. No chemicals, no waste, no fossil fuel
