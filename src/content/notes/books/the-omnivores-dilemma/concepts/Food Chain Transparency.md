# Food Chain Transparency

"Eating is an agricultural act, as Wendell Berry famously said. It is also an ecological act, and a political act, too."

---

## The Principle

If we could see what lies on the far side of the increasingly high walls of our industrial agriculture, we would surely change the way we eat. The industrial food chain is designed to be opaque — barcodes instead of stories, anonymous meat instead of named animals.

The sustainable food chain is transparent: you know the farmer, you visit the farm, you understand how your food was raised.

---

## The Evidence

- Denmark experimented with barcodes that show farm images, genetics, feed, and slaughter date
- Supermarkets won't adopt this because most industrial meat couldn't withstand scrutiny
- Joel Salatin's customers visit his farm regularly — total transparency
- Industrial CAFOs are fenced, gated, and hidden from public view
