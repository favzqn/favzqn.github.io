# Industrial Corn Monoculture

"By replacing solar energy with fossil fuel, by raising millions of food animals in close confinement, by feeding those animals foods they never evolved to eat, we are taking risks with our health and the health of the natural world that are unprecedented."

---

## The System

Corn has conquered the American food chain through a combination of evolutionary advantages (C-4 photosynthesis, extreme efficiency), government subsidies, and industrial processing. The result: a monoculture that touches everything.

- 97% of what a corn plant is comes from the air
- Corn feeds the cattle in feedlots, the chickens in factories, the fish in farms
- Corn becomes HFCS, maltodextrin, ethanol, adhesives, and hundreds of other products
- A McDonald's meal is overwhelmingly corn: soda (100%), milkshake (78%), nuggets (56%), cheeseburger (52%), fries (23%)

---

## The Collapse of Diversity

Pollan shows how corn monoculture has destroyed the diversified farm:
- Corn pushed animals off the land (feedlots replaced pastures)
- Corn pushed people off the land (mechanization replaced labor)
- The feedlot is a machine for converting corn into beef
- Government subsidies created a surplus that distorted the entire food system
