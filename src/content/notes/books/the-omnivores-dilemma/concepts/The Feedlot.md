# The Feedlot

"37,000 cattle standing dullly or lying around in grayish mud that eventually dawns on you isn't mud at all."

---

## The System

Cattle enter feedlots as calves at 8 months and stay for ~150 days. They stand on their own waste in pens of ~100 animals. The feedlot converts corn into beef — the only reason cattle eat corn at all.

---

## The Health Problems

Cattle evolved to eat grass. Corn makes them sick:
- **Acidosis**: Corn makes the rumen acidic, causing heartburn, ulcers, bloat, liver disease
- **Liver abscesses**: 15-30% of feedlot cattle have abscessed livers; some pens run as high as 70%
- **Bloat**: Fermentation produces gas; without relief, the animal suffocates
- **Immune system collapse**: Leaves them vulnerable to pneumonia, coccidiosis, feedlot polio
- **Learned helplessness**: Animals in confinement stop caring — demoralized pigs allow their tails to be chewed

Antibiotics (Rumensin, Tylosin) keep them alive just long enough to be slaughtered. Most antibiotics sold in America end up in animal feed.
