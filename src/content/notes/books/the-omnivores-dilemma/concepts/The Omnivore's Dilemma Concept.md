# The Omnivore's Dilemma

The fundamental question: "What should we eat?" Nature equipped us to answer this question through our evolved tastes — sweetness signals energy, bitterness signals poison, umami signals protein. But modern agriculture has overwhelmed these signals.

---

## The Problem

Most animals eat one or a few things. Their menus are hardwired. Humans, as omnivores, can eat almost anything — which means we must constantly decide what to eat. This is both our evolutionary advantage and our modern crisis.

The industrial food chain has flooded us with cheap corn-derived food that hijacks our evolved preferences (sweetness, salt, fat) while providing minimal nutrition. The omnivore's dilemma has become: how do we eat well when everything is available but most of it is harmful?
