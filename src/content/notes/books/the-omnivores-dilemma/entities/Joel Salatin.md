# Joel Salatin

Owner of Polyface Farm in Swoope, Virginia (Shenandoah Valley). Self-described "beyond organic" farmer, author, and speaker. Often called the most famous sustainable farmer in America.

---

## Polyface Farm

A 550-acre farm that runs entirely on grass and symbiotic animal relationships:
- Cows graze on pasture in rotational paddocks
- Chickens follow cows, eating insects and scratching through manure
- No chemicals, no confinement, no antibiotics
- Profitable without subsidies
- Customers visit regularly — total transparency

---

## Philosophy

"Make the farm an R&D project instead of a salary project." Salatin's father William started the farm as an experiment after losing everything in Venezuela. Joel turned it into a model for sustainable agriculture.

"We just want to opt out. That's all the Indians ever wanted — to keep their tepees."

---

## The Pessimistic Optimist

Salatin is optimistic that sustainable agriculture can feed the world, but pessimistic about the political will to change. He sees industrial agriculture as a system that externalizes its true costs — environmental damage, animal suffering, human health consequences — onto the public.
