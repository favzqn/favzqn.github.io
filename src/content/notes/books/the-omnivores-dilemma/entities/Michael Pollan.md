# Michael Pollan

American author, journalist, and professor of journalism at UC Berkeley. Known for his books investigating the relationships between humans and the natural world, particularly through food.

---

## Key Works
- *The Omnivore's Dilemma* (2006) — the American food chain
- *In Defense of Food* (2008) — "Eat food. Not too much. Mostly plants."
- *Cooked* (2013) — the transformation of nature into culture through cooking

---

## Philosophy

Pollan's central insight: eating is not just a biological act but an ecological, political, and moral one. Every meal connects you to a chain of consequences — for your health, for the animals, for the land, for the farmers. The more you know about these connections, the more satisfying eating becomes.
