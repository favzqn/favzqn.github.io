# Pratfall Effect

Showing vulnerability makes competent people MORE likable. A small mistake or imperfection humanizes you.

---

## The Research

**Aronson (1966)**: Participants listened to a "superstar" candidate answer quiz questions. In one condition, the candidate knocked over a cup of coffee. Participants highly preferred the candidate who made the mistake.

## The Mechanism

- Perfect people are intimidating
- Small flaws make them relatable
- Vulnerability signals safety — "they're not perfect, so they won't judge me"
- It's the difference between a hard wooden chair and a worn leather couch

## Superman vs. Batman

Superman has no real weaknesses. He's boring. Batman is a human with flaws, fears, and limitations. He's compelling. People connect with vulnerability, not perfection.

## How to Use It

- Don't hide your quirks or eccentricities
- Admit small mistakes openly
- Share minor vulnerabilities
- Your friends like you BECAUSE of your imperfections, not despite them

## Key Insight

> "Your value to your friends definitely doesn't come from a place of perfection. They like you because of your imperfections and what makes you you."
