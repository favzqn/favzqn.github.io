# Speed as Charisma

Quick responses are rated as more charismatic, funny, and intelligent than accurate but slow ones.

---

## The Research

Participants rated friends' quickness, humor, and charisma based on test performance. Those who responded quickly were rated higher on ALL traits — even when their answers were wrong.

> "Quickness was more important than IQ. Instead, we found that how smart people were was less important than how quick they were."

## The Mechanism

- Quick responses signal confidence
- Confidence signals knowledge
- Slow responses signal hesitation
- Hesitation signals uncertainty
- Perception beats reality

## How to Use It

- Respond quickly, even if not perfectly
- Practice free association to build the quick-response muscle
- A quick anything is better than a slow monologue
- Speed activates emotional/perceptual systems, not rational ones

## Key Insight

> "It doesn't matter what you say, just as long as you say something quickly. People are emotion and perception-driven, and quickness activates those."
