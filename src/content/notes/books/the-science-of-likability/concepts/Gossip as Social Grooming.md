# Gossip as Social Grooming

Gossip is the human version of primates picking bugs out of each other's fur — it's how bonds are formed.

---

## The Research

**Robin Dunbar (1998)**: Primates engage in social grooming — physical touch that builds bonds. Humans lost the ability to groom each other (we wear clothes, we're mobile), so we developed language as a substitute. Gossip IS grooming.

## The Mechanism

- Talking about shared social connections creates a bond
- Sharing information about others signals trust
- Gossip always has the option of leading to larger, more intimate conversations
- It doesn't have to be negative — even positive gossip bonds people

## The Danger

> "If you're not talking about yourself, people will make judgments about you based on how you describe other people. They equate how you read other people with your own personality traits."

Positive gossip → you look good
Negative gossip → you look like the evil you describe

## Key Insight

> "What we downplay as gossip can actually be the basis for forming relationships. Gossip always has the option of leading somewhere large and intimate, no matter how small it can start."
