## Core Concepts

- [[Mood Priming]] — trigger positive memories to improve someone's mood
- [[Benjamin Franklin Effect]] — asking enemies for favors makes them like you
- [[Equity Theory (Likability)]] — relationships seek balance; keep score mentally
- [[Similarity Principle]] — we like those who resemble us
- [[Transference and Expectation]] — act the part and people treat you accordingly
- [[Pygmalion Effect]] — people become who you expect them to be
- [[Propinquity and Mere Exposure]] — proximity and repetition create trust
- [[Pratfall Effect]] — vulnerability increases likability
- [[Reactance Theory]] — restrict freedom and people rebel
- [[Speed as Charisma]] — quick responses beat accurate slow ones
- [[Benign Moral Violations]] — humor lives in the sweet spot between offensive and boring
- [[Gossip as Social Grooming]] — talking about others bonds humans
