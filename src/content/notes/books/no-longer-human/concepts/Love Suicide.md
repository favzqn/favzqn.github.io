# Love Suicide

*Shinjuu* (心中) — the Japanese phenomenon of joint suicide, literally "center of the heart."

Yozo and Tsuneko attempt suicide together at Kamakura. She dies. He is saved.

---

## The Context

Yozo has reached the bottom: no money, no purpose, no connection. The underground movement has consumed him. Women have exhausted him. His father has effectively disowned him. When Tsuneko proposes death, he consents "easily" — but with "an element of make-believe."

The suicide is not romantic. It is logistics: "I perceived with clarity that I could not go on living." Tsuneko asks him to pay for milk. He has three copper coins. The humiliation of poverty — not the poetry of death — is what drives him to the water.

---

## The Aftermath

Tsuneko dies. Yozo is saved. He weeps for her — "of all the people I had ever known, that miserable Tsuneko really was the only one I loved." The one person he could be honest with is gone.

The suicide attempt becomes a criminal charge, a newspaper scandal, and a family disgrace. But for Yozo, it is simply the next humiliation in a life of humiliations.
