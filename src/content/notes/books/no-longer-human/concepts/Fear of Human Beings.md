# Fear of Human Beings

Yozo's fundamental emotion is not sadness or anger but **terror** — a visceral, physical fear of other people.

> "I have always shook with fright before human beings. Unable as I was to feel the least particle of confidence in my ability to speak and act like a human being, I kept my solitary agonies locked in my breast."

---

## The Fear Has Layers

1. **Fear of judgment** — any criticism strikes him "with the force of a thunderbolt"
2. **Fear of anger** — when people rage, he sees "a wild animal in its true colors, one more horrible than any lion, crocodile or dragon"
3. **Fear of intimacy** — the front door of another person's house is "more terrifying than the gate of Inferno in the Divine Comedy"
4. **Fear of exposure** — Takeichi's whisper ("You did it on purpose") plunges him into "the raging flames of hell"
5. **Fear of obligation** — every kindness creates a bond he cannot repay

---

## The Result

Fear produces performance (The Clown), which produces isolation, which deepens the fear. It is a closed loop. Yozo cannot escape because the escape route (honesty) requires the very thing he fears most: being seen as he truly is.
