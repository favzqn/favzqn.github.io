# Morphine and Addiction

Yozo's descent into morphine addiction is the final stage of his escape from human existence.

The crippled pharmacy woman gives him morphine "to be used when you need a drink so badly you can't stand it." He takes one shot a day. Then two. Then three. Then four. He cannot work without it.

---

## The Function

Morphine does what clowning could not: it eliminates fear, insecurity, and self-consciousness without requiring performance. "My insecurity, fretfulness and timidity were swept away completely; I turned into an expansively optimistic and fluent talker."

For the first time, Yozo can function without the exhausting effort of The Clown. But the cost is total: he becomes "an out-and-out addict" producing pornographic drawings, borrowing money endlessly, and having "a very ugly affair with the crippled woman from the pharmacy."

---

## The Spiral

> "I want to die. I want to die more than ever before. There's no chance now of a recovery. No matter what sort of thing I do, no matter what I do, it's sure to be a failure, just a final coating applied to my shame."

Addiction completes the loop: fear → clowning → exhaustion → substance → deeper fear → more substance. The only exit is the mental hospital.
