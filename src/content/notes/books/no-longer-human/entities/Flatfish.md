# Flatfish

Yozo's guarantor after the suicide attempt. Named for his resemblance to a flatfish (his eyes especially).

---

## Role

Flatfish is a relative of Yozo's father, an antique dealer who makes money through suspicious property transfers. He takes Yozo in after college expulsion, but his house is more prison than home.

---

## The Speech Problem

Flatfish never says what he means. When Yozo needs to know that his family will fund his education, Flatfish speaks in riddles:

> "If you do not feel like confusing your problems to me I'm afraid there's nothing I can do for you."

> "The question is not the money. It's what you feel."

The simple fact — "your family will pay for school" — is never stated. Yozo, already terrified of human interaction, cannot navigate this ambiguity. He runs away.

---

## The Deeper Meaning

Flatfish represents the adult world's communication style: indirect, guarded, full of "unnecessary precautions" and "incomprehensible vanity." Yozo cannot operate in this world because he takes language literally. When people say one thing and mean another, he is lost.
