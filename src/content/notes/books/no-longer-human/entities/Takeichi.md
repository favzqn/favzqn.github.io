# Takeichi

The puny, unremarkable classmate who sees through Yozo's clowning.

---

## The Moment

During physical training, Yozo deliberately misses the horizontal bar and lands in the sand — a planned comedic failure. Everyone laughs. Takeichi creeps up behind him and whispers: *"You did it on purpose."*

This is the novel's turning point. Yozo's mask — the one thing protecting him from the world — has been pierced. "I felt as if I had seen the world before me burst in an instant into the raging flames of hell."

---

## The Prophecy

Takeichi tells Yozo two things:
1. "You did it on purpose" — you are performing
2. "I'll bet lots of women will fall for you" — a "demoniacal prophecy"

The first prediction comes true: Yozo's clowning is eventually seen through. The second comes true: women are drawn to him. But Takeichi also said Yozo would become a great artist — that prediction fails.

---

## Takeichi's Role

Takeichi is the only person who sees Yozo clearly and says so. He is not cruel about it — he is simply honest. In a world of universal insincerity, Takeichi is the one person who tells the truth.

He represents the possibility that Yozo could have been known — and perhaps accepted. But Yozo's response is not honesty but escalation: he tries to win Takeichi over with more performance, not less.
