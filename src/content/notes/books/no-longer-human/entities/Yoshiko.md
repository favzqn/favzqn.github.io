# Yoshiko

Yozo's wife. A woman of "divine ignorance" — innocent to the point of not understanding what is happening around her.

---

## The Marriage

Yozo marries Yoshiko in a rare moment of stability. She is gentle, trusting, and completely unaware of his inner turmoil. She accepts his moodiness, his silences, his clowning without question.

---

## The Violation

A clerk violates Yoshiko. Yozo watches and does nothing. He describes it with the detached horror of someone who cannot intervene in human affairs — even when the person he should protect is his own wife.

> "I even watched in silence when my own wife was violated."

This is the ultimate expression of Yozo's paralysis. He cannot act. He cannot defend. He can only observe.

---

## The Mental Hospital

When Yozo is taken to the mental hospital, Yoshiko hands him the morphine — still believing it is "energy-building medicine." Her ignorance is both her protection and her tragedy.
