# Josh Kaufman

American author and business educator (born 1984). Author of *The Personal MBA* and *The First 20 Hours*. Known for making complex business concepts accessible to non-business audiences.

---

## Key Ideas

- You don't need an MBA to understand business
- 25 core concepts cover 95% of what you learn in business school
- Learning is a skill — you can master anything quickly
- Business is about creating value for others
- The best businesses solve real problems for real people

---

## Core Works

- *The Personal MBA* (2010) — business literacy without the MBA
- *The First 20 Hours* (2013) — rapid skill acquisition

---

## Background

- Studied at the University of Cincinnati
- Left corporate life to study and write about business
- His blog and newsletter attracted a large following
- The Personal MBA became a New York Times bestseller
- He teaches that business is learnable by anyone
