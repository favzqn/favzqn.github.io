# The Five Parts of Every Business

Every business, no matter how complex, consists of five fundamental activities. Understanding these five parts is the foundation of business literacy.

---

## The Five Parts

1. **Value Creation** — discovering what people need and creating a solution
2. **Marketing** — attracting attention and building trust
3. **Sales** — converting attention into transactions
4. **Delivery** — fulfilling the promise to the customer
5. **Finance** — managing the flow of money

---

## Why It Matters

- Any business problem can be traced to one of these five parts
- If one part is broken, the whole business suffers
- You don't need to master all five — but you need to understand all five
- The best businesses excel at all five simultaneously
