# The Iron Law of the Market

No matter how good your product is, if people don't want it, you don't have a business. Demand must exist before you create supply.

---

## Key Ideas

- Creating something nobody wants is the fastest way to fail
- "If you create something and nobody wants it, you've created waste"
- The biggest risk in business is building something nobody needs
- Test demand before you invest in creation
- "The iron law of the market is brutal: if you don't have demand, nothing else matters"

---

## How to Apply It

- **Validate first** — will people pay for this before you build it?
- **Start small** — test with a minimum viable product
- **Listen to customers** — let them tell you what they need
- **Don't fall in love with your idea** — fall in love with the problem
- **Follow the demand** — go where the money already flows
