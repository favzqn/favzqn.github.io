# Cognitive Biases

Cognitive biases are systematic errors in human thinking that affect decisions and judgments. Understanding them is essential for business, marketing, and negotiation.

---

## Key Biases

- **Loss Aversion** — losses hurt more than gains feel good
- **Anchoring** — the first number sets the frame
- **Social Proof** — people follow what others do
- **Confirmation Bias** — people see what they want to see
- **Availability Heuristic** — recent/vivid events seem more common
- **Sunk Cost Fallacy** — "I've already invested so much"
- **Dunning-Kruger Effect** — incompetent people overestimate themselves
- **Endowment Effect** — you value what you own more than what you don't

---

## How to Use Them

- **Loss Aversion** — frame offers as avoiding losses, not gaining benefits
- **Social Proof** — show that others are buying
- **Anchoring** — present the highest price first
- **Scarcity** — limited availability increases desire
- **Reciprocity** — give something free, and people feel obligated
