# Systems Thinking

Systems thinking is the ability to see the whole system, not just its parts. Every business is a system of interconnected parts — changing one affects all the others.

---

## Key Ideas

- A business is a system, not a collection of parts
- Changing one part affects all other parts
- The best solutions address the system, not the symptom
- Feedback loops drive system behavior
- "The performance of the whole is not the sum of the performance of the parts"

---

## How to Practice

- **Map the system** — draw the connections between parts
- **Find leverage points** — small changes that produce big effects
- **Watch for feedback loops** — reinforcing and balancing
- **Think in cycles** — cause and effect are circular
- **Consider second-order effects** — what happens after what happens?
