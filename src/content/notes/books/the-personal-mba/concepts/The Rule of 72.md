# The Rule of 72

Divide 72 by the annual growth rate to find how many years it takes for something to double. It is the simplest way to understand compound growth.

---

## The Formula

**Years to double = 72 ÷ Annual Growth Rate**

- 1% growth → 72 years to double
- 4% growth → 18 years to double
- 8% growth → 9 years to double
- 12% growth → 6 years to double
- 24% growth → 3 years to double

---

## Why It Matters

- Compound growth is the most powerful force in finance
- Small differences in growth rates produce enormous differences over time
- Understanding compound growth is essential for investing, saving, and business planning
- "Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world"
