# Comparative Advantage

Comparative advantage means doing what you do best — the thing you can do at a lower opportunity cost than anyone else. It is the foundation of specialization and trade.

---

## Key Ideas

- Focus on what you do best — outsource the rest
- You don't need to be the best at everything — just the best at one thing
- "The key to business success is finding the thing you can do better than anyone else"
- Comparative advantage creates win-win trades
- Spreading yourself too thin destroys your advantage

---

## How to Apply

- **Identify your strength** — what comes naturally to you?
- **Do only that** — delegate everything else
- **Trade with others** — let them do what they do best
- **Build skills** — your advantage grows with practice
- **Don't compete on everything** — compete on your strengths
