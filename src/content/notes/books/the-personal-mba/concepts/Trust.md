# Trust

Trust is the foundation of every business transaction. People buy from those they know, like, and trust. Without trust, attention is meaningless.

---

## Key Ideas

- Trust is the bridge between attention and sales
- Trust is built through consistency, transparency, and results
- The best businesses build trust before they ask for money
- Trust is fragile — easy to lose, hard to rebuild
- "People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it"

---

## How to Build Trust

- **Be consistent** — deliver on promises every time
- **Be transparent** — hide nothing, explain everything
- **Show results** — let your work speak for itself
- **Give before you ask** — provide value first
- **Be human** — people trust people, not brands
