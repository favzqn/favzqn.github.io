# 12 Forms of Value

There are 12 fundamental forms of value that businesses can provide. Mastering multiple forms creates powerful competitive advantages.

---

## The 12 Forms

1. **Product** — a tangible physical object
2. **Service** — a performance or task
3. **Shared Resource** — a community or platform others can use
4. **Subscription** — ongoing access
5. **Resale** — buying at wholesale, selling at retail
6. **Lease/Rental** — temporary access
7. **Agency** — acting on someone's behalf
8. **Audience Aggregation** — attention as a product
9. **Loan** — lending assets temporarily
10. **Insurance** — protection against risk
11. **Capital** — investment
12. **Option** — the right to buy/sell at a future price

---

## How to Use Them

- Stack multiple forms to create more value
- A restaurant provides product (food), service (cooking), and audience (ambiance)
- The more forms you combine, the harder you are to copy
- Look for unmet needs — what form is missing?
