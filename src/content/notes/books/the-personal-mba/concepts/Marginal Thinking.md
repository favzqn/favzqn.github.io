# Marginal Thinking

Marginal thinking means evaluating the cost and benefit of doing one more unit of something. It is the foundation of rational economic decision-making.

---

## Key Ideas

- Should I do one more? That's the marginal question
- Fixed costs are sunk — focus on the next unit
- The right price is where marginal cost meets marginal benefit
- "The cost of something is what you give up to get it" (opportunity cost)
- Most business decisions are marginal decisions

---

## How to Apply

- **Price at the margin** — what will the next customer pay?
- **Produce at the margin** — is one more unit worth the cost?
- **Invest at the margin** — is one more hour of work worth it?
- **Hire at the margin** — does this person create more value than they cost?
- **Stop at the margin** — when cost exceeds benefit, stop
