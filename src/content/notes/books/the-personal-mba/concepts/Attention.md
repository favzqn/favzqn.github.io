# Attention

Attention is the scarcest resource in business. Without it, nothing else matters — no one can buy from you if they don't know you exist.

---

## Key Ideas

- No attention = no transaction
- Attention is a prerequisite for every other business activity
- The internet dramatically reduced the cost of getting attention
- Quality attention (from the right people) is more valuable than quantity
- The best marketing doesn't feel like marketing

---

## How to Get Attention

- **Content** — create something useful, entertaining, or remarkable
- **Referrals** — let your customers market for you
- **PR** — newsworthy stories attract attention
- **Advertising** — paid attention (less trusted but effective)
- **Community** — build a tribe that pays attention to you
