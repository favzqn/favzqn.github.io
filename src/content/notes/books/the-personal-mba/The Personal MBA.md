# The Personal MBA

*The Personal MBA: Master the Art of Business* by Josh Kaufman (2010). A comprehensive overview of core business concepts — value creation, marketing, sales, delivery, finance, and human psychology — without the cost of a formal MBA.

---

## Structure

### Part I: Value Creation
- [[The Five Parts of Every Business]] — value creation, marketing, sales, delivery, finance
- [[12 Forms of Value]] — products, services, shared resources, etc.
- The Process of Value Creation — understanding needs, creating solutions

### Part II: Marketing
- [[Attention]] — the first step in marketing
- [[Trust]] — the foundation of every transaction
- Experience — the context of the offering

### Part III: Sales
- The Power of Story — narratives drive decisions
- Negotiation — creating value for both sides
- Utility, Cost, and Value — the pricing framework

### Part IV: Value Delivery
- Working with the Grain — aligning with natural incentives
- [[Comparative Advantage]] — doing what you're best at
- [[The Iron Law of the Market]] — demand must exist first

### Part V: Finance
- The Fundamental Accounting Concepts — profit, loss, cash flow
- [[The Rule of 72]] — compound growth
- [[Marginal Thinking]] — the economics of one more

### Part VI: The Human Mind
- The 4 Reasons People Buy — gain pleasure, avoid pain, seek social approval, be consistent
- [[Cognitive Biases]] — the mental shortcuts that drive decisions
- Incentives — the most powerful force in business

### Part VII: Working with Others
- Personality Types — the five-factor model
- [[Systems Thinking]] — seeing the whole, not just the parts
- Process vs. Outcome — evaluating decisions correctly
