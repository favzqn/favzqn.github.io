# Jose Silva

American self-help author and founder of the Silva Method (1914–1999). Developed the system through decades of experimentation with meditation, hypnosis, and brainwave training.

---

## Key Contributions

- Developed the Silva Method system of meditation and mental training
- Pioneered the use of alpha brainwave states for self-improvement
- Created the Three Finger Technique as a rapid access tool
- Trained millions of people worldwide through his method
- Published *The Silva Mind Control Method* (1978), a bestseller

---

## Background

- Grew up in Laredo, Texas
- Worked as an electronics technician
- Became interested in hypnosis and the mind in the 1950s
- Experimented with children and brainwaves
- Discovered that children could easily enter alpha state
- Developed the Silva Method based on these findings

---

## Core Ideas

- The mind can be trained to enter alpha at will
- Alpha state is the key to healing, creativity, and intuition
- Visualization in alpha produces real physical effects
- Everyone has latent psychic abilities
- The method is practical, not mystical
