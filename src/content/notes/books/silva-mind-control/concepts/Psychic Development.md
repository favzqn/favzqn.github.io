# Psychic Development

Silva claims that the alpha state opens access to psychic abilities — intuition, telepathy, remote viewing, and precognition. These are presented as natural capacities of the mind, not supernatural powers.

---

## The Principle

- Everyone has latent psychic abilities
- The alpha state removes the interference of beta consciousness
- With training, you can access information beyond the five senses
- Intuition is the most common and useful "psychic" ability

---

## Types of Psychic Abilities in the Silva Method

- **Intuition**: Knowing without knowing how you know
- **Telepathy**: Receiving thoughts from others
- **Remote viewing**: Seeing distant locations mentally
- **Precognition**: Sensing future events
- **Healing at a distance**: Sending healing energy to others

---

## How to Practice

- Enter alpha regularly with [[Alpha Meditation]]
- Practice sensing the energy of others
- Use the [[Mental Screen]] to visualize distant people or places
- Trust your first impressions — don't analyze
- Keep a journal of intuitive hits and validations

---

## Skepticism and Openness

- Psychic abilities are controversial in science
- Silva's claims are based on anecdotal evidence, not rigorous studies
- The practical value is in enhanced intuition and awareness
- Approach with curiosity, not belief or disbelief
- What matters is whether the practice improves your life
