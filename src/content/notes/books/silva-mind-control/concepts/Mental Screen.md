# Mental Screen

The mental screen is an internal visualization space — a mental theater where you project images, scenarios, and healing imagery. It is the core tool of the Silva Method.

---

## Definition

A controlled internal space where you visualize outcomes, healing, and solutions. Located in the mind's eye, typically imagined as a movie screen or stage in front of you.

---

## How It Works

1. Enter alpha state using [[The Three Finger Technique]]
2. Imagine a screen in front of you (or behind your forehead)
3. Project vivid, detailed images onto the screen
4. Use present tense: "I am healthy" not "I will be healthy"
5. Engage all senses — see, hear, feel, smell, taste

---

## Uses of the Mental Screen

- **Healing**: Visualize the desired outcome (healthy organ, healed body)
- **Problem-solving**: Project possible solutions and watch them unfold
- **Goal achievement**: See yourself already having achieved the goal
- **Dream programming**: Set up the screen before sleep for dream guidance
- **Creative work**: Visualize completed projects before starting

---

## Key Principles

- The mind cannot distinguish between vivid imagination and reality
- What you visualize with emotion, the body responds to as real
- Present tense is more powerful than future tense
- The more vivid the image, the stronger the effect
- Repetition strengthens the mental pathways
