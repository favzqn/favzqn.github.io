# Sleep Learning

Silva teaches that the mind can learn and absorb information during sleep. By programming the subconscious before sleep, you can use the sleeping hours for self-improvement.

---

## How It Works

1. Before sleep, use [[The Three Finger Technique]] to enter alpha
2. Give yourself a specific suggestion: "Tonight I will learn..."
3. Visualize the information being absorbed by your subconscious
4. Fall asleep with the suggestion active
5. The subconscious processes the information during sleep

---

## Applications

- **Language learning**: Absorb vocabulary and grammar while sleeping
- **Skill acquisition**: Practice mental rehearsal of new skills
- **Memory consolidation**: Strengthen memories from the day
- **Self-improvement**: Program positive habits and beliefs
- **Healing**: Reinforce healing imagery during sleep

---

## Evidence

- Sleep learning is supported by some research on memory consolidation
- The brain replays and strengthens neural pathways during sleep
- Active learning during sleep is limited — but passive absorption is real
- The Silva Method's approach aligns with modern sleep science
