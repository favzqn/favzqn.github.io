# Alpha Meditation

Alpha meditation is the core practice of the Silva Method. It trains the mind to enter and sustain the alpha brainwave state (8–12 Hz), where the mind is relaxed, focused, and highly receptive.

---

## The Three Brainwave States

- **Beta** (14–30 Hz): Normal waking consciousness — thinking, analyzing, worrying
- **Alpha** (8–12 Hz): Relaxed awareness — calm, creative, receptive, focused
- **Theta** (4–7 Hz): Deep meditation — vivid imagery, insight, healing

Most people spend too much time in beta and not enough in alpha. The Silva Method trains you to access alpha at will.

---

## The Practice

1. Use [[The Three Finger Technique]] to enter alpha
2. Count down from 10 to 1 (descending staircase)
3. At the bottom, sit in your mental chair in your [[Mental Screen]]
4. Remain in alpha for 15–20 minutes
5. Use this time for visualization, healing, or problem-solving
6. Count up from 1 to 5 to return to beta

---

## Benefits of Alpha Meditation

- Reduced stress and anxiety
- Improved focus and concentration
- Enhanced creativity and intuition
- Better sleep quality
- Greater self-awareness
- Physical healing through mental imagery

---

## How Alpha Differs from Other Meditation

- **Silva Method**: Active visualization in alpha (not empty mind)
- **Mindfulness**: Observing thoughts without attachment
- **Transcendental Meditation**: Mantra-based, effortless
- **Zazen**: Sitting meditation, posture-focused

The Silva Method is unique in combining relaxation with directed mental imagery.
