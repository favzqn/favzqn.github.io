# Dream Control

Silva teaches that you can program your dreams to provide answers, guidance, and creative solutions. By setting up the mental screen before sleep, you direct the dreaming mind to solve problems.

---

## How It Works

1. Before sleep, use [[The Three Finger Technique]] to enter alpha
2. Set up your [[Mental Screen]]
3. Visualize the problem or question clearly
4. Give yourself a suggestion: "Tonight I will dream the answer to..."
5. Imagine waking up with the answer
6. Fall asleep in alpha

---

## Why It Works

- The subconscious mind works on problems during sleep
- Alpha state before sleep primes the subconscious
- The dreaming mind accesses information the waking mind misses
- Dreams are the mind's natural problem-solving mode

---

## How to Remember Dreams

- Keep a dream journal by your bed
- Write immediately upon waking — before the dream fades
- Set a gentle alarm to wake during REM sleep
- Use the three-finger technique to program dream recall
- The more you record, the more you remember

---

## Applications

- **Problem-solving**: Get answers to complex questions
- **Creative work**: Dream new ideas, music, art
- **Healing**: Dream about health and wholeness
- **Decision-making**: Dream about different choices and see outcomes
- **Learning**: Program the mind to learn during sleep
