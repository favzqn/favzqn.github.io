# Healing in the Mind

Silva teaches that the mind can heal the body through directed visualization in the alpha state. The brain sends signals to every cell; by changing the mental image, you change the physical reality.

---

## The Principle

- Every cell in the body receives signals from the brain
- Disease begins as a mental pattern before it becomes physical
- By visualizing health in the alpha state, you send healing signals to the body
- The body responds to vivid mental imagery as if it were real

---

## How to Practice

1. Enter alpha using [[The Three Finger Technique]]
2. Use the [[Mental Screen]] to visualize the affected area
3. See it healthy, functioning perfectly, glowing with vitality
4. Use present tense: "My body is healing" not "I hope I get better"
5. Engage all senses — feel the warmth, see the health, hear the vitality
6. Repeat daily until the healing manifests

---

## Types of Healing

- **Self-healing**: Healing your own illness or injury
- **Healing others**: Sending healing energy to someone else
- **Preventive healing**: Maintaining health through regular visualization
- **Emotional healing**: Releasing trauma, fear, and negative patterns

---

## Evidence and Skepticism

- Silva claimed high success rates in clinical studies
- Placebo effect is a recognized phenomenon — belief influences health
- Visualization alone should not replace medical treatment
- The method is complementary, not alternative
