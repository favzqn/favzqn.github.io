# Jonathan Haidt

American social psychologist and author (born 1963). Professor at NYU Stern School of Business. Known for his work on moral psychology, political psychology, and the psychology of censorship.

---

## Key Works

- *The Anxious Generation* (2024) — the phone-based childhood crisis
- *The Coddling of the American Mind* (2018, with Greg Lukianoff) — how good intentions are harming young people
- *The Righteous Mind* (2012) — why good people disagree about politics and religion
- *The Happiness Hypothesis* (2006) — ancient wisdom meets modern psychology

---

## Core Ideas

- Childhood was rewired by phones between 2010 and 2015
- Social media is the primary cause of the teen mental health crisis
- Play-based childhood must be restored
- Individual solutions aren't enough — we need cultural norms
- "We are running the world's largest experiment on children"

---

## Background

- BA from Yale, PhD from University of Pennsylvania
- Professor at University of Virginia for 16 years
- Now at NYU Stern School of Business
- Co-founder of heterodoxacademy.org
- His work bridges psychology, politics, and public policy
