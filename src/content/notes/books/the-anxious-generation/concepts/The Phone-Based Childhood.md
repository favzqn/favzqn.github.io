# The Phone-Based Childhood

The phone-based childhood is one where smartphones and social media are the primary infrastructure of adolescent social life. It replaces the play-based childhood that shaped every previous generation.

---

## What It Looks Like

- Social life happens on screens, not in person
- Self-worth is measured in likes, followers, and comments
- Conflict happens in group chats, not on the playground
- Identity is performed online, not discovered through play
- Boredom is immediately relieved by a screen

---

## The Comparison

| Play-Based Childhood | Phone-Based Childhood |
|---|---|
| In-person social life | Digital social life |
| Self-directed play | Algorithm-directed consumption |
| Boredom → creativity | Boredom → screen |
| Conflict resolution | Cyberbullying |
| Risk-taking → growth | Safety → fragility |
| Parents protect from danger | Parents protect from discomfort |
