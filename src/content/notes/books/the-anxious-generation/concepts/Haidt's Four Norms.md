# Haidt's Four Norms

Haidt proposes four cultural norms to reverse the phone-based childhood and restore play-based development.

---

## The Four Norms

1. **No smartphones before high school** — delay smartphone ownership until age 14+
2. **No social media before 16** — delay social media accounts until age 16+
3. **Phone-free schools** — remove phones during school hours
4. **More free play** — give children unsupervised time to play

---

## Why These Work

- They address the root cause (phones), not just the symptoms
- They work best as cultural norms, not individual choices
- They require collective action — one family alone can't solve it
- They restore the conditions for healthy development
- They protect the most vulnerable ages (10–14) during brain development
