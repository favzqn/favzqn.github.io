# Collective Action Problems

The phone-based childhood is a collective action problem — no single family can solve it alone. Parents who restrict their kids' phone use see them socially excluded. This is why cultural norms, not individual choices, are the answer.

---

## Why Individual Solutions Fail

- If my kid doesn't have Instagram, they're left out of social life
- The cost of restriction falls on the individual, but the benefit is collective
- Parents who restrict feel guilty; parents who don't feel anxious
- The default is to give in — and the default wins at scale

---

## How Norms Solve It

- When everyone agrees to wait, no one is left out
- Norms change the default — from "everyone has a phone" to "no one has one yet"
- Norms are enforced by social pressure, not law
- Once a critical mass adopts the norm, it becomes self-reinforcing
- "The only way out is through collective action"
