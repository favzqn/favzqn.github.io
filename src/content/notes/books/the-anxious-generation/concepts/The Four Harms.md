# The Four Harms

Haidt identifies four ways social media specifically harms adolescent development.

---

## The Four Harms

1. **Social Deprivation** — screens replace face-to-face interaction
2. **Sleep Deprivation** — phones in bedrooms destroy sleep
3. **Attention Fragmentation** — constant notifications shatter focus
4. **Addiction** — variable reward loops create compulsive use

---

## How They Work Together

- Social media promises connection but delivers isolation
- Late-night scrolling destroys sleep needed for brain development
- Notifications fragment attention during the day
- The dopamine loops make it impossible to stop
- Together, they create a perfect storm of adolescent vulnerability
