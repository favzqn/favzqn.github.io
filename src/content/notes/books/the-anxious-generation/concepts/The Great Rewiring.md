# The Great Rewiring

Between 2010 and 2015, childhood was fundamentally rewired. Smartphones and social media replaced play-based childhood with phone-based childhood, and the results have been devastating.

---

## What Changed

- **2010**: Most teens had basic phones, no smartphones
- **2012**: Smartphone adoption crosses 50%
- **2015**: Instagram, Snapchat, and group texting become the primary social infrastructure
- **Result**: An entire generation grew up with phones as the center of social life

---

## The Timeline

- Before 2010: play-based childhood — kids played outside, organized their own games
- 2010–2015: the Great Rewiring — smartphones became ubiquitous
- After 2015: phone-based childhood — social media replaced in-person interaction
- The mental health crisis began in 2012 — exactly when smartphone adoption crossed the tipping point

---

## Why It Matters

- This was the largest uncontrolled experiment in human history
- No one designed this — it just happened
- The effects were immediate and dramatic
- Teen depression, anxiety, and suicide rates all spiked simultaneously
- "We are running the world's largest experiment on children"
