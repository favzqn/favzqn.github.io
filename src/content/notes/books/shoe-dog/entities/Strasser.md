# Strasser

Nike's legal strategist who fought the U.S. Customs battle.

---

## Key Facts

- Preppy exterior: blue blazers, pink shirts, bow ties
- Accent suggested summers in Newport, rowing for Yale
- Could be very witty, even silly — then change on a dime to scary serious
- Dispatched to Washington, D.C. to be closer to politicians
- His obsession with the Customs case made some people question his stability

---

## His Role

- Led the fight against U.S. Customs seizure of Nike shoes
- Phil's philosophy: "Obsessives were the only ones for the job"
- Trusted by Phil because Strasser trusted Werschkul
- His persistence won the legal battle that saved Nike
