# Bob Woodell

Operations manager of Blue Ribbon Sports. Wheelchair-bound after a car accident, but his mind was unstoppable.

---

## Key Facts

- Car accident left him wheelchair-bound
- Phil saw his talent for problem-solving
- Became operations manager — "one small task" was all he needed
- Cash his own paychecks and explain the overage to himself
- "Still had the grip of an athlete"

---

## His Role

- Brought order to chaos
- Handled everything Phil didn't have time for: correspondence, hiring, expense accounts
- The operational backbone of Blue Ribbon

---

## His Legacy

- Proved that physical limitations don't define capability
- His loyalty and problem-solving skills were essential to Nike's survival
- One of the "misfits" who built the company
