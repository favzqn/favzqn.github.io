# Jeff Johnson

Blue Ribbon's first employee. Vision-driven, intense, relentless.

---

## Key Facts

- First employee of Blue Ribbon Sports
- Lived in the factory, ate, slept, breathed the business
- Wrote the company's early mission and values
- Had an apartment with a 75-gallon saltwater fish tank and an octopus named Stretch
- "Feeding time" — watching Stretch eat crabs was a dark metaphor for business

---

## His Role

- Vision-driven — saw what Nike could become before anyone else
- Wrote the foundational values that shaped the company
- His intensity could be overwhelming, but it was exactly what Blue Ribbon needed
- Phil relied on him for everything

---

## His Legacy

- One of the founding figures of Nike
- His values and vision shaped the company's culture
- The first to believe in the "Crazy Idea"
