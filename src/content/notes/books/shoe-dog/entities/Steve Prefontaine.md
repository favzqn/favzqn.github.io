# Steve Prefontaine

"Pre." Legendary American runner. Blue Ribbon Sports' first athlete endorsee. Died at 24.

---

## Key Facts

- "Somebody may beat me — but they're going to have to bleed to do it"
- Phil's first athlete signing
- Known for his aggression and never-give-up attitude
- Drove a butterscotch MG bought with his first Blue Ribbon paycheck
- Died in a car accident in 1975, age 24

---

## His Legacy

- His spirit became Nike's DNA
- Phil was devastated by his death — it drove him harder
- The Pre font at Oregon's Hayward Field
- "The race is not always to the swift, but to those who keep running"
- His intensity and fire became the heart of Nike
