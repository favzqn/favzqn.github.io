# Phil Knight

Founder of Nike. Author of *Shoe Dog*. A runner who became a businessman — and never stopped running.

---

## Key Facts

- Born in Portland, Oregon
- Stanford MBA — wrote the paper that became Nike
- Backpacking trip around the world before starting business
- Started Blue Ribbon Sports in 1964 with $500 from his father
- Renamed to Nike in 1978
- CEO from 1964 to 2004
- Board chairman to this day

---

## His Personality

- Shy, introspective, poetic
- Hated conflict but fought when necessary
- "Always somewhat restless, somewhat twitchy"
- Loved running more than business
- Married to Penny — "she waited, and waited"

---

## His Philosophy

- Trust your crazy ideas
- Surround yourself with people smarter than you
- Debt is a constant companion — embrace it
- The marathon matters more than the sprint
- Culture > Strategy

---

## His Legacy

- Built Nike from a $500 loan to a $30 billion company
- His memoir *Shoe Dog* is considered one of the best business memoirs ever written
- "Just Do It" — the slogan that embodies his philosophy
