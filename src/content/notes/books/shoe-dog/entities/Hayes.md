# Hayes

Operations and buying manager at Blue Ribbon/Nike. Known for his eccentric decisions and bulldozer fetish.

---

## Key Facts

- Went to Maine to buy a rubber mill at an auction
- Fell in love with the auction site — an old redbrick factory on an island in the Saco River
- Bought the factory for $200,000 without asking Phil
- After drinking, declared: "At prices like this? A man can't afford not to drink!"
- Later revealed he was "pulling Phil's chain" — but Phil still had to buy it

---

## His Role

- Handled purchasing and factory operations
- Brought energy and humor to the team
- His impulsive decisions sometimes drove Phil crazy
- But his instincts were usually right
