# Bill Bowerman

Track coach at the University of Oregon. Co-founder of Nike. The man who made Phil Knight a tiger.

---

## Key Facts

- Lived on a mountaintop — "his private Olympus"
- Rigged a mailbox with explosives to teach a trucker a lesson
- "Patton with a stopwatch"
- Co-founded Blue Ribbon with Phil Knight — 50/50 partnership
- Invented the waffle sole (Nike's first innovation)
- "WBW" — Whatever Bowerman Wants

---

## His Method

- "I'll make you a tiger" — break down, rebuild
- Forced Phil to run when sick — posted his best time
- Tested his athletes relentlessly
- Created the environment that produced Pre, Knight, and Nike

---

## His Legacy

- Won multiple national championships at Oregon
- Produced dozens of Olympic athletes
- His innovations in shoe design shaped the entire industry
- His partnership with Phil created the foundation of Nike
