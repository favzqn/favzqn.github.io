# Penelope Knight

Phil Knight's wife. The quiet strength behind Nike's founder.

---

## Key Facts

- Married to Phil for over 50 years
- "She waited, and waited"
- Waited while he journeyed, while he got lost
- Waited night after night while he made his slow way home — usually late, the dinner cold
- Waited the last few years while he relived it all on the page

---

## Her Role

- The emotional anchor of the family
- Raised the children while Phil built the company
- Supported Phil through every crisis — bankruptcy, lawsuits, near-death
- Phil's dedication in *Shoe Dog*: "Penny, I couldn't have done it without you"

---

## Key Lesson

> Behind every great founder is someone who waited, supported, and believed — even when the founder wasn't sure himself.
