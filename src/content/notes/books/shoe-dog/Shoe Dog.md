## Business Lessons

1. Start with a crazy idea and chase it relentlessly
2. Surround yourself with people smarter than you
3. Loyalty and trust are more valuable than money
4. Nearly dying teaches you what matters
5. The best companies are built by misfits
6. Debt is a constant companion — embrace it
7. Culture > Strategy
8. The marathon matters more than the sprint
