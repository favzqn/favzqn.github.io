# Near-Death Experiences

The constant crises that almost killed Blue Ribbon and Nike.

---

## The Bank of California

- Phil's bank for years
- Then one day: "We don't bank with you anymore"
- Phil was devastated — called it the worst moment of his life
- Had to find a new bank: First State Bank of Oregon
- Bank president promised $1 million in credit — "about his bank's limit"

---

## The IRS Battle

- The U.S. government dunned Nike for $25 million
- Claimed Knight was a tax cheat
- Phil had to fight the IRS while running the company
- Nearly lost everything
- Werschkul was dispatched to Washington, D.C. to fight the battle

---

## The Onitsuka Betrayal

- Onitsuka (Japanese shoe company) tried to take over Blue Ribbon
- They announced the "acquisition" to the Japanese press without Phil's knowledge
- Phil was caught in a "tawdry love triangle" — Onitsuka vs. Nissho
- Had to simultaneously fight and keep the peace

---

## The Customhouse Crisis

- U.S. Customs seized Nike shoes
- Claimed they were mislabeled
- Phil had to fight a legal battle while the company was vulnerable

---

## Key Lesson

> Nearly dying teaches you what matters. Every crisis stripped away the nonessential and revealed what the company — and Phil — truly stood for.
