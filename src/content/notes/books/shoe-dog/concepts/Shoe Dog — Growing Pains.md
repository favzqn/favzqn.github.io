# Growing Pains

The painful process of growing from startup to established company.

---

## The Exeter Factory

- Blue Ribbon's first real factory in Exeter, New Hampshire
- The factory was old and needed constant repairs
- Hayes bought a second factory in Saco, Maine — on impulse

---

## International Expansion

- The company expanded globally
- Fought U.S. Customs over shoe imports
- Strasser and Werschkul fought the legal battles in Washington

---

## The Financial Roller Coaster

- Constantly short on cash
- Phil went to banks begging for loans
- First State Bank of Oregon gave them $1 million — about the bank's limit
- The IRS dunned them for $25 million

---

## Key Lesson

> Growth is painful. Every step forward reveals new problems. The key is to keep running — even when it hurts.
