# The Birth of Nike

The transition from Blue Ribbon Sports to Nike.

---

## The Nike Name

- Phil wanted a name that evoked victory
- The Greek goddess of victory: Nike
- The Acropolis, the Parthenon — ancient power
- Simple, strong, easy to pronounce

---

## The Swoosh

- Carolyn Davidson, a graphic design student
- Created the "wing-whoosh thingamajig"
- Phil's reaction: "I don't love it, but maybe it'll grow on me"
- It grew on the entire world

---

## The Orange Box

- Phil wanted boxes that stood out on shelves
- Chose neon orange — the boldest color in the rainbow
- White lowercase "nike" on the side
- Became iconic

---

## The Chicago Show (1972)

- National Sporting Goods Association Show
- This was Nike's debut — "Super Bowl and Olympics and Bar Mitzvah"
- The first Nikes weren't great quality — shiny leather, crooked swoosh
- But sales reps placed orders
- Nike was born

---

## Key Lesson

> The name, the logo, the packaging — they all start rough. But consistency and persistence make them iconic.
