# The Crazy Idea

Phil Knight's Stanford seminar paper on Japanese running shoes became the foundation of Nike.

---

## The Origin

- Stanford MBA class on entrepreneurship
- Wrote a research paper arguing Japanese running shoes could disrupt the German-dominated market (like cameras did)
- Professor gave him an A. Classmates reacted with "formal boredom"
- But the idea wouldn't leave him — it evolved from assignment to obsession

---

## The Backpacking Trip

- Before launching the business, Phil traveled around the world
- Visited temples, churches, shrines across Asia, Middle East, Europe
- Craved "connection with a capital C" — Tao, Logos, Dharma, Spirit
- Walked the track before running the race

---

## The Pitch to Dad

- Needed his father's approval (and money)
- Pitched the idea in the TV nook while his dad watched Red Buttons
- Dad said yes — $500 loan
- That $500 started everything

---

## Key Lesson

> Trust your crazy ideas. The things that captivate you — even if others find them boring — might be the things that change the world.
