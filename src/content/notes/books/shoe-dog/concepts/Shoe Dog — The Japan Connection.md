# The Japan Connection

The complex relationship with Onitsuka (Japanese shoe company) that both built and nearly destroyed Blue Ribbon.

---

## The Origin

- Phil's Stanford paper argued Japanese shoes could disrupt the market
- He traveled to Japan in 1962 to pitch Onitsuka on a partnership
- Met with Onitsuka executives — they were interested
- Blue Ribbon became the U.S. distributor for Onitsuka Tigers

---

## The Relationship

- Phil and Strasser managed the Japan relationship
- Nissho Iwai was the Japanese trading company that financed imports
- Phil felt like "a married man caught in a tawdry love triangle"

---

## The Betrayal

- Onitsuka tried to take over Blue Ribbon
- They announced the "acquisition" to the Japanese press without Phil's knowledge
- Phil had to fight to keep control of his own company
- This betrayal pushed Phil to create Nike — his own brand

---

## Key Lesson

> Never depend entirely on one partner. When the relationship turns, you need your own brand, your own identity, your own future.
