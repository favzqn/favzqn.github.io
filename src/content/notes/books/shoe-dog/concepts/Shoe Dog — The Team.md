# The Team

Building a culture of misfits who became a family.

---

## The Misfits

- Phil hired people who didn't fit anywhere else
- Jeff Johnson — intensity that could be overwhelming
- Bob Woodell — wheelchair-bound, became operations genius
- Del Hayes — bought a factory on a whim while drunk
- Strasser — preppy exterior, obsessive interior
- They were "odd, brilliant, crazy people"

---

## The Culture

- No corporate structure in the early days
- Everyone did everything
- Late nights at the kitchen table, mapping the future
- "I wish, on just one of those nights, I'd had a tape recorder"
- The spirit came from the people, not the office

---

## Loyalty

- Woodell would cash his own paychecks and explain the overage to himself
- Johnson lived in the factory
- Phil would drive hours to see Pre run
- When Phil needed to fire someone, it was always personal

---

## The Family

- Penny was the backbone — "she waited, and waited"
- The company grew from a few guys in an apartment to 68,000 employees
- But the spirit remained: misfits, dreamers, runners

---

## Key Lesson

> Hire people who are smarter, crazier, and more passionate than you. Then trust them completely. The best companies are built by misfits.
