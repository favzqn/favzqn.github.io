# Running as Philosophy

The runner's mindset as a metaphor for business and life.

---

## The Runner's Creed

- "Somebody may beat me — but they're going to have to bleed to do it" — Steve Prefontaine
- Bowerman: "I'll make you a tiger" — breaking down and rebuilding
- The race is won in the last 200 yards
- Dig deep when it hurts most

---

## Bowerman's Method

- Rigged a mailbox with explosives to teach a lesson
- Forced Phil to run even when sick — posted one of his best times
- "Who's the coach of this team?"
- Tough love creates toughness

---

## The Marathon Mindset

- Business is a marathon, not a sprint
- Phil was always running — literally and figuratively
- "I'll make you a tiger" became the company's spirit
- The long game wins

---

## Pre's Legacy

- Steve Prefontaine — the most famous runner of his generation
- First athlete Phil ever signed
- His death at 24 was devastating
- His spirit lived on in Nike's DNA
- "The race is not always to the swift, but to those who keep running"

---

## Key Lesson

> Business is a marathon. Run with purpose. Run with passion. Run until you bleed. And then keep running.
