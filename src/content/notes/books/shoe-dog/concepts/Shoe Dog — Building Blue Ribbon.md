# Building Blue Ribbon

The early days of Blue Ribbon Sports — from apartment to legitimate company.

---

## The Beginning

- Phil started Blue Ribbon Sports in 1964
- First office: Phil's apartment
- First product: Japanese Tiger running shoes (Onitsuka)
- Bowerman was the co-founder — 50/50 partnership
- First order: 1,000 pairs of Tigers, $1,000

---

## The Growth

- Jeff Johnson was the first employee — lived in the factory
- Bob Woodell became operations manager after his accident
- They sold shoes out of the back of cars at track meets
- "Selling Tigers out of Tigard" — humble beginnings

---

## The Struggle

- Phil worked at Price Waterhouse during the day
- Ran Blue Ribbon on evenings and weekends
- Slept very little
- "I was always somewhat restless, somewhat twitchy"

---

## Key Lesson

> Every great company starts in someone's apartment. The key is to start, and to never stop.
