# The Partnership

The founding partnerships that built Blue Ribbon and eventually Nike.

---

## Phil Knight + Bill Bowerman

- Bowerman was Phil's track coach at Oregon — "Patton with a stopwatch"
- Phil pitched him on Japanese shoes at the Cosmopolitan Hotel
- Bowerman wanted in: "Fifty-fifty"
- Phil was terrified: "I was daring to negotiate with God"
- Bowerman put up $500 — half the first order
- This partnership became the backbone of everything

---

## Phil Knight + Jeff Johnson

- Johnson was the first employee — vision-driven, intense
- Wrote the company's early mission and values
- Lived in the factory, ate, slept, breathed the business
- His apartment was a fish tank with an octopus named Stretch
- Phil relied on Johnson's relentless energy

---

## Phil Knight + Bob Woodell

- Woodell became operations manager after a car accident left him wheelchair-bound
- Phil saw his talent for problem-solving and organization
- "One small task" was all Woodell needed
- His grip was still that of an athlete

---

## Key Lesson

> Great companies are built by partnerships — find people who are smarter than you, trust them completely, and never let them go.
