# Chris Voss

Former FBI hostage negotiator and author of *Never Split the Difference*. He spent 24 years in the FBI, including four years as the lead international kidnapping negotiator.

---

## Key Contributions

- Developed tactical empathy as a negotiation framework
- Pioneered the use of hostage negotiation tactics in business
- Created the "Black Swan" negotiation methodology
- Teaches at Georgetown University's McDonough School of Business
- His company, The Black Swan Group, trains Fortune 500 companies

---

## Core Ideas

- Tactical empathy: feeling the other side's feelings and letting them know
- The power of "No" — people need to say no to feel safe
- "That's right" is the breakthrough phrase
- Mirroring and labeling build trust and rapport
- Calibrated questions give the illusion of control
- Black Swans change everything — always look for them

---

## Background

- Born in 1962
- Joined the FBI in 1986
- Became the lead international kidnapping negotiator in 2003
- Negotiated with terrorists, kidnappers, and hostage-takers worldwide
- Left the FBI in 2007 to teach negotiation to businesses
- His methods are based on real-world, life-or-death negotiations
