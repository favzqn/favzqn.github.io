# Labeling

Labeling is the art of identifying and naming the other person's emotions. "It seems like..." or "It sounds like..." gives voice to what they're feeling.

---

## How It Works

- They say: "I've been working 80-hour weeks and I'm exhausted"
- You label: "It sounds like you're feeling overwhelmed and underappreciated"
- They respond: "Exactly! That's exactly what it is"
- Now they feel heard — and you've built trust

---

## Types of Labeling

- **Positive labeling**: "It seems like you're excited about this opportunity"
- **Negative labeling**: "It sounds like you're worried about the outcome"
- **Immediate labeling**: Label emotions as they arise in the moment
- ** retrospective labeling**: "Looking back, it seems like that was a difficult time"

---

## Why It Works

- Naming an emotion reduces its power
- It shows you're listening and understanding
- It disarms defensiveness — hard to argue with someone who gets you
- It creates a sense of safety and trust
- It gives you control over the emotional dynamic

---

## How to Practice

- **Start with "It seems like..." or "It sounds like..."**
- **Be specific** — don't just say "you seem upset"
- **Be wrong sometimes** — being wrong is better than being ignored
- **Label emotions, not thoughts** — feelings, not opinions
- **Stay silent after labeling** — let them confirm or correct
