# Mirroring

Mirroring is repeating the last 1–3 words someone just said. It builds rapport by insinuating similarity and encourages the other person to elaborate.

---

## How It Works

- They say: "I've been under so much pressure at work"
- You mirror: "Pressure at work?"
- They continue: "Yes, my boss keeps piling on projects and I can't keep up"
- You mirror: "Can't keep up?"
- They keep talking — and you learn more

---

## Why It Works

- Mirrors are subconsciously perceived as a sign of similarity
- Similarity builds trust and rapport
- It encourages the other person to keep talking
- It gives you time to think while appearing engaged
- It costs nothing and works immediately

---

## How to Practice

- **Repeat the last 1–3 words** — not more
- **Use a downward inflection** — make it a statement, not a question
- **Stay silent after mirroring** — let them fill the space
- **Don't mirror emotions or tone** — just the words
- **Use in conversations, meetings, and conflicts**
