# That's Right

"That's right" is the phrase that signals a breakthrough in negotiations. When the other side says "That's right," they feel understood and are ready to move forward.

---

## "That's Right" vs "You're Right"

- **"That's right"** = They feel understood and validated → breakthrough
- **"You're right"** = They want you to shut up and go away → no progress

---

## How to Get "That's Right"

1. **Mirror** their key phrases
2. **Label** their emotions
3. **Summarize** their position in your own words
4. When you hear "That's right," you've won the emotional battle

---

## The Summary Formula

1. "It seems like..." (label)
2. "It sounds like..." (label)
3. "It feels like..." (label)
4. Summarize: "So what you're saying is..."
5. Pause and wait for "That's right"

---

## Why It Works

- People want to feel understood more than they want agreement
- "That's right" means they trust you to understand them
- Once they feel understood, they open up to solutions
- It shifts the conversation from adversarial to collaborative
- It is the most powerful phrase in negotiation
