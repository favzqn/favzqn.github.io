# Anchoring

Anchoring is the practice of setting the first number in a negotiation. The first number becomes the reference point — the negotiation orbits around it.

---

## Why Anchoring Works

- The first number becomes the "anchor" — all subsequent numbers are compared to it
- People adjust from the anchor, but not enough — they stay close to it
- An extreme anchor pulls the negotiation in your direction
- Even a ridiculous anchor shifts the conversation

---

## The Ackerman Model

1. **Set your target price** — your goal
2. **Anchor your first offer at 65%** of your target
3. **Use empathy and calibrated questions** to reduce their resistance
4. **Raise your offer** in increasing increments: 85%, 95%, 100%
5. **Use a precise, non-round number** at the end: $37,893 instead of $38,000
6. **On your final number**, throw in a non-monetary item to signal your limit

---

## How to Practice

- **Always anchor first** — if you don't, they will
- **Make the anchor extreme** — but not absurd
- **Use precise numbers** — $47,385 feels more researched than $50,000
- **Don't apologize for the anchor** — state it with confidence
- **Be prepared to walk away** — the anchor only works if you're willing to leave
