# The Accusation Audit

The accusation audit is the practice of naming every negative thing the other side could say about you before they say it. It disarms criticism by acknowledging it first.

---

## How It Works

- Before the negotiation, list all the negative things they might think or say
- Then voice them: "You're probably thinking I'm being greedy..."
- "You might feel like I'm trying to take advantage of you..."
- "You could be thinking I don't care about your situation..."
- The other side thinks: "They get it — I don't need to attack"

---

## Why It Works

- It takes the power away from the negatives
- It shows self-awareness and humility
- It builds trust — you understand their perspective
- It defuses criticism before it starts
- It creates a sense of safety and fairness

---

## How to Practice

- **Before any negotiation**, list the negatives:
  - What might they think about you?
  - What fears might they have?
  - What objections might they raise?
- **Voice them at the start** — not in the middle
- **Use a calm, matter-of-fact tone** — not defensive
- **Be specific** — "You might think I'm being unfair about the timeline"
