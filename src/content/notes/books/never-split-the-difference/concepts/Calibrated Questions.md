# Calibrated Questions

Calibrated questions are open-ended "How" and "What" questions that give the other side the illusion of control while guiding them toward your desired outcome.

---

## The Key Questions

- "How am I supposed to do that?" — makes them solve your problem
- "What about this is important to you?" — uncovers their interests
- "How can we solve this problem?" — makes them collaborate
- "What are we trying to accomplish here?" — reframes the negotiation
- "How does this affect you?" — builds empathy

---

## Why They Work

- "How" and "What" questions give the other side a sense of control
- They make the other side feel heard and respected
- They force the other side to think about your constraints
- They shift the burden of solving the problem to the other side
- They are non-confrontational — you're asking, not demanding

---

## How to Practice

- **Replace "Why" with "How"** — "Why did you do that?" vs "How did you approach that?"
- **Use "What" instead of "Why"** — "What about this is important?" vs "Why is this important?"
- **Stay silent after asking** — let them think
- **Don't ask "Why"** — it sounds accusatory
- **Use calibrated questions to redirect** when the conversation stalls
