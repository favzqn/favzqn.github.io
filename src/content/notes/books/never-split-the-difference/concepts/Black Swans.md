# Black Swans

Black Swans are unknown unknowns — pieces of information that, if discovered, can completely change the negotiation. They are the hidden levers that make deals work.

---

## The Concept

- Nassim Taleb's "black swan" theory: rare, unpredictable events that change everything
- In negotiation: there is always something you don't know that would change everything
- Finding the black swan is the key to breakthrough
- Every negotiation has at least one black swan

---

## Where to Find Black Swans

- **Cultural differences** — what's normal in their culture?
- **Personal fears** — what are they afraid of losing?
- **Hidden needs** — what do they need but won't say?
- **Decision-makers** — who else is involved?
- **Deadlines** — are they under time pressure?
- **Past experiences** — what happened before?

---

## How to Discover Black Swans

- **Ask calibrated questions** — "How am I supposed to do that?"
- **Label emotions** — feelings reveal hidden needs
- **Use the accusation audit** — naming negatives uncovers fears
- **Listen for "That's right"** — it means you've found something real
- **Be curious** — the black swan is always hiding in plain sight
