# Late-Night FM DJ Voice

The late-night FM DJ voice is a calm, slow, downward-inflecting tone that conveys authority and empathy. It is the most effective voice for negotiations.

---

## How It Works

- Speak slowly and calmly
- Use a downward inflection at the end of sentences
- Project warmth and concern, not aggression
- Think: late-night radio host, not morning DJ

---

## The Three Voices

1. **The late-night FM DJ voice** — calm, slow, downward inflection → best for negotiations
2. **The direct/positive voice** — assertive, confident → good for giving instructions
3. **The playful/positive voice** — upbeat, energetic → good for building rapport

---

## Why It Works

- A calm voice de-escalates tension
- Downward inflection signals authority and confidence
- People mirror the emotion they hear — calm voice = calm response
- It creates a sense of safety and trust
- It forces you to slow down and think before speaking

---

## How to Practice

- **Slow down** — speak at half your normal speed
- **Lower your pitch** — deeper voice = more authority
- **Use downward inflection** — "Let's solve this problem." not "Let's solve this problem?"
- **Stay calm under pressure** — especially when they get loud
- **Practice in low-stakes situations** — phone calls, meetings
