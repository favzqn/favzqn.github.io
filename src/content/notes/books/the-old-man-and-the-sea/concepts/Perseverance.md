# Perseverance

Santiago perseveres beyond all reason — three days fighting a marlin alone, old, starving, with a cramping hand and no help.

---

## Key Ideas

- "I may not be as strong as I think, but I am not so weak"
- Santiago fishes alone because no one will go with him
- He perseveres not for money or fame but for himself
- "I will show him what a man can do and what a man endures"
- Perseverance is not about winning — it's about not stopping

---

## How Santiago Perseveres

- He talks to himself and the marlin
- He remembers the lions on the beach — his source of strength
- He takes care of his body — eats, rests, adjusts
- He accepts the pain without complaining
- He focuses on the next action, not the outcome
