# Grace Under Pressure

Hemingway's ideal: maintaining dignity, composure, and style in the face of adversity. Santiago embodies this throughout his ordeal.

---

## Key Ideas

- Grace under pressure means staying composed when everything falls apart
- Santiago never complains, never panics, never gives up
- He faces each challenge with calm determination
- Even when the sharks come, he fights back with dignity
- "He was too simple to wonder when he had attained humility. But he knew he had attained it"

---

## What It Looks Like

- Staying calm when the line cuts your hand
- Accepting pain without screaming
- Fighting back even when you know you'll lose
- Returning to sea after 84 days of failure
- Smiling at the boy with the skeleton behind you
