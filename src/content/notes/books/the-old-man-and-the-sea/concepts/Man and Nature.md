# Man and Nature

Santiago's relationship with the sea is one of respect, not domination. He takes from nature but acknowledges its power and beauty.

---

## Key Ideas

- Santiago calls the sea "la mar" — feminine, nurturing, not masculine and hostile
- He sees the fish as a brother, not prey
- He respects the dolphins, turtles, and stars
- Nature is not the enemy — it is the arena of meaning
- "Everything kills everything else in some way"

---

## The Balance

- Nature gives and takes — the marlin feeds the sharks
- Man takes and gives — Santiago takes the fish, gives respect
- The struggle is not against nature but within it
- Man is part of nature, not above it
- Santiago's respect for nature is his greatest strength
