# Dignity in Defeat

Hemingway's central theme: a man can be destroyed but not defeated. Defeat is external; dignity is internal.

---

## Key Ideas

- "A man can be destroyed but not defeated"
- Santiago loses the marlin to sharks but retains his honor
- The skeleton is a testament to his battle, not his failure
- True defeat is giving up, not losing
- The body breaks; the spirit endures

---

## The Paradox

- Santiago "loses" — the marlin is eaten by sharks
- But he "wins" — he proved his worth to himself and others
- The boy still believes in him
- Other fishermen respect the skeleton
- Defeat on the outside; victory on the inside
