# The Old Man and the Sea

*The Old Man and the Sea* by Ernest Hemingway (1952). A novella about an aging Cuban fisherman's epic battle with a giant marlin in the Gulf Stream. Hemingway's last major work of fiction.

---

## Structure

- Santiago's 84 days without catching a fish
- The three-day battle with the great marlin
- The sharks that strip the marlin to a skeleton
- Santiago's return and the meaning of defeat
