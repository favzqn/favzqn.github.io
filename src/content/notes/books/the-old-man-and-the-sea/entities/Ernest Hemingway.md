# Ernest Hemingway

American novelist, short-story writer, and journalist (1899–1961). Nobel Prize in Literature (1954). Known for his economical, understated prose style.

---

## Key Works

- *The Old Man and the Sea* (1952) — his last major work
- *A Farewell to Arms* (1929) — WWI novel
- *For Whom the Bell Tolls* (1940) — Spanish Civil War
- *The Sun Also Rises* (1926) — the Lost Generation
- *The Snows of Kilimanjaro* (1936) — short story masterpiece

---

## Core Ideas

- "Grace under pressure" — the ideal of courage and composure
- The iceberg theory — what's left out is as important as what's included
- Dignity in defeat — a man can be destroyed but not defeated
- Simplicity as depth — fewer words, more meaning

---

## Legacy

- His prose style influenced virtually all modern English fiction
- Nobel Prize for *The Old Man and the Sea*
- The "Hemingway hero" — the tough, graceful, stoic man
- His life was as dramatic as his fiction — war, adventure, bullfighting, deep-sea fishing
- Died by suicide in 1961
