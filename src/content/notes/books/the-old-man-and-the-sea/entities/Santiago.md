# Santiago

The protagonist of *The Old Man and the Sea*. An aging Cuban fisherman who has gone 84 days without catching a fish. He is Hemingway's ideal of dignity, perseverance, and grace under pressure.

---

## Key Qualities

- **Perseverance** — 84 days without a fish, still goes out
- **Dignity** — never complains, never gives up
- **Respect** — treats the marlin as a brother
- **Humility** — accepts his place in nature
- **Courage** — fights sharks with a harpoon and a knife

---

## The Journey

1. 84 days without a fish — everyone thinks he's unlucky
2. Goes far out alone — beyond where others dare
3. Hooks the great marlin — fights it for three days
4. Kills the marlin — a victory of will
5. Sharks attack — he fights but loses the flesh
6. Returns with a skeleton — defeated but not destroyed
7. Sleeps and dreams of lions — the cycle continues
