# Woody Allen

Screenwriter and director known for his extraordinary productivity.

---

## Key Facts

- 44 films in 44 years (1969–2013)
- 23 Academy Award nominations
- Never owned a computer — wrote everything on a manual typewriter
- German Olympia SM3 manual typewriter
- Free from electronic distraction

---

## Relevance

Example of extreme productivity through deep work. His rejection of computers kept him free from digital distraction, allowing sustained creative output.
