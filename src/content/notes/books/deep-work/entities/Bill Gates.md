# Bill Gates

Microsoft co-founder. Example of extreme deep work ability.

---

## Key Facts

- Started Microsoft in 8 weeks of obsessive focus (winter 1974)
- Saw the Altair on the cover of Popular Electronics
- Would collapse into sleep on his keyboard mid-line of code
- Paul Allen: "a prodigious feat of concentration"
- Walter Isaacson: "The one trait that differentiated [Gates] was focus"

---

## Think Weeks

- Conducted twice a year
- Isolated himself in a lakeside cottage
- Read and thought big thoughts
- 1995 Think Week: wrote "Internet Tidal Wave" memo → turned Microsoft toward Netscape
