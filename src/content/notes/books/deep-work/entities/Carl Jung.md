# Carl Jung

Swiss psychiatrist. Built Bollingen Tower in 1922 as a retreat for deep thinking — not escape from work, but to advance it.

---

## Bollingen Tower

- Located in Swiss canton of St. Gallen, near Lake Zurich
- Basic two-story stone house, expanded with private office after trip to India
- No electricity — oil lamps and fireplace
- Routine: rise 7am, big breakfast, 2 hours undistracted writing, afternoon meditation/walks, bed by 10pm

---

## Context

- Published *Psychological Types* (1921) — disagreed with Freud
- Needed to stay sharp, produce articles/books for analytical psychology
- Was busy with lectures and counseling in Zurich but wanted *deeper* thought
- Retreated to Bollingen to advance his work, not escape it

---

## Legacy

One of the most influential thinkers of the 20th century. His commitment to deep work (though he didn't call it that) played a key role. Example of the [[Bimodal Philosophy]] — weeks at the Tower, then weeks in Zurich.
