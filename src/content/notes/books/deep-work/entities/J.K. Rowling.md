# J.K. Rowling

Author of the Harry Potter series. Famously absent from social media during writing.

---

## Key Facts

- Wrote Harry Potter novels without social media presence
- Staff started Twitter account in 2009 (during The Casual Vacancy)
- First tweet: "This is the real me, but you won't be hearing from me often I am afraid, as pen and paper is my priority at the moment."
- Prioritized deep creative work over online presence

---

## Relevance

Example of protecting deep work by avoiding social media, even as it rose in popularity among media figures.
