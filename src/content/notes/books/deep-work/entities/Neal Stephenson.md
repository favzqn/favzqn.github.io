# Neal Stephenson

Acclaimed cyberpunk author who helped form our popular conception of the Internet age.

---

## Key Facts

- Near impossible to reach electronically — no email on website
- Purposefully bad at using social media
- His explanation: "If I organize my life in such a way that I get lots of long, consecutive, uninterrupted time-chunks, I can write novels. [If I instead get interrupted a lot] what replaces it? Instead of a novel that will be around for a long time… there is a bunch of e-mail messages that I have sent out to individual persons."

---

## Relevance

Irony: the author who imagined the Internet age deliberately avoids it to produce his work. Deep work requires protecting attention from the very technology he writes about.
