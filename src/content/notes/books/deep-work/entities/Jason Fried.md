# Jason Fried

Co-founder of Basecamp (formerly 37signals). Implemented the 4-day workweek.

---

## Key Facts

- Co-founded 37signals/Basecamp
- Shortened workweek from 5 to 4 days in 2007
- Productivity didn't drop — shallow work was the fat that got squeezed out
- "People should enjoy the weather in the summer"
- "The point of the 4-day work week is about doing less work"
