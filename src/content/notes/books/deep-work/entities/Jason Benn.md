# Jason Benn

Former financial consultant who transformed his career through deep work. Key example in Newport's introduction.

---

## Before

- Graduated University of Virginia, economics degree
- Hired at financial firm producing reports for banks
- Report creation: 6 hours of manual Excel manipulation
- Built macros to automate it to a button click
- Realized his skills could be captured in an Excel macro
- Admitted to spending "98% of his time" surfing the web some days

---

## The Transformation

- Quit job, moved home
- Locked himself in a room with no computer — only textbooks, notecards, highlighter
- Highlighted textbooks, transferred ideas to notecards, practiced out loud
- Eventually clocked 5+ disconnected hours/day
- Read ~18 books on programming
- After 2 months, attended Dev Bootcamp (100-hour/week crash course)

---

## After

- Graduated top of class (only 50% graduated on time)
- Landed developer job at SF tech startup ($25M venture funding)
- Salary: $40k → $100k
- Rented apartment across from office for early morning deep work
- "On good days, 4 hours of focus before first meeting, then 3-4 more in afternoon"
