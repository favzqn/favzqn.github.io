# Cal Newport

Author of *Deep Work*. Theoretical computer scientist, Georgetown University professor.

---

## Background

- Doctoral training in MIT's Theory of Computation group
- Tenure-track professor at Georgetown University
- Published 4 books, earned PhD, wrote peer-reviewed papers at high rate — all in 10 years post-college graduation
- Rarely works past 5-6pm

---

## His Practice

- No Facebook, Twitter, or social media (only a blog)
- No web surfing, gets news from Washington Post and NPR
- Author website has no personal email
- First smartphone in 2012 (wife's ultimatum before son born)
- 3-4 hours/day of deep work, 5 days/week
- Builds days around core of deep work, batches shallow tasks at peripheries
- Doesn't touch computer between work arrival and next morning

---

## Key Ideas

- Coined "Deep Work" and "Shallow Work"
- The Deep Work Hypothesis
- Deep work as "the superpower of the 21st century"
- Four depth philosophies: monastic, bimodal, rhythmic, journalistic
