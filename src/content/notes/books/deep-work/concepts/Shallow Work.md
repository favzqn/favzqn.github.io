# Shallow Work

Noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted. These efforts tend to not create much new value in the world and are easy to replicate.

---

## Characteristics

- Performed while distracted
- Noncognitively demanding
- Logistical-style tasks
- Easy to replicate
- Does not create new value
- Often feels urgent but is dispensable

---

## Examples

- Sending and receiving email like "human network routers"
- Quick hits of distraction between larger efforts
- Automated by Excel macros (Jason Benn's old job)
- Meetings that could be emails
- Social media posting

---

## The Problem

Knowledge workers increasingly replace deep work with shallow work. Spend enough time in frenetic shallowness and you permanently reduce your capacity for deep work.

The 37signals experiment showed that when they cut the workweek from 5 to 4 days, productivity didn't drop — because the shallow work was the fat that got squeezed out.
