# Bimodal Philosophy

Divide time into clear deep and shallow periods. Dedicate extended blocks to depth.

---

## How It Works

- Alternate between periods of deep focus and regular life
- Deep periods: days or weeks of uninterrupted depth
- Shallow periods: handle obligations, meetings, email

---

## Example: Carl Jung

- Weeks at Bollingen Tower (deep work in the woods)
- Weeks in Zurich (lectures, counseling, meetings)
- Switched between modes regularly

---

## When to Use

- When you need extended periods of depth but can't go monastic
- When your work allows for seasonal or periodic deep stretches
- When you need to maintain relationships/obligations but still want depth
