# Rule #4: Drain the Shallows

Minimize shallow work by scheduling every minute and setting boundaries.

---

## Fixed-Schedule Productivity

Set a hard stop time for your workday and stick to it. This forces you to:
- Eliminate shallow work
- Protect deep work time
- Be ruthlessly efficient with what remains

Example: Jason Fried at Basecamp — 4-day workweek, same productivity.

---

## Key Strategies

### 1. Schedule Every Minute
- Account for every hour of your day
- Plan the day in advance, not reactively
- Adjust the schedule as needed, but always have a plan

### 2. Quantify Depth
- Track hours of deep work per day
- Use this as your lead measure
- Aim to increase over time

### 3. Ask: What Would Happen If I Stopped Doing This?
- For every shallow task, ask if it's truly necessary
- If stopping wouldn't matter, stop doing it
- Batch necessary shallow work into smaller blocks

### 4. Become Indispensable
- Focus on deep work that creates rare and valuable skills
- The more indispensable you become, the more control you have over your schedule

---

## Key Example: 37signals/Basecamp

- Cut workweek from 5 to 4 days
- Productivity didn't drop
- Shallow work was the fat that got squeezed out
- "People become stingy with their time and that's a good thing"
