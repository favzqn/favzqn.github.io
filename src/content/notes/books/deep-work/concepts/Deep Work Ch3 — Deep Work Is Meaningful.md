# Deep Work Is Meaningful (Chapter 3)

The philosophical argument: deep work produces a meaningful, satisfying life.

---

## Three Arguments for Meaning

### 1. Craftsmanship
Deep work connects you to your craft at a fundamental level. Ric Furrer, a blacksmith, spends 8 hours hammering a Viking sword — every blow is carefully controlled, producing mastery and satisfaction.

### 2. Neurological
Our brains construct our worldview based on what we pay attention to. A deep life focused on meaningful things produces a meaningful worldview.

### 3. Social
Deep work produces things that are valuable and rare, which in turn generates social reward and recognition.

---

## Key Examples

- **Ric Furrer:** Blacksmith in Wisconsin, creates Viking-era swords by hand — every hit carefully controlled
- **Duke Chapel:** Built by Duke University over 5 years, created a lasting symbol and community anchor

---

## Key Insight

Deep work is not just economically valuable — it's how humans find meaning. When you focus deeply on meaningful work, your life feels meaningful. Distraction produces a scattered, less satisfying existence.
