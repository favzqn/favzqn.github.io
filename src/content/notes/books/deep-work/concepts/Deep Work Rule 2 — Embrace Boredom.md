# Rule #2: Embrace Boredom

Train your mind to focus by avoiding distraction even outside of work.

---

## Core Idea

The ability to concentrate is a skill that improves with practice. If you can't sustain attention during boring moments, you won't be able to sustain it during deep work.

---

## Key Strategies

### 1. Schedule Deep Work Sessions
- Set specific times for deep focus
- Practice sitting with your thoughts during downtime
- Don't default to phone/boredom killers

### 2. Meditate Productively
- During walks or commutes, focus on a single problem
- Don't let your mind wander to easy stuff
- Practice keeping attention on one thing

### 3. Practice Deep Scheduling
- Account for every minute of your day
- Schedule blocks for deep work, shallow work, and breaks
- Review at end of day to improve

---

## Key Example: Adam Marlin

- Orthodox Jew, daily Talmud study
- Started in his twenties, now "dances intellectual circles" around Ivy League graduates
- Daily practice of stretching to cognitive limits improved his business creativity

---

## Key Insight

Deep work is a muscle. Train it by embracing boredom and avoiding the default to distraction.
