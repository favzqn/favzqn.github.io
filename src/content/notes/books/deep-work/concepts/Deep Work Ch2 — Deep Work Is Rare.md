# Deep Work Is Rare (Chapter 2)

Despite deep work's economic value, it's becoming rarer in the modern workplace.

---

## Three Trends Working Against Depth

1. **Open offices:** Facebook's "largest open floor plan," Square's shared desks — serendipity over focus
2. **Instant messaging:** IBM sends 2.5M IMs/day internally — constant interruption
3. **Social media presence:** NYT encourages 800+ employees to tweet — distraction as norm

---

## The Paradox

Deep work is more valuable than ever, yet companies actively discourage it through:
- Open office plans
- Instant messaging culture
- Social media mandates
- Busyness as proxy for productivity

---

## Why This Happens

- **Principle of Least Resistance:** In a business environment, it's easier to be busy than to think deeply
- **Busyness as Proxy:** Being busy in a visible way is often used as a proxy for productivity
- **Metrics of Visible Activity:** What gets measured gets managed, but depth is hard to measure

---

## Key Insight

The modern workplace actively undermines deep work through its norms and tools. Companies that recognize this gap can gain a competitive advantage.
