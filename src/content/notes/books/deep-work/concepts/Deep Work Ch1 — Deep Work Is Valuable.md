# Deep Work Is Valuable (Chapter 1)

Part 1 of *Deep Work* makes the economic argument: deep work produces rare and valuable skills.

---

## Core Argument

Three types of abilities are becoming rare and valuable:
1. **Ability to work with smart machines** (technology)
2. **Ability to create art, produce emotion, exercise judgment** (creative/human)
3. **Ability to build relationships** (social)

The best way to develop these abilities is through deep work.

## The Two Cultures

- **Hub-and-Spoke model:** Bureaucratic companies need both collaboration and depth
- **Pragmatic Bubbles:** Small teams of deep workers can outperform large teams of shallow workers (Basecamp, small VC firms)

---

## Key Insight

Deep work is the economic engine. The economy increasingly rewards those who can produce at an elite level, and deep work is how you get there.
