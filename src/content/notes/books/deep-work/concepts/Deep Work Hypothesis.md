# Deep Work Hypothesis

> The ability to perform deep work is becoming increasingly rare at exactly the same time it is becoming increasingly valuable in our economy. As a consequence, the few who cultivate this skill, and then make it the core of their working life, will thrive.

This is the foundational thesis of Cal Newport's *Deep Work*.

---

## Two Strands

1. **Increasing scarcity:** Open offices, social media, instant messaging fragment attention
2. **Increasing value:** Information economy rewards deep, hard-to-replicate work

---

## The Opportunity

Most knowledge workers are stuck in shallowness. Those who resist this trend and prioritize depth gain a massive economic and personal advantage.

---

## Evidence

- Nate Silver: 70%+ of NYTimes traffic on election night
- David Heinemeier Hansson: created Ruby on Rails, works from Malibu
- John Doerr: $3B+ net worth through deep venture analysis
- Bill Gates: started Microsoft in 8 weeks of obsessive focus
