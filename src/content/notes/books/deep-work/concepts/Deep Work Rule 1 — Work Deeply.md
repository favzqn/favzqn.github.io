# Rule #1: Work Deeply

How to actually implement deep work in your life.

---

## The Problem

Willpower is limited. You can't just "try harder." You need a system.

---

## Four Depth Philosophies

### [[Monastic Philosophy]]
Eliminate or radically reduce shallow obligations. Go all-in on depth.
- Example: Donald Knuth — no email, no web, writes books
- Best for: writers, researchers, solo practitioners

### [[Bimodal Philosophy]]
Divide time into clear deep and shallow periods. Dedicate extended blocks to depth.
- Example: Carl Jung at Bollingen — weeks at the Tower, then weeks in Zurich
- Best for: people with periodic deep needs

### [[Rhythmic Philosophy]]
Build a regular daily habit of deep work. Same time every day.
- Example: J.K. Rowling — writes every morning
- Best for: most people, most situations

### [[Journalistic Philosophy]]
Fit deep work wherever you can. Switch on and off quickly.
- Example: Walter Isaacson — wrote while waiting for appointments
- Best for: experienced deep workers only

---

## Build Rituals

- Where you'll work
- How long
- What rules (no internet, specific outputs)
- How you'll support the effort (coffee, walks, etc.)

---

## Execute Like a Business

Four principles:
1. **Focus on what's important** (WIG — Wildly Important Goal)
2. **Act on lead measures** (hours of deep work, not results)
3. **Keep a compelling scoreboard** (track deep hours)
4. **Create a cadence of accountability** (weekly review)
