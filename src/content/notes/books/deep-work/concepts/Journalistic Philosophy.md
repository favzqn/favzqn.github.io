# Journalistic Philosophy

Fit deep work wherever you can. Switch on and off quickly.

---

## How It Works

- No fixed schedule for deep work
- Seize opportunities when they arise
- Switch into deep mode quickly, even in small windows

---

## Example: Walter Isaacson

- Wrote biographies while waiting for appointments
- Used any available 30-minute block
- Switched between deep and shallow rapidly

---

## When to Use

- Only for experienced deep workers
- When you've already trained your concentration
- NOT for beginners — you need the monastic, bimodal, or rhythmic philosophy first
