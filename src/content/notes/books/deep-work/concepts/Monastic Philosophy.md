# Monastic Philosophy

Eliminate or radically reduce shallow obligations. Go all-in on depth.

---

## How It Works

- Cut out meetings, emails, social media
- Focus almost exclusively on deep work
- Best for: writers, researchers, solo practitioners

---

## Example: Donald Knuth

- Computer scientist, author of *The Art of Computer Programming*
- No email
- No web
- No office hours
- Spends years on each book chapter

---

## When to Use

- When your work is primarily solo
- When you can eliminate most shallow obligations
- When depth is your primary value driver
