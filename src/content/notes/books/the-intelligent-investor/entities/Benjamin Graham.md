# Benjamin Graham

Benjamin Grossbaum (1894–1976), known as the father of value investing. Born in London, moved to New York. Columbia University professor, Wall Street practitioner, author of *Security Analysis* (1934) and *The Intelligent Investor* (1949).

---

## Legacy

Graham transformed investing from superstition and guesswork into a modern profession. Before Graham, money managers operated like a medieval guild. After Graham, investing had a rigorous analytical framework.

His two key contributions:
1. **Security Analysis** — the textbook that professionalized money management
2. **The Intelligent Investor** — the first book to describe the emotional framework and analytical tools essential to financial success

---

## Philosophy

- Buy stocks below their intrinsic value (margin of safety)
- Treat the market as a servant, not a master (Mr. Market)
- Control your emotions — the investor's worst enemy is himself
- Simplicity over complexity
- Patience over activity

---

## Students

Graham taught at Columbia University. His most famous students include Warren Buffett and Walter Schloss, who went on to become among the greatest investors of all time.
