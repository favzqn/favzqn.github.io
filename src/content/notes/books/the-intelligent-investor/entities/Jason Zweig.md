# Jason Zweig

Financial journalist and commentator. Wrote the updated commentary for the 4th edition of *The Intelligent Investor* (2003). Former senior writer at *Money* magazine, columnist at *The Wall Street Journal*.

---

## Role in the Book

Zweig preserved every word of Graham's original 1973 text while adding modern commentary that illustrates how Graham's principles apply to contemporary markets. His commentary covers:

- The dot-com bubble and crash (2000-2002)
- The rise of index funds
- Behavioral finance research
- Modern portfolio theory critiques
- Survivorship bias in historical returns

---

## Key Contributions

Zweig emphasizes that Graham's principles are timeless — the specific examples change, but the psychology does not. He notes: "The Intelligent Investor was the first book ever to describe, for individual investors, the emotional framework and analytical tools that are essential to financial success."
