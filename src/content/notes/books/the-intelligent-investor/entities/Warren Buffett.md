# Warren Buffett

Warren Edward Buffett (1930–), chairman and CEO of Berkshire Hathaway. Student of Benjamin Graham at Columbia University. Called Graham's book "by far the best book on investing ever written."

---

## The Connection to Graham

Buffett credits Graham with three key lessons:

1. **Think independently** — don't follow the crowd
2. **Margin of safety** — buy below intrinsic value
3. **Mr. Market** — use the market's irrationality

---

## The Superinvestors Speech

In 1984, Buffett delivered "The Superinvestors of Graham-and-Doddsville" at Columbia University, demonstrating that Graham's value investing approach consistently beat the market. This speech is included as Appendix 1 in The Intelligent Investor.

---

## Buffett's Evolution

While Graham taught Buffett to buy "cigar butt" stocks (cheap, second-rate companies), Buffett (influenced by Charlie Munger) evolved to buying wonderful companies at fair prices rather than fair companies at wonderful prices. But the core principle — margin of safety — remained constant.
