# Mr. Market

Graham's famous allegory for the stock market: Mr. Market is a manic-depressive business partner who shows up every day offering to buy your shares or sell you his. Some days he's euphoric, offering high prices. Other days he's depressed, offering low prices.

---

## The Lesson

Mr. Market is there to serve you, not to instruct you. You are free to accept his offer or ignore him entirely. He will be back tomorrow with a different price and a different mood.

The intelligent investor takes advantage of Mr. Market's irrationality — buying when he's depressed (low prices, high margin of safety) and selling when he's euphoric (high prices, thin margin of safety).

---

## The Trap

Most investors do the opposite: they buy when Mr. Market is euphoric (because prices are going up) and sell when he's depressed (because prices are going down). This is the "rule of opposites" — the more enthusiasm investors show, the more certain they are to be proved wrong in the short run.
