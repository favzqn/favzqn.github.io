# Protein for Fat Loss

The most important macronutrient for body composition during a deficit.

---

## Why Protein Matters

1. **Highest TEF** — 20-30% of protein calories are burned during digestion
2. **Most satiating** — keeps you fuller longer than carbs or fat
3. **Preserves LBM** — prevents muscle loss during a deficit
4. **Leucine drives MPS** — branched-chain amino acid stimulates muscle protein synthesis
5. **Anti-catabolic** — prevents muscle breakdown

## Dosing

- **General**: 1.6-2.2g per kg of lean body mass
- **During dieting**: higher end of the range
- **Maximum studied safely**: 4.4g per kg of body weight (no documented negative effects)

## The Research

A study feeding 4.4g/kg of protein (almost 300 extra kcal/day) prevented fat gain in a surplus. The thermic effect was so high it offset the excess calories.

## Key Myths Debunked

- **"Protein damages kidneys"** — no evidence in healthy people, even at high intakes
- **"Protein causes cancer"** — correlation ≠ causation; confounded by overall calorie intake
- **"Protein damages liver"** — no evidence in healthy people

## Key Insight

> "Protein is the master macro. If you get one thing right, get protein right."
