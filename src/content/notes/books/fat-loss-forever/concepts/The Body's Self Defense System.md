# The Body's Self Defense System

The body fights weight loss with a three-pronged attack designed to restore depleted energy reserves.

---

## The Three Prongs

### 1. Slow
Metabolic adaptation reduces TDEE. The body becomes more "efficient" — it runs on less energy. BMR, NEAT, and EA all decrease.

### 2. Restore
Hunger hormones drive weight regain. Ghrelin ↑, leptin ↓. The body also increases refeeding efficiency — you store MORE fat from the same calories post-diet than pre-diet.

### 3. Prevent
Fat cell hyperplasia — if you regain weight too fast, the body creates NEW fat cells. These cells are permanent. They make future weight loss harder and weight regain easier.

## The Evolutionary Logic

> "If you are deliberately placing your body in an energy deficit by attempting to diet frequently, what signal are you sending your body? You are telling your body that energy is sparse and that it needs to defend its energy stores."

The body doesn't know you're dieting on purpose. It thinks you're starving. So it fights to survive.

## The Set Point

Leptin acts as an adipose tissue thermostat. When fat cells shrink, leptin drops, and the body fights to return to its "set point." Maintaining a reduced weight for several years may establish a new set point.

## Key Insight

> "Any weight loss strategy that fails to acknowledge and plan for this emerging metabolic influence is likely to have little success in facilitating long-term weight management."
