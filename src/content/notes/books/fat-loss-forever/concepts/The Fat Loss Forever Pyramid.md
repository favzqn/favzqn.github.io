# The Fat Loss Forever Pyramid

A hierarchy of fat loss priorities, from most to least important.

---

## The Pyramid (Bottom to Top)

| Level | Component | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Behavior & Lifestyle | Sustainability and adherence |
| 02 | Caloric Deficit | Required for fat loss |
| 03 | Exercise | Resistance training preserves LBM |
| 04 | Self-Monitoring | Weigh-ins, food tracking, body fat |
| 05 | Protein & Fiber | Maximize satiety, preserve muscle |
| 06 | Carbs & Fat Macros | Distribute based on preference |
| 07 | Supplements | The cherry on top |

## How to Use It

Start from the bottom. Don't worry about supplements (level 7) if you haven't established a caloric deficit (level 2). Don't stress about carb/fat ratios (level 6) if you're not tracking protein (level 5).

## Key Insight

> "The most important thing is adherence. The best theoretical diet is useless if you can't stick to it."
