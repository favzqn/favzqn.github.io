# Energy Balance

Calories in vs. calories out regulates weight. No diet violates thermodynamics.

---

## The Equation

**TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) = BMR + NEAT + EA + TEF**

- **BMR** (Basal Metabolic Rate): ~60-70% of TDEE — energy at rest
- **NEAT** (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis): fidgeting, walking, daily movement
- **EA** (Exercise Activity): structured exercise
- **TEF** (Thermic Effect of Food): energy to digest food (protein = 20-30%, carbs = 5-10%, fat = 0-3%)

## The Research

Multiple metabolic ward studies confirm: when calories and protein are equated, weight loss is the same regardless of carb/fat ratio. The carbohydrate-insulin model (CIM) — which claims carbs cause fat gain through insulin — is debunked by multiple controlled studies.

## What Actually Matters

1. **Calorie deficit** — required for weight loss
2. **Protein intake** — preserves muscle, highest TEF, most satiating
3. **Fiber** — reduces "net" calories, adds satiety
4. **Carb/fat ratio** — personal preference; doesn't significantly affect fat loss

## Key Insight

> "If you don't lose weight, then you weren't in a caloric deficit. Period."

Many people believe CICO doesn't work because they ate at what "should have been" a deficit based on calculations. But calculations are estimates — the body is the final arbiter.
