# Diet Breaks

Periodic returns to maintenance calories during a diet to interrupt metabolic adaptation and preserve lean body mass.

---

## The Tasmania Study

University of Tasmania study: two groups dieted for the same total deficit weeks. One group dieted continuously; the other took 2-week breaks at maintenance.

**Results**: The diet break group lost MORE fat AND retained MORE lean body mass. However, it took them 30 weeks to complete 16 weeks of actual dieting.

## How It Works

1. Diet for 2 weeks at a deficit
2. Return to maintenance for 1-2 weeks
3. Repeat until goal is reached

The breaks allow:
- Hormonal recovery (leptin, thyroid)
- Metabolic rate to partially normalize
- Psychological relief (adherence improvement)
- Better LBM retention

## Norton's Preferred Method

> "I really like a two-week deficit phase followed by a one-week diet break. Adherence can improve quite a bit when people realize they can push through 14 days of deficit to have a week at maintenance."

## Key Insight

Diet breaks don't make the diet easier — they make it longer but more sustainable. The trade-off is time vs. adherence.
