# Metabolic Adaptation

The body's defense mechanism that slows metabolism during caloric restriction. What was once a deficit becomes maintenance — and weight loss stalls.

---

## How It Works

Every component of TDEE decreases during dieting:

- **BMR**: decreases as body mass decreases + hormonal changes (thyroid, leptin)
- **NEAT**: decreases significantly — you fidget less, move less, take fewer steps
- **EA**: decreases — you burn less during exercise
- **TEF**: absolute amount decreases (less food = less thermogenesis)

## The Biggest Loser Study

Participants from *The Biggest Loser* demonstrated over 20% metabolic adaptation that persisted SIX YEARS after the show ended. Their metabolic rate was still suppressed, making it nearly impossible to maintain weight loss.

## The Hormonal Cascade

- **Leptin** ↓ (satiety hormone drops — you feel hungrier)
- **Ghrelin** ↑ (hunger hormone rises — you want to eat more)
- **Thyroid hormones** ↓ (metabolism slows)
- **Testosterone** ↓ (recovery and muscle maintenance impaired)

## Key Insight

> "Every time you diet, the body learns to become more efficient at storing energy. Each subsequent diet becomes harder."
