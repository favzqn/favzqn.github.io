# Confrontation Without Conflict

Confronting someone without making them defensive — the praise-criticism-praise sandwich.

---

## The Praise-Criticism-Praise Sandwich

1. **Start positive** — make them comfortable, not defensive
2. **Insert critique** — tactfully, including positive aspects
3. **End positive** — so their response is to the positive, not the criticism

If you dive straight into criticism, you initiate a defensive posture that poisons everything after.

## Focus on Consequences, Not Judgments

- BAD: "You need to park better."
- GOOD: "I keep having to park on the lawn, which means my shoes get muddy. Would it be possible to shift your parking up a few yards?"

You're not making a judgment about them. You're pointing out how you're being negatively affected. This spurs them to want to fix it.

## Walk Them Through the Analysis

Don't state the conclusion. Lay the premises out logically and let them reach the conclusion themselves. They take ownership of your critique.

## Be Solution-Oriented

Focus on actionable solutions, not who's right. Sometimes it's more productive to treat the symptoms than to address the underlying issue. "Agree to disagree" and find a compromise.

## Key Insight

> "Countless friendships have been destroyed by unhealthy and misguided confrontation. Part of social success is dealing with uncomfortable situations in smooth and graceful ways."
