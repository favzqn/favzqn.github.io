# First Impression Engineering

We only get one shot. Once a negative impression is formed, everything after is viewed through that lens.

---

## The Five Pillars

### 1. Eye Contact

In western culture, lack of eye contact conveys evasion, weakness, or dishonesty. But don't stare — that's a challenge.

**Smile with your eyes**: lift your eyebrows (non-threatening) and crinkle your eyes at the edges. The easiest way: genuinely smile.

### 2. Expressive Voice

Words don't convey what you want without proper expression and inflection. The difference between being good at sarcasm and being an asshole is entirely in the voice. Exaggerate positivity ten times beyond what you think is necessary.

### 3. Focus on Similarities

People open up to those who resemble them. When you find similarities, the person sees themselves in you — and who doesn't like themselves?

### 4. Reflect Emotional Cues

Laugh when they laugh. Be somber when they're somber. This isn't fake — it's social lubricant. When they feel you get them emotionally, they let you in further.

### 5. Consistent Body Language

Words, tone, voice, and body must agree. Inconsistent signals sabotage trust. Avoid: crossed arms, leaning back, fidgeting, covering your face.

## Key Insight

> "Making a great first impression involves both verbal and non-verbal signals. You need a high level of consistency so whatever signals you are sending lead to higher levels of intimacy, confidence, and trust."
