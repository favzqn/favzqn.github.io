# Conversation Exit Strategy

The recency effect means people remember the last thing best. How you leave a conversation shapes their entire memory of you.

---

## The Recency Effect

The most recent part of an interaction is what people remember best. A great conversation with a fumbled exit becomes a bad memory.

## The Four Rules

### 1. Give Them Opportunities to Finish Stories

If someone is partway through a story, let them finish. Interrupting or leaving mid-story ruins the emotional payload. They'll walk away feeling misunderstood.

### 2. Deliver the Feedback They Seek

People engage for entertainment, information, or pleasure. Identify what they want and give it to them before you leave.

### 3. Use a Witty Summation

End with a joke or open-ended comment that can be the basis of future conversation. Examples:
- "Interesting conversation on the bathroom habits of ants… I'll keep in mind the tips you gave me!"
- "Wow, okay, I need to go look up and verify that fact… right now!"

### 4. Don't Overstay

If you see one-word answers or they stop facing you, leave. Don't be the person who requires rescuing.

## Key Insight

> "You can be as conversationally fluent and charming during a conversation, but if you leave it in the wrong way, it can seriously skew people's perception of you in a negative way."
