# Digital Conversation

Without body language, facial expressions, and tone, digital communication strips most of the message. What remains is dangerously incomplete.

---

## The Problem

We communicate most of our message through non-verbal means. Digital strips three of four channels. A simple smile or gesture is completely lost. "What did he mean 'I'll see you later?'" — passive-aggressive? Funny? Sarcastic? Oblivious?

## The Rules

### 1. Make It 100% Crystal Clear

If in doubt, clarify. Taking a quick aside to explain your intent is always less painful than the alternative — your best friend thinking you don't want to help them move.

### 2. Smile on the Phone

Smiling changes your voice — it adds warmth and emotion. Studies show our minds follow our bodies: smiling literally improves your mood. Start taking calls standing and smiling.

### 3. Emote More Than You Think Necessary

Without visual cues, you need to exaggerate your vocal expression. What feels like a lot to you isn't much in reality.

## Key Insight

> "If you are ever in doubt of the message you are sending, make 100% crystal clear of the intent to the other person. The alternative is always worse."
