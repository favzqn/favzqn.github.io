# Social Cue Reading

Social cues are verbal and non-verbal hints that guide interaction implicitly. They tell us what people are really saying.

---

## Key Cues

### Nodding

Sign of agreement — or at least making themselves agreeable to you. Either way, it's good vibes. Use this openness to ask deeper questions.

### Mentioning a Topic

If someone mentions a specific person, event, or object, they want to talk about it. Don't let the allusion slide — ask about it. It's foreshadowing.

### One-Word Answers

Every question met with one-word answers and no vocal inflection = this person is done talking to you. Don't overstay your welcome. If they don't turn to face you, it's doubly finished.

### Leaning In

Engaged and interested. Take advantage by asking more pointed questions and branching to related subtopics.

### Adding New Details

When they add a new detail or hint at a new story, they want to explore it. Follow the thread.

## Key Insight

> "Social cues reduce the amount of ambiguity in the communications we receive from others. It's why we can say something and mean completely the opposite."
