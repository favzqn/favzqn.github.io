# Storytelling as Cornerstone

Conversation is a series of interesting remarks and related stories. Great storytelling is why you remember someone as a great conversationalist.

---

## The Three Rules

### 1. Focus on the Central Point

Before telling the story, know the thesis. Every detail must relate to the central point or provide context for it. Poor storytellers talk on and on and never get to the point.

### 2. Ensure Emotional Payload

People are emotional creatures. We make emotional decisions and justify them rationally. Your story must make people feel something — laughing, crying, angry, moved. Otherwise the reaction is "So what?"

### 3. Paint a Picture

Let them smell what you smell. Make the colors vivid. Add sound and dynamism. Great stories make you feel like you are there. Don't reduce your story to a series of instructions.

## Key Insight

> "Great storytellers make great first impressions in social settings because they are great communicators. They are able to help us to connect better with our emotions and intellect."
