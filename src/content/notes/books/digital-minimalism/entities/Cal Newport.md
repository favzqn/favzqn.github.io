# Cal Newport

American computer science professor at Georgetown University and author. Known for his books on productivity, focus, and the relationship between technology and meaningful work.

---

## Core Works

- *Deep Work* (2016) — focused work in a distracted world
- *Digital Minimalism* (2019) — choosing a focused life in a noisy world
- *A World Without Email* (2021) — reimagining knowledge work
- *Slow Productivity* (2024) — the philosophy of doing less but better
- *So Good They Can't Ignore You* (2012) — why skills trump passion

---

## Core Ideas

- Deep work is rare and valuable
- Digital minimalism is pro-intention, not anti-technology
- Solitude is about freedom from other minds, not being alone
- Productivity is about depth, not speed
- The key to a good life is not doing more but doing what matters

---

## Background

- PhD from MIT in computer science
- Writes one of the most popular productivity blogs (calnewport.com)
- Does not have social media accounts
- His ideas have influenced millions to rethink their relationship with technology
