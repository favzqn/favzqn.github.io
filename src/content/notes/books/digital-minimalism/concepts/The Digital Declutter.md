# The Digital Declutter

The Digital Declutter is a 30-day reset where you remove all optional digital technologies from your life, then reintroduce only the ones that serve your values.

---

## The Process

### Step 1: The 30-Day Reset (Days 1–30)
- Remove all optional technology from your life
- "Optional" = anything you don't absolutely need for work or basic communication
- Use this time to rediscover analog activities
- Don't replace digital time with more screen time

### Step 2: Rediscover (During the 30 days)
- What do you enjoy doing without screens?
- What activities make you lose track of time?
- What would you do with 4 extra hours a day?

### Step 3: Reintroduce Selectively (Day 31+)
- For each technology, ask: "Does this serve my values?"
- If yes, decide how and when to use it
- Create strict usage rules
- If no, don't reintroduce it

---

## Why It Works

- A clean break is easier than gradual reduction
- It reveals how much time screens actually consume
- It helps you discover what you actually value
- It creates space for deeper activities
- It resets your attention span
