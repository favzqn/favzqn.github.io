# Solitude

Newport redefines solitude: it's not about being alone. It's about being free from the input of other minds. You can be alone and not have solitude (scrolling your phone), or be in a crowd and have solitude (not thinking about what others think).

---

## Key Ideas

- "Solitude is not about being alone — it is about being free from other minds"
- The problem is not being alone — it's the constant input of others' opinions
- Your phone eliminates solitude even when you're physically alone
- Solitude is where you process thoughts, develop ideas, and hear your inner voice
- Without solitude, you react instead of think

---

## Why Solitude Matters

- It allows deep reflection and self-discovery
- It is essential for creativity and problem-solving
- It strengthens your sense of self
- It reduces anxiety and mental clutter
- It is the foundation of meaningful work and relationships

---

## How to Practice

- **Leave your phone behind** — walk, hike, sit without it
- **Don't carry earbuds everywhere** — let your mind wander
- **Schedule alone time** — protect it like a meeting
- **Walk without audio** — think instead of consume
- **Start small** — 15 minutes of silence a day
