# The Attention Economy

Tech companies make money by capturing your attention and selling it to advertisers. Every feature is designed to be addictive. Understanding this is the first step to resisting it.

---

## Key Ideas

- "If the product is free, you are the product"
- Tech companies employ thousands of people whose only job is to make you click
- Features like infinite scroll, notifications, and autoplay are engineered for addiction
- The business model requires maximizing engagement, not user wellbeing
- "The attention economy is a zero-sum game — your attention is their revenue"

---

## How They Do It

- **Variable rewards** — unpredictable notifications trigger dopamine
- **Social validation** — likes and comments exploit the need for approval
- **Infinite scroll** — no natural stopping point
- **Autoplay** — removes the need to choose
- **Personalization** — shows you what keeps you engaged longest
