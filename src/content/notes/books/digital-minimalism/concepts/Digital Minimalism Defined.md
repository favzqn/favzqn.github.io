# Digital Minimalism Defined

Digital minimalism is a philosophy of technology use in which you focus your online time on a small number of carefully selected activities that support what you value, and then happily miss out on everything else.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Digital minimalism is not anti-technology — it is pro-intention"
- It's not about rejecting technology — it's about choosing it deliberately
- The key question: "Is this technology serving my values?"
- If it doesn't serve your values, it doesn't serve you
- "Clarity about what matters provides clarity about what does not"

---

## The Principles

1. **Clutter is costly** — every tool takes something from you
2. **Optimization is important** — don't just accept defaults, customize
3. **Intentionality is satisfying** — making deliberate choices feels good
