# Reclaim Leisure

Newport argues that we've replaced real leisure — craft, sport, community, conversation — with passive screen consumption. Reclaiming leisure means returning to activities that produce genuine satisfaction.

---

## Key Ideas

- True leisure is active, not passive
- It requires skill, effort, and engagement
- It often involves other people
- It produces lasting satisfaction, not momentary pleasure
- "Leisure is not something that you consume — it is something you create"

---

## The Analog Leisure Menu

- **Crafts**: woodworking, cooking, knitting, painting
- **Sports**: running, swimming, climbing, team sports
- **Community**: volunteering, clubs, classes, groups
- **Conversation**: dinner parties, walking talks, phone calls
- **Reading**: books, not articles; depth, not breadth
- **Walking**: without headphones, just thinking
