# Don't Click Like

Social media replaces real conversation with shallow gestures. Newport argues that true connection comes from conversation, not from clicking "like."

---

## Key Ideas

- "Conversation" means actual human-to-human interaction
- Social media gives the illusion of connection without the substance
- "Liking" a post is not communication — it's a signal
- Real relationships require attention, time, and vulnerability
- "The cost of a thing is the amount of what I will call life which is required to be exchanged for it"

---

## The Replacement

- Instead of commenting on a friend's post, call them
- Instead of sharing an article, discuss it over coffee
- Instead of following someone's life online, visit them
- Instead of texting, meet in person
- "Quality conversation requires mutual investment"
