# Patience and Slowness

Rilke counsels patience — great things take time, and the slow process of becoming is not a delay but the work itself.

---

## Key Ideas

- "All feelings tend, as far as they are developing, to complete themselves in silence"
- "Be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart"
- Don't rush the process — the seed needs time to become the tree
- "Nothing is as far off as what is close to your heart"
- The best things happen slowly, almost invisibly

---

## The Paradox of Slowness

- The faster you go, the further you are from the goal
- The slower you go, the closer you are to the center
- Patience is not waiting — it is being present to the process
- Slowness is not laziness — it is depth
- "Perhaps all the dragons in our lives are princesses who are only waiting to see us act"

---

## How to Practice

- **Don't count the days** — make the days count
- **Trust the process** — the answer is coming, even when you can't see it
- **Work quietly** — don't announce your progress
- **Let things ripen** — don't force the fruit before it's ready
- **Be gentle with yourself** — growth is slow, and that's okay
