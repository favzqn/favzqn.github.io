# Art as Inner Necessity

Rilke teaches that art is not a career or a hobby — it is an inner necessity. You create because you must, not because you want fame or approval.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Ask yourself in the most silent hour of your night: must I write?"
- If you can live without writing, don't write
- Art is the expression of the inner life, not a performance for others
- "Dive into your feelings and explore every thought and image"
- The artist creates because the work demands it — not for external reasons

---

## The Test

- "Must I write?" — if the answer is yes, you are an artist
- Don't ask "should I write" — the question is not moral but existential
- If you can do anything else, do it — art is not a profession
- The artist is not special — they are simply compelled
- "Art is the expression of the inner life"

---

## The Danger of Ambition

- Ambition seeks external validation; art seeks inner truth
- Don't write to be published — write because you must
- Don't create for an audience — create for the work itself
- The moment you create for approval, the inner voice goes silent
- "Your art is not the world's business"
