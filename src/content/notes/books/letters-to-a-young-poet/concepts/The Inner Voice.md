# The Inner Voice

Rilke's central teaching is to trust the inner voice — the quiet knowing that arises from deep within, before the mind starts analyzing and doubting.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Go into yourself and see how deep the place is from which your life arises"
- Don't ask whether you should write — if you must write, you will
- The inner voice speaks in feelings, not words
- External opinions are noise; the inner voice is signal
- "You must change your life" — the inner voice always calls you deeper

---

## How to Listen

- **Be still** — silence the external noise
- **Go inward** — not to the mind, but to the chest, the gut, the breath
- **Don't interpret** — feel the answer before translating it into words
- **Be patient** — the inner voice speaks slowly, in feelings
- **Act on it** — knowing without acting deadens the voice

---

## The Danger of Advice

- Don't let others tell you what to create
- "Find out the reason that commands you to write"
- If you can live without writing, don't write
- The inner voice is the only authority on your life
- "No one can advise or help you — no one"
