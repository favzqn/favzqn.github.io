# Letters to a Young Poet

*Letters to a Young Poet* by Rainer Maria Rilke (1929). Ten letters written by Rilke to Franz Xaver Kappus, a 19-year-old military student, between 1903 and 1908. A meditation on art, solitude, creativity, and the inner life.

---

## Structure

The ten letters cover:

- [[The Inner Voice]] — trust your own feelings, not external opinions
- [[Solitude and Inner Growth]] — solitude is not loneliness but fertile ground
- [[Living the Questions]] — don't seek answers; live the questions
- [[Art as Inner Necessity]] — create because you must, not for approval
- [[Patience and Slowness]] — great things take time
- Love and Solitude — love and solitude are not opposites
- The Body and Senses — the body is not the enemy of the spirit
- [[Beauty as Conversion]] — beauty is the beginning of transformation
- Work and Silence — work quietly, without explaining
- You Must Change Your Life — the final, transformative imperative
