# Franz Xaver Kappus

Austrian military student (1883–1976) who wrote to Rilke seeking advice about poetry and life. Rilke's ten replies became *Letters to a Young Poet*.

---

## Background

- Studied at the Military Academy of Wiener Neustadt
- Wrote to Rilke in 1903, seeking advice about his poetry
- Rilke was only 27 at the time, already a published poet
- Kappus continued writing but never achieved fame as a poet
- Later became a journalist and editor
- Published the letters in 1929, three years after Rilke's death

---

## The Letters

- Kappus asked practical questions: Should I publish? Am I good enough?
- Rilke gave philosophical answers: Trust yourself, go inward, be patient
- The letters are not about poetry — they are about life
- Kappus was disappointed by the letters at first — he wanted technical advice
- He later recognized their profound wisdom
