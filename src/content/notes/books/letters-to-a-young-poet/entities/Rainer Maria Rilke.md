# Rainer Maria Rilke

Bohemian-Austrian poet (1875–1926). One of the most important German-language poets. Author of *Duino Elegies*, *Sonnets to Orpheus*, and *Letters to a Young Poet*.

---

## Key Works

- *Duino Elegies* (1923) — ten long poems on existence, mortality, and the divine
- *Sonnets to Orpheus* (1923) — fifty-five sonnets on art, love, and transformation
- *Letters to a Young Poet* (1929) — ten letters to Franz Xaver Kappus
- *The Notebooks of Malte Laurids Brigge* (1910) — a novel about a young poet in Paris

---

## Core Ideas

- Solitude is the precondition for art and inner growth
- The inner voice is the only authority on your life
- Beauty is the beginning of transformation
- "You must change your life"
- Art is an inner necessity, not a profession
- Patience and slowness are the ways of depth

---

## Legacy

- *Letters to a Young Poet* is one of the most quoted books on creativity
- Influenced writers, artists, and thinkers for a century
- His poetry is known for its depth, beauty, and spiritual insight
- He lived a life of deliberate solitude and artistic devotion
