# Sleep and Mental Health

Sleep deprivation doesn't just make you tired — it causes psychiatric disorders. Walker argues that many mental health conditions are, in part, sleep disorders.

---

## Key Ideas

- After just one night of only 4–5 hours of sleep, anxiety levels rise by 60%
- Sleep deprivation causes the amygdala to be 60% more reactive
- People with insomnia are 10x more likely to develop depression
- Sleep loss amplifies emotional reactivity — everything feels worse
- "There is no major psychiatric condition in which sleep is not disrupted"

---

## The Bidirectional Problem

- Bad sleep → worse mental health
- Worse mental health → bad sleep
- The cycle reinforces itself
- Treating sleep often improves mental health dramatically
- CBT-I (cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia) can treat both
