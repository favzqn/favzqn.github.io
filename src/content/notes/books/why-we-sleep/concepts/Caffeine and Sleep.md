# Caffeine and Sleep

Caffeine is the most widely used psychoactive drug in the world, and it devastates sleep quality even when you don't feel it.

---

## Key Ideas

- Caffeine has a half-life of 5–7 hours (half still in you after 6 hours)
- A coffee at 3 PM means half the caffeine is still in you at 9 PM
- Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors — you can't feel sleepiness
- Even if you fall asleep, caffeine reduces deep sleep by 15–20%
- "Caffeine does not give you energy — it blocks the signal that you need sleep"

---

## The Caffeine-Sleep Death Spiral

1. You sleep poorly → tired the next day
2. You drink caffeine to compensate → sleep quality drops
3. You sleep even worse → more caffeine the next day
4. Repeat until chronic sleep deprivation
