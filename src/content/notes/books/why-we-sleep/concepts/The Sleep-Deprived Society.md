# The Sleep-Deprived Society

Modern society is in the midst of a catastrophic sleep deprivation crisis. We sleep less than any generation in history, with devastating consequences.

---

## Key Ideas

- One-third of adults don't get enough sleep regularly
- Sleep deprivation is now classified as a public health epidemic by the CDC
- The average sleep has decreased from 9 hours to under 7 hours in 100 years
- "Human beings are the only species that deliberately deprive themselves of sleep for no apparent gain"
- The shorter your sleep, the shorter your life

---

## Why It Happens

- **Industrialization** — artificial light and 24-hour schedules
- **Technology** — screens, notifications, always-on culture
- **Cultural glorification** — "I'll sleep when I'm dead" mentality
- **Economic pressure** — working more, sleeping less
- **Misinformation** — people don't know how bad sleep deprivation is
