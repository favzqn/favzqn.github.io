# Dreaming as Therapy

Walker's "overnight therapy" hypothesis: REM sleep takes the emotional edge off difficult experiences, allowing you to remember the event without the pain.

---

## Key Ideas

- During REM sleep, the brain processes emotional experiences
- The stress chemistry (norepinephrine) is shut off during REM
- This allows you to re-process the memory without the emotional charge
- "REM sleep is overnight therapy — it takes the sharp emotional edges off experiences"
- PTSD sufferers have disrupted REM — they can't process the trauma

---

## The Process

1. You experience something stressful during the day
2. During REM sleep, the brain replays the experience
3. But norepinephrine is shut off — no stress chemistry
4. The memory is reprocessed without the emotional charge
5. You wake up able to think about it more objectively
