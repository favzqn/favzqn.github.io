# REM vs NREM

Sleep has two main types that alternate throughout the night, each serving different functions. Both are essential.

---

## NREM Sleep (Non-Rapid Eye Movement)

- **Light sleep** (stages 1–2) — transition from wakefulness
- **Deep sleep** (stage 3, slow-wave sleep) — physical restoration, immune repair, growth
- Dominant in the first half of the night
- "The physical restoration shift"

## REM Sleep (Rapid Eye Movement)

- **Dream sleep** — vivid dreaming, paralysis of voluntary muscles
- Emotional processing, memory consolidation, creativity
- Dominant in the second half of the night
- "The mental restoration shift"

---

## Why Both Matter

- Missing deep sleep = physical illness, weakened immunity
- Missing REM sleep = emotional instability, poor memory
- You need both in the right proportions
- Cutting sleep short by 2 hours eliminates most REM sleep
- Alcohol blocks REM; caffeine blocks deep sleep
