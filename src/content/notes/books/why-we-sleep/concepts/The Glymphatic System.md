# The Glymphatic System

The glymphatic system is the brain's waste-clearance system. It only activates during deep sleep, flushing out toxic proteins that accumulate during waking hours.

---

## Key Ideas

- The brain produces metabolic waste throughout the day
- During deep sleep, brain cells shrink by 60%, opening channels for fluid to flow
- Cerebrospinal fluid washes through the brain, removing waste
- One of the key toxins removed is beta-amyloid — the protein linked to Alzheimer's
- Sleep deprivation allows toxins to build up, increasing Alzheimer's risk

---

## Why It Matters

- Chronic sleep deprivation may directly cause Alzheimer's disease
- Even one night of poor sleep increases beta-amyloid levels
- The glymphatic system only works during deep sleep — not during naps
- "The best thing you can do to prevent Alzheimer's is to sleep"
