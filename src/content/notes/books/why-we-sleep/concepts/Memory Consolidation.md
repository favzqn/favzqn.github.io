# Memory Consolidation

Sleep is the brain's filing system — it takes new experiences and moves them from short-term to long-term memory. Without sleep, learning is impossible.

---

## How It Works

- During the day, new memories are fragile and temporary
- During sleep, the brain replays the day's experiences
- Deep sleep consolidates factual and procedural memories
- REM sleep consolidates emotional and creative memories
- The hippocampus "replays" experiences to the neocortex for storage

---

## The Science

- Sleep after learning improves recall by 20–40%
- A nap after learning significantly boosts memory
- Pulling an all-nighter before an exam is the worst strategy
- Sleep deprivation reduces the ability to form new memories by 40%
- "Sleep is the greatest legal performance-enhancing drug that most people are neglecting"
