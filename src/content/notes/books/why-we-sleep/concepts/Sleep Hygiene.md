# Sleep Hygiene

Practical, evidence-based habits that dramatically improve sleep quality. Walker emphasizes these are not optional — they are essential.

---

## The Big Seven

1. **Consistent schedule** — same bedtime and wake time, even weekends
2. **Cool bedroom** — 65°F/18.5°C is optimal
3. **Dark room** — blackout curtains, no screens
4. **No caffeine after noon** — 5–7 hour half-life
5. **No alcohol before bed** — destroys REM sleep
6. **Exercise daily** — but not within 2–3 hours of bed
7. **No screens 60 minutes before bed** — blue light suppresses melatonin

---

## Additional Tips

- **Get morning sunlight** — resets your circadian clock
- **Don't lie awake in bed** — get up after 20 minutes, do something boring
- **Use the bed only for sleep** — train your brain to associate bed with sleep
- **Avoid naps after 3 PM** — they steal sleep pressure
- **Wind-down routine** — reading, stretching, meditation
