# Matthew Walker

British-born neuroscientist and professor at the University of California, Berkeley. Director of the Center for Human Sleep Science. Author of *Why We Sleep*.

---

## Key Contributions

- Pioneered research on the link between sleep and Alzheimer's
- Discovered the glymphatic system's role in brain cleaning
- Demonstrated that REM sleep is "overnight therapy"
- Showed that sleep deprivation amplifies emotional reactivity
- Made sleep science accessible to the general public

---

## Core Ideas

- Sleep is the single most important thing for health
- The shorter your sleep, the shorter your life
- "There is not one major organ in the body that isn't optimized by sleep"
- Caffeine is the most misunderstood drug in the world
- Dreaming is overnight therapy — processing emotional memories

---

## Background

- PhD in Neurophysiology from Newcastle University
- Professor at Harvard Medical School before Berkeley
- Founded the Center for Human Sleep Science
- Appears frequently on podcasts and in media
- His book has been translated into over 30 languages
