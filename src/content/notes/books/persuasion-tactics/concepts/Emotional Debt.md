# Emotional Debt

"Emotional debt and the psychological phenomenon of reciprocity is when you compel someone to act for or with you because they feel like they owe you."

---

## How It Differs from Reciprocation

Reciprocation (Cialdini) is a mutual exchange — I give, you give back. Emotional debt is heavier — it's a feeling of crushing guilt. You feel you've taken advantage of someone and want to rectify that feeling.

The key difference: emotional debt can be created by pointing out disparities or inequities, not just by giving gifts.

---

## Creating Emotional Debt

1. **Sacrifice your time** — help someone when you're busy, and make them aware of the sacrifice
2. **Imply the cost** — "I took time out of my schedule to help you with this..."
3. **Point out what they've received** — make them aware of benefits they've already enjoyed
4. **Reference what others haven't done** — "Most people wouldn't have done this for you"
