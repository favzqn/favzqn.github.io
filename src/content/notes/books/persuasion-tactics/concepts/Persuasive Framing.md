# Persuasive Framing

"If you can marginalize the cons and maximize the pros, and vice versa for the opposing option, you can create a frame where there is only one plausible option."

---

## The Principle

Framing is about how you characterize the options in front of someone. You don't change the facts — you change which facts are salient.

---

## The Two Directions

**For your option**: Maximize pros, minimize cons. Make benefits vivid and concrete. Make costs vague and abstract.

**Against the opposing option**: Maximize cons, minimize pros. Make costs vivid and concrete. Make benefits vague and abstract.

---

## The False Dichotomy

King identifies Hitler's primary technique as false dichotomy — reducing complex choices to binary options where one leads to catastrophe. "We must do X, or else Y happens."

This is framing at its most extreme: you define the options so that only one is acceptable.
