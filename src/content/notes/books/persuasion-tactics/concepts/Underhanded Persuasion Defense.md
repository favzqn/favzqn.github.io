# Underhanded Persuasion Defense

"When you can understand these tactics, and subsequently be able to see them in action against you, you will immediately be able to call them out."

---

## The Four Underhanded Tactics

### 1. Double Bind
Present two choices, both detrimental. "You're not being logical" — either agree and cede control, or remain "illogical."

**Defense**: Seek third options. Both options are assumptions — poke holes in the options themselves.

### 2. Praeteritio
Mention something negative while claiming you won't mention it. "I could mention when you stole from the cashier, but I won't do that."

**Defense**: Call it out directly. "You just mentioned it while saying you wouldn't."

### 3. Paralipsis
Assert that you'll omit something, thereby drawing attention to it. "Let's focus on the benefits and not the cost."

**Defense**: Same as praeteritio — expose the transparent ploy.

### 4. Straw Man
Distort someone's argument into something easily attacked. "So you're saying the health of our youth has a price?"

**Defense**: Know your actual argument and refuse to let it be distorted. "That's not what I said. Here's what I actually said."

---

## The Meta-Defense

King's ultimate defense against underhanded persuasion: **awareness**. Once you can name the tactic being used against you, it loses its power. "Don't let them hide behind obliviousness or plausible deniability. They know what they are doing."
