# Communication Styles

Four distinct communication styles. When you pinpoint which style someone uses, you can literally speak their language and be heard more easily.

---

## The Four Styles

### 1. Analytical
- Data-driven, fact-oriented
- Wants proof, numbers, evidence
- Responds to: statistics, case studies, detailed analysis
- Frustrated by: vagueness, emotion-only arguments

### 2. Intuitive
- Big-picture thinker
- Wants the bottom line, not the details
- Responds to: vision, goals, summaries
- Frustrated by: excessive detail, slow pacing

### 3. Personal
- Relationship-focused
- Wants to know how decisions affect people
- Responds to: stories, empathy, connection
- Frustrated by: cold logic, impersonal data

### 4. Functional
- Process-oriented
- Wants step-by-step plans and timelines
- Responds to: organization, structure, methodology
- Frustrated by: ambiguity, lack of clear next steps

---

## The Key Insight

Most people try to persuade using THEIR communication style. The persuader's skill is matching the TARGET's style. An analytical person won't be moved by personal stories. A personal person won't be moved by spreadsheets.
