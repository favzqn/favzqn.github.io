# Maslow's Hierarchy Applied

Understanding where people fall on Maslow's hierarchy lets you frame your persuasive argument to speak to their most pressing needs.

---

## The Hierarchy (Bottom to Top)

1. **Physiological** — food, water, shelter, sleep
2. **Safety** — security, stability, protection
3. **Belonging** — love, friendship, community
4. **Esteem** — respect, recognition, status
5. **Self-Actualization** — reaching full potential, meaning, purpose

---

## The Application

- A person struggling with safety needs won't be persuaded by self-actualization arguments
- A person at the esteem level won't respond to belonging appeals
- Match your argument to where they ARE, not where you want them to be
