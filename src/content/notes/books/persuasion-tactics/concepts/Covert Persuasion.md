# Covert Persuasion

"The best type of persuasion is when people will do things without prompting."

---

## The Spectrum

King organizes persuasion on a spectrum from overt to covert:

**Overt**: Direct arguments, debates, presentations. Both parties know persuasion is happening. "I think you should do X because..."

**Covert**: Invisible influence. The target doesn't realize they're being persuaded. They believe the idea is their own. This is more effective because it bypasses resistance.

---

## The Covert Techniques

1. **Embrace the Status Quo** — be happy about NOT having what you want them to buy, while dropping benefits
2. **Accept the Status Quo** — bemoan the barriers to change, making the current situation unbearable
3. **Talk Around the Idea** — discuss adjacent topics until the target arrives at your conclusion
4. **Play Dumb** — ask innocent questions that let the other person brainstorm your solution

---

## Why It Works

"When someone feels like they have generated an idea by themselves, they have no barriers to acting upon it. In fact, their resolve is typically strengthened because they want to confirm and demonstrate how clever they can be."

People resist being persuaded because it threatens their autonomy. Covert persuasion removes the threat by making the target feel in control.
