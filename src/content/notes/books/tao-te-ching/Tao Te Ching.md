# Tao Te Ching

*Tao Te Ching* by Lao Tzu, translated by Ursula K. Le Guin (2009). A poet's rendering of the foundational text of Taoism.

---

## Structure

The book consists of 82 short chapters (poems), organized around core themes:

- [[Wu Wei]] — non-action, effortless action, the way of water
- [[The Uncarved Block]] — simplicity, naturalness, potential
- [[The Tao]] — the way, the nameless, the source
- [[Softness Overcomes Hardness]] — water, yielding, flexibility
- [[The Sage]] — the ideal leader, empty vessel, leads by following
- [[Emptiness and Fullness]] — the utility of emptiness, the valley
- [[Non-Contention]] — winning without fighting, stillness
- Knowing without knowing, mystery

---

## Key Concepts

- [[Wu Wei]] — action through non-action
- [[The Uncarved Block]] — simplicity and original nature
- [[The Tao]] — the way that cannot be named
- [[Softness Overcomes Hardness]] — water as metaphor for power
- [[Emptiness and Fullness]] — the usefulness of the empty space
- [[Non-Contention]] — winning without fighting
- [[The Sage]] — the ideal person who leads by following
