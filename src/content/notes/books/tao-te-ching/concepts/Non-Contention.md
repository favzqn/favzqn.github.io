# Non-Contention

The Tao Te Ching teaches that the greatest victory is the one not fought. Contention arises from ego; non-contention arises from alignment with the Tao.

---

## Key Ideas

- "The best fighter is never angry"
- "A good soldier does not fight"
- "Do not compete with anyone; no one can compete with you"
- "Winning creates enemies; winning without fighting creates friends"
- "The Way is to combat nothing, to use what the people give you, and to follow the Way of Heaven"

---

## The Art of Not Fighting

- Argue your point and lose the relationship
- Hold your tongue and keep the peace
- The one who yields always wins
- Victory through force breeds resentment
- Victory through yielding breeds respect

---

## How to Practice

- **Don't argue to win** — listen to understand
- **Yield when challenged** — step back, create space
- **Don't compete** — run your own race
- **Avoid provocation** — don't take the bait
- **Be still** — stillness defuses conflict
