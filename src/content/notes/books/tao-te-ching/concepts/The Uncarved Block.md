# The Uncarved Block

The uncarved block (pu) represents the original, natural state of things — before civilization, education, and desire carve away its wholeness.

---

## Definition

A metaphor for the state of pure potential and simplicity. Before the block is carved, it contains all possible forms. Once carved, it becomes one thing and loses the rest.

---

## Key Ideas

- Simplicity is not ignorance — it is wholeness
- Civilization carves the block into specific forms, losing the rest
- Return to the uncarved block: see things as they are, not as categories
- Names and labels cut reality into pieces
- "The whole world can be held in the belly of a baby"

---

## How to Practice

- **Simplify your life** — reduce possessions, commitments, desires
- **See without labeling** — experience before categorizing
- **Embrace uncertainty** — the uncarved block is full of potential
- **Return to beginner's mind** — forget what you know, see freshly
- **Value what is ordinary** — the plain, unadorned, natural
