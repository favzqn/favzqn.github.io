# The Sage

The sage is the ideal person in Taoism — one who lives in harmony with the Tao, leads without dominating, and acts without forcing.

---

## Qualities of the Sage

- **Empty** — like a valley, receptive to all
- **Still** — like a mirror, reflecting without judgment
- **Humble** — puts others first, yet others elevate them
- **Effortless** — acts without striving
- **Content** — needs nothing, yet provides for all
- **Invisible** — works behind the scenes
- **Paradoxical** — is strong by being weak, is first by being last

---

## The Sage as Leader

- Leads by following the people's own direction
- Does not impose their will
- The people say "we did it ourselves"
- Rules lightly, like the wind — barely noticed, yet effective
- "When the best leader's work is done, the people say: we did it ourselves"

---

## The Sage's Relationship to Knowledge

- Knows without learning; acts without teaching
- Does not hoard knowledge — gives it away
- Knows the limits of knowledge — comfortable with mystery
- "The more you know, the less you understand"
