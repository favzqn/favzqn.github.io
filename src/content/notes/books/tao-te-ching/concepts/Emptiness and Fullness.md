# Emptiness and Fullness

The useful part of a bowl is its emptiness. The useful part of a room is the space between the walls. Emptiness is not absence — it is the precondition for use.

---

## Key Ideas

- "We shape clay into a pot, but it is the emptiness inside that holds whatever we want"
- "We hammer wood for a house, but it is the inner space that makes it livable"
- "We work with being, but non-being is what we use"
- Fullness is actually emptiness — a full schedule has no room for life
- Emptiness is actually fullness — an empty mind is full of possibility

---

## The Paradox

- The more you fill, the less room you have
- The more you empty, the more you can receive
- A cup full of tea can hold no more tea
- A cup empty of tea can hold anything

---

## How to Practice

- **Leave space** in your schedule, your plans, your relationships
- **Empty your mind** before filling it
- **Don't over-explain** — silence is more powerful than words
- **Simplify** — remove until only the essential remains
- **Be available** — emptiness is availability
