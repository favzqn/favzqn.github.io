# The Tao

The Tao is "the Way" — the fundamental, nameless source of all things. It cannot be spoken of, yet it is the origin of everything.

---

## Definition

The Tao is the underlying order of the universe — not a god, not a force, but the natural way things are. It is both the source of all things and the pattern they follow.

---

## Key Ideas

- "The Way that can be told is not the eternal Way"
- "The name that can be named is not the eternal name"
- The Tao is formless, yet gives form to everything
- It is always present, yet invisible
- The Tao does nothing, yet nothing is left undone
- It is the void from which all things arise and to which they return

---

## The Paradox

- The Tao cannot be defined — definitions limit it
- The Tao cannot be named — names separate it from itself
- Yet it is the most real thing there is
- It is both nothing and everything
- To understand it, stop trying to understand it
