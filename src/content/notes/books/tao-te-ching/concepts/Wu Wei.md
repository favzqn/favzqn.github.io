# Wu Wei

Wu wei means "non-action" or "effortless action." It is not passivity or laziness — it is acting in harmony with the natural flow of things, without forcing or struggling.

---

## Definition

Action that does not involve struggle or friction. It is the art of going with the grain of reality rather than against it. Water flows downhill without effort — that is wu wei.

---

## Key Ideas

- The more you try, the less you achieve
- Force creates resistance; yielding creates flow
- The best leader acts without claiming credit
- Do your work, then step back — that is the Tao
- "The Way is always still, and never acts, yet everything is done"

---

## How to Practice

- **Act when it's time to act**, not before or after
- **Stop before you're finished** — leave room for completion
- **Don't force outcomes** — plant seeds and let them grow
- **Reduce effort** — the harder you try, the worse it gets
- **Follow natural rhythms** — sleep when tired, eat when hungry, work when inspired
