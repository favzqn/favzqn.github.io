# Ursula K. Le Guin

American novelist, poet, and translator (1929–2018). Known for science fiction and fantasy (*The Left Hand of Darkness*, *The Dispossessed*, *Earthsea*). Her translation of the *Tao Te Ching* (2009) is widely praised for its poetic clarity.

---

## Key Contributions

- Brought literary depth and feminist perspective to science fiction
- Explored Taoist themes throughout her fiction (*The Dispossessed* is essentially a Taoist novel)
- Her *Tao Te Ching* translation emphasizes the poetic and philosophical over the religious
- Added commentary and personal reflections to each chapter
- Made the text accessible to Western readers without losing its depth

---

## Taoist Influence

- Her parents were anthropologists — she understood cultural relativism
- Her fiction explores balance, duality, and the Tao
- *The Dispossessed* is subtitled "An Ambiguous Utopia" — a Taoist paradox
- She lived on the Oregon coast — a landscape of water and stone, softness and hardness
