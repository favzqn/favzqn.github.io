# Lao Tzu

Legendary Chinese philosopher and author of the *Tao Te Ching*, the foundational text of Taoism. The name "Lao Tzu" means "Old Master." His historical existence is debated — he may be a composite of several thinkers.

---

## Key Ideas

- The Tao (the Way) is the source and pattern of all things
- Wu wei (non-action) is the way to live in harmony with reality
- Simplicity, humility, and yielding are true strengths
- The sage leads by following, wins without fighting
- Names and labels limit reality — the Tao cannot be named

---

## Legacy

- The *Tao Te Ching* is one of the most translated books in history (second only to the Bible)
- Founded Taoism, one of the great philosophical/religious traditions
- Influenced Buddhism, Confucianism, Chinese medicine, martial arts, and Western philosophy
- His work has influenced thinkers from Heidegger to the Beats to modern minimalism
