# Sapiens

*Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind* by Yuval Noah Harari (2015). A sweeping narrative of human history from the emergence of Homo sapiens in Africa to the present.

---

## Structure

### Part I: The Cognitive Revolution
- [[Cognitive Revolution]] — language and fiction made Homo sapiens dominant
- [[The Tree of Knowledge]] — language enabled new forms of cooperation
- A Day in the Life of Adam and Eve — the hunter-gatherer lifestyle

### Part II: The Agricultural Revolution
- [[The Agricultural Revolution]] — history's biggest fraud
- [[Imagined Orders]] — imagined orders and social hierarchies
- The Memory of Blood — writing, bureaucracy, and the decline of memory

### Part III: The Unification of Humankind
- [[The Dream of Money]] — universal exchange systems
- The Imperial Vision — empires as unifying forces
- The Law of Religion — religions as tools of social cohesion

### Part IV: The Scientific Revolution
- [[The Discovery of Ignorance]] — science as the admission of not knowing
- [[The Capitalist Creed]] — credit, growth, and the engine of modernity
- [[The End of Homo Sapiens]] — transhumanism and the future
