# The Agricultural Revolution

Harari calls the Agricultural Revolution "history's biggest fraud." Farming made humans work harder, eat worse, and suffer more disease — yet it was irreversible.

---

## The Trap

- Farming produced more food but more people, not more leisure
- Farmers worked longer hours than hunter-gatherers
- Diet became less varied — more grain, less meat and vegetables
- Disease spread through dense settlements and domesticated animals
- Social hierarchies and inequality emerged

---

## Why It Happened

- Each generation made small improvements, not realizing the trap
- More food = more children = need more food = more farming
- The population grew faster than the quality of life improved
- "We did not domesticate wheat. It domesticated us."
