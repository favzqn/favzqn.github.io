# The Discovery of Ignorance

The Scientific Revolution began not with a discovery but with an admission: "I do not know." Before the 16th century, most cultures believed all important knowledge was already known.

---

## Key Ideas

- Before science, the dominant belief was that ancient texts held all truth
- The admission of ignorance was revolutionary — it made new discovery possible
- Science, empire, and capitalism formed a feedback loop
- Research was funded by empires seeking competitive advantage
- The cycle: observation → hypothesis → experiment → new technology → more power

---

## The Three Pillars

1. **Admission of ignorance** — we don't know everything
2. **Centrality of observation and mathematics** — evidence over authority
3. **Acquisition of new powers** — knowledge leads to technology
