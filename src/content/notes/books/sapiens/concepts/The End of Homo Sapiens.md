# The End of Homo Sapiens

Harari's final provocation: Homo sapiens may be the last generation of the old evolution and the first to design its own successor. Natural selection is being replaced by intelligent design.

---

## Key Ideas

- For 4 billion years, evolution operated through natural selection
- Now, humans are beginning to direct their own evolution
- Three paths of change: biological engineering, cyborg engineering, inorganic life
- The question is not "what do we want to become?" but "what do we want to want?"
- "Is there anything more dangerous than dissatisfied and irresponsible gods who don't know what they want?"

---

## The Three Paths

1. **Biological engineering** — genetic modification, selective breeding
2. **Cyborg engineering** — combining organic and inorganic parts
3. **Inorganic life** — artificial intelligence and consciousness
