# The Dream of Money

Money is the most universal and efficient system of mutual trust ever devised. It works because everyone believes in it — a shared fiction of unprecedented power.

---

## Key Ideas

- Money is not a material reality — it is a psychological construct
- It works because everyone believes in it
- Any two people who trust the same imaginary system can cooperate
- Money replaced barter, which required a "double coincidence of wants"
- The value of money is purely determined by collective imagination

---

## The Evolution of Money

1. **Barter** — direct exchange, limited and inefficient
2. **Shell money** — early symbolic exchange
3. **Coinage** — standardized, portable value
4. **Paper money** — backed by trust, not gold
5. **Digital money** — purely abstract, exist only as numbers
