# Imagined Orders

Imagined orders are the shared myths that hold societies together — money, religion, laws, human rights, nations. They exist only because people believe they exist.

---

## Key Ideas

- "How do you cause people to believe in an imagined order? First, you never admit that the order is imagined"
- The Code of Hammurabi and the American Declaration of Independence are both imagined orders
- They are not lies — they are intersubjective realities (shared by many minds)
- Every large-scale human cooperation is based on an imagined order
- Money is the most universal and efficient imagined order ever created

---

## The Three Types of Imagined Order

1. **Legal** — laws, rights, and duties (constitutions, codes)
2. **Economic** — markets, currencies, and trade (capitalism, communism)
3. **Religious** — gods, spirits, and moral codes (Christianity, Islam, Buddhism)

---

## Why They Work

- They enable millions of strangers to cooperate
- They are embedded in the material world (architecture, technology)
- They shape our desires and expectations
- They are self-reinforcing — the more people believe, the more real they become
