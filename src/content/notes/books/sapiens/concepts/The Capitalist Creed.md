# The Capitalist Creed

Capitalism is not just an economic system — it is a religion of growth. Its core belief: credit and investment produce more wealth, which produces more credit, in an ever-expanding cycle.

---

## Key Ideas

- Before capitalism, the total wealth of the world was seen as fixed
- The modern economy is based on the assumption of future growth
- Credit makes growth possible — you borrow against future wealth
- "The discovery of ignorance created the credit economy"
- Banks and governments create money out of thin air — based on trust in the future

---

## The Cycle

1. I invest my money in a factory
2. I borrow more money from the bank
3. The factory produces goods and profit
4. I repay the bank with interest
5. The economy grows, credit expands, more investment

---

## The Dark Side

- Growth requires resources — environmental destruction
- Growth benefits some at the expense of others — inequality
- The system collapses if people stop believing in the future
- "Culture tends to argue that it forbids only that which is unnatural"
