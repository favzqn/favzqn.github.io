# Cognitive Revolution

The Cognitive Revolution (~70,000 years ago) was the turning point when Homo sapiens developed the ability to think in ways no other animal could — through language, fiction, and shared myths.

---

## Key Ideas

- Language allowed humans to cooperate in large flexible groups
- Fiction — the ability to discuss things that don't exist — was the key advantage
- Shared myths (religions, nations, money) enabled mass cooperation
- Other species can only communicate about immediate reality
- "You could never convince a monkey to give you a banana by promising him limitless bananas after death in monkey heaven"

---

## Why It Matters

- The Cognitive Revolution explains why Homo sapiens conquered the world
- It was not physical strength but cooperative ability
- Language enabled gossip, planning, and social bonding
- Fiction enabled law, religion, economics, and politics
- All human institutions are built on shared fictions
