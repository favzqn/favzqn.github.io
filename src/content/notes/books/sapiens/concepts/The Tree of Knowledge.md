# The Tree of Knowledge

Harari argues that the unique breakthrough of Homo sapiens was the ability to transmit information about things that do not exist — the "Tree of Knowledge" that separates humans from all other species.

---

## Key Ideas

- Most communication in nature is about immediate reality
- Only Homo sapiens can discuss things that exist only in the imagination
- This ability enabled gossip, planning, and mythology
- It created the "Tree of Knowledge" — a vast network of shared information
- From this tree grew every human institution: religion, law, commerce, art

---

## The Power of Language

- Language allows us to create and believe in shared fictions
- Shared fictions enable mass cooperation between strangers
- The agricultural revolution required new forms of shared mythology
- Writing extended the tree beyond the limits of memory
- The scientific revolution added a new branch: the admission of ignorance
