# Yuval Noah Harari

Israeli historian, philosopher, and author (born 1976). Professor of history at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Author of *Sapiens*, *Homo Deus*, and *21 Lessons for the 21st Century*.

---

## Key Ideas

- Human history is shaped by shared fictions — myths, religions, money, nations
- The Agricultural Revolution was "history's biggest fraud"
- Science, capitalism, and empire are intertwined
- Homo sapiens may be the last generation of natural evolution
- The greatest question: "what do we want to want?"

---

## Core Works

- *Sapiens* (2015) — the history of humankind
- *Homo Deus* (2017) — the future of humanity
- *21 Lessons for the 21st Century* (2018) — current challenges

---

## Background

- Born in Haifa, Israel
- Studied history at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem
- PhD from the University of Oxford
- Became a vegetarian after studying the treatment of animals
- Practices Vipassana meditation
