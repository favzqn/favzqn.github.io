# Flow

*Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience* by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi (1990). A groundbreaking psychology book that identifies the conditions for the optimal human experience — complete absorption in an activity.

---

## Structure

### Chapter 1–3: Introduction and Foundations
- Happiness is not something that happens — it is something you create
- The flow experience: complete absorption in an activity
- [[The Flow State]] — the optimal experience

### Chapter 4–6: Conditions of Flow
- [[Challenge-Skill Balance]] — the activity must match your abilities
- Clear Goals and Immediate Feedback — knowing what to do and how you're doing
- Concentration on the Task — total focus on the present moment
- Merging of Action and Awareness — you become the activity
- [[Loss of Self-Consciousness]] — the self disappears into the action
- Sense of Control — you feel in command of the situation
- Transformation of Time — time distorts in flow

### Chapter 7–9: Flow in Different Areas of Life
- Flow in the Body — sports, exercise, physical mastery
- Flow in the Mind — reading, thinking, learning
- Flow in Work — finding flow in your career
- Flow in Relationships — deep connection with others

### Chapter 10–12: The Inner Life
- [[Autotelic Self]] — the personality that finds flow in everyday life
- [[Enjoyment]] — the reward of flow
- The Complex Self — the self that grows through flow
