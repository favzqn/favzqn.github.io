# Enjoyment

Csikszentmihalyi distinguishes between pleasure and enjoyment. Pleasure is passive and requires no effort. Enjoyment requires active engagement and produces growth.

---

## Pleasure vs Enjoyment

- **Pleasure**: Eating a good meal, lying in the sun, watching TV
- **Enjoyment**: Playing a sport, writing a poem, solving a problem
- Pleasure is passive — it happens to you
- Enjoyment is active — you create it
- Pleasure doesn't produce growth; enjoyment does

---

## The Conditions of Enjoyment

1. **It must bevoluntary** — not forced or coerced
2. **It must be achievable** — within your skill level
3. **It must require concentration** — total focus
4. **It must have clear goals** — you know what to do
5. **It must provide feedback** — you know how you're doing
6. **It must require effort** — not too easy, not too hard
7. **It must give you a sense of control** — you feel in command
8. **It must not be self-conscious** — the ego disappears

---

## The Reward of Flow

- Enjoyment is the reward of flow
- It is not a means to an end — it is the end itself
- "Enjoyment appears at the boundary between boredom and anxiety"
- The best moments in life are moments of enjoyment
- "People who learn to control inner experience will be able to determine the quality of their lives"
