# Autotelic Self

The autotelic self is the personality that finds flow in everyday life. It is not a genetic trait but a learned way of being — finding intrinsic reward in activities regardless of external outcomes.

---

## The Word

- "Auto" = self, "telos" = goal
- An autotelic activity is one that is rewarding in itself
- The autotelic self does things because they are interesting, not for external rewards
- It is the opposite of the extrinsic self, which lives for approval, money, or status

---

## Characteristics

- **Curiosity** — finds everything interesting
- **Persistence** — doesn't give up when things get hard
- **Low self-centeredness** — focused on the activity, not the ego
- **Intrinsic motivation** — does it because it's rewarding
- **Present-moment awareness** — lives in the now
- **Flexibility** — adapts to challenges

---

## How to Develop It

- **Set intrinsic goals** — do things for the experience, not the outcome
- **Find challenge in everyday activities** — even mundane tasks can be engaging
- **Practice concentration** — train your attention
- **Develop skills** — the more skills you have, the more flow opportunities
- **Be curious** — ask questions, explore, learn
