# Loss of Self-Consciousness

In flow, the sense of self disappears. You are not thinking about yourself — you are the activity. This is not dissociation but total absorption.

---

## The Paradox

- Self-consciousness is the enemy of flow
- When you worry about how you look, you can't perform
- When you forget yourself, you perform at your best
- The self expands by disappearing
- "The self expands through acts of complexity"

---

## How It Works

- In flow, attention is fully consumed by the activity
- There is no attention left for self-monitoring
- The ego — with its fears, doubts, and insecurities — goes quiet
- You become the activity — the climber becomes the climb
- This is the opposite of self-consciousness, not the absence of awareness

---

## The Growth Effect

- After flow, you remember yourself as someone who did something difficult
- Each flow experience expands your sense of self
- You become more than you were before
- "The self expands through acts of complexity"
- Flow is how the self grows
