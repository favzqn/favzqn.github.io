# Intrinsic Motivation

Intrinsic motivation is doing something for its own sake — because it is interesting, enjoyable, or meaningful, not for external rewards like money, fame, or approval.

---

## Intrinsic vs Extrinsic

- **Intrinsic**: I write because I love writing
- **Extrinsic**: I write because I want to be published
- **Intrinsic**: I play because it's fun
- **Extrinsic**: I play because I want to win
- Intrinsic motivation produces flow; extrinsic motivation produces anxiety

---

## Why It Matters

- Intrinsic motivation produces better performance
- Intrinsic motivation produces more creativity
- Intrinsic motivation produces more persistence
- Intrinsic motivation produces more satisfaction
- "People who learn to control inner experience will be able to determine the quality of their lives"

---

## How to Cultivate

- **Find the interesting in the mundane** — even boring tasks have challenges
- **Set your own goals** — don't let others define what matters
- **Focus on the process** — not the outcome
- **Develop curiosity** — ask questions, explore
- **Reward yourself with the activity itself** — not external prizes
