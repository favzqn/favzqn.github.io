# Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

Hungarian-American psychologist (1934–2021). Professor at Claremont Graduate University. Pioneered the study of flow and optimal experience. One of the most influential positive psychologists.

---

## Key Contributions

- Identified and named the "flow" state
- Developed the concept of the "autotelic self"
- Created the Experience Sampling Method (ESM)
- Wrote 12 books and over 120 articles
- His work influenced positive psychology, education, and business

---

## Core Ideas

- Happiness is not something that happens — it is something you create
- Flow is the optimal human experience
- The best moments require effort, not passivity
- The self expands through acts of complexity
- "People who learn to control inner experience will be able to determine the quality of their lives"

---

## Background

- Born in Fiume, Italy (now Rijeka, Croatia) in 1934
- Survived World War II in Hungary
- Moved to the United States in 1956
- Studied psychology at the University of Chicago
- Became professor at the University of Chicago, then Claremont Graduate University
- Died in 2021 at age 87
