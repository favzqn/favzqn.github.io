# Sentimental Love

The subtitle signals this is a love story told through feeling, not action. Nothing happens. Everything is felt.

---

## The Form

White Nights is a "sentimental love story" — not a romance with plot, but a romance with emotion. The four nights contain no kissing, no declarations of undying love, no dramatic gestures. They contain walking, talking, and feeling.

## The Content

- The dreamer loves Nastenka. She feels gratitude and pity for him.
- Nastenka loves the lodger. He has been absent for a year.
- The dreamer offers everything. Nastenka offers friendship.
- When forced to choose, Nastenka chooses the lodger without hesitation.

## The Point

Sentimental love is not reciprocal. It is experienced fully by one person and received as something else by the other. The dreamer's love is real — it just isn't returned.

## Key Insight

> "You simply feel sorry for me, my little friend! What's done is done!"

The dreamer knows, even as he speaks, that Nastenka's feelings are pity, not love. He says it anyway. The sentimental lover speaks their truth regardless of the response.
