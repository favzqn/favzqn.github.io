# Nastenka

The young woman the dreamer meets on the canal embankment. Raised by a blind grandmother, she fell in love with a lodger who left for Moscow and promised to return.

---

## Character

- Young, curious, warm, unafraid
- Raised by a blind grandmother who pinned her to a chair to keep her from wandering
- Fell in love with a lodger who introduced her to Walter Scott and the theatre
- Has been waiting a year for him to return
- Is torn between gratitude for the dreamer and love for the lodger

## The Choice

When the lodger finally appears on the fourth night, Nastenka runs to him without hesitation. She writes the dreamer a letter asking for forgiveness. She hopes he will love her "as a sister."

## Key Insight

Nastenka is not cruel. She is honest. She told the dreamer from the beginning that she loved someone else. She gave him every warning. He chose to love her anyway.
