# The Dreamer (White Nights)

The unnamed narrator of *White Nights*. A lonely man in St. Petersburg who has no friends, no relationships, and no life outside his fantasies.

---

## Character

- Lives alone in a remote part of St. Petersburg
- Has no friends or acquaintances
- Talks to buildings as if they are friends
- Considers himself a "dreamer" — someone who lives in imagination
- Is nervous and trembling with women
- Has a rich inner world of fantasies, adventures, and love affairs
- Knows he is wasting his life but cannot change

## Arc

1. Alone, walking in the white nights
2. Meets Nastenka — first real human connection
3. Four nights of walking, talking, feeling
4. Falls in love — offers everything
5. Loses her to the lodger
6. Returns to solitude, but grateful: "A whole minute of bliss!"
