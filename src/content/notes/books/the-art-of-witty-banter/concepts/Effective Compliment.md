# Effective Compliment

Complimenting choices and effort, not inherited traits, to maximize impact and authenticity.

---

## The Rules

1. **Compliment things they care about**: if it doesn't matter to them, the compliment feels fake
2. **Compliment choices, not genetics**: their style, decisions, effort — not their eye color
3. **Compliment what they want to be seen as**: pay attention to how they want to be perceived

## Examples

- Good: "I love your Hawaiian shirts — you always stand out" (validates their style choice)
- Bad: "You have beautiful eyes" (they didn't choose their eyes)
- Good: "Your fitness routine is really paying off" (validates their effort)
- Bad: "You're so tall" (they can't control that)

## Why It Works

- Validates their decisions and thought process
- Confirms that others see their uniqueness
- Creates reciprocity — people are kind to those who are kind first
- Avoids the "flattery" trap that makes you look untrustworthy

## Key Insight

Pay attention to what people spend money on, how they present themselves, and what they talk about proudly. Those are the targets for maximum-impact compliments.
