# Go Beyond the Literal

The gateway skill to witty banter: stop answering statements at face value and instead follow the humorous thread.

---

## The Problem

When someone says "I spent a fortune at the Apple store today," most people respond with "Oh, how much?" — a literal, face-value answer that's boring and doesn't build rapport.

## The Solution

Every statement is an invitation to a joke or humorous context. People subconsciously initiate jokes all the time. You just have to notice and follow the thread.

## Examples

| Literal (boring) | Beyond the literal (witty) |
|---|---|
| "How much?" | "It's so expensive there I had to sell a kidney to buy my new phone." |
| "What song?" | "Something from his new album called 'Songs to Make Your Ears Bleed.'" |
| "What team?" | "What sport?" (following the "deity" metaphor) |

## How It Works

1. Notice when someone's statement has a non-literal hook (metaphor, exaggeration, double meaning)
2. Follow the humorous interpretation instead of the factual one
3. Stay in the joke — both parties keep building on the same humorous premise
4. Each statement pushes the absurdity further

## Key Insight

Going beyond the literal is not about telling jokes — it's about recognizing the jokes that are already embedded in everyday statements and following them.
