# HPM SBR EDR Framework

Nine response frameworks from Patrick King's *The Art of Witty Banter* that give you options for responding to practically any topic in conversation.

---

## HPM — History, Philosophy, Metaphor

Draw from your own experience, opinion, or analogy:

- **History**: reply with a personal memory or experience ("That reminds me of when I...")
- **Philosophy**: reply with an opinion or feeling ("I think the real issue is...")
- **Metaphor**: reply with an analogy ("That's just like snowboarding, isn't it?")

HPM is internal and personal — it has nothing to do with the other person. It evokes what the topic brings out of you.

## SBR — Specific, Broad, Related

Ask questions that create context and springboards:

- **Specific**: ask about specific details ("What was the best part?")
- **Broad**: ask broad questions about the topic ("Where was this? Who did you go with?")
- **Related**: ask about something directly or broadly related to the subject

SBR enables smooth transitions from main topic to sub-topic to completely new topic.

## EDR — Emotion, Detail, Restate

Engage by validating the other person:

- **Emotion**: acknowledge and ask about their feelings ("That must have been exciting!")
- **Detail**: ask for details and how they relate to the person ("What happened next?")
- **Restate**: restate what they said to show understanding ("So you went skiing in the mountains last weekend?")

EDR creates a tremendous sense of comfort and validation. People like to be the center of attention.

---

## Key Insight

Together, these nine frameworks add engagement, personalization, depth, and intrigue to any conversation. If you are stumped or see an awkward silence approaching, you have nine distinct options to choose from.
