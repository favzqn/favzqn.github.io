# Double Explanations

Preparing two versions of answers to common questions: a layman's explanation to draw people in, then an expert explanation to add depth.

---

## The Problem

Conversations follow predictable patterns. The first ten questions you answer when meeting someone are always the same. People give boring, generic answers to these questions.

## The Technique

1. **Layman's Explanation**: a short, pithy, relatable answer ("I do paperwork for businesses")
2. **Expert Explanation**: a more detailed version that adds substance ("I'm a corporate lawyer specializing in business transactions and corporate filings")

Lead with the layman's explanation because it's accessible. The expert explanation is available if they ask for more.

## Why It Works

- Stays general enough to reach the most people
- Specific enough to not be boring
- Gives you an opening to further explain yourself
- Lays a wider funnel to engage people

## Key Insight

Prepare three interesting answers for each of the ~10 predictable questions. That alone will make you a significantly better conversationalist.
