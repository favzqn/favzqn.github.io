# What Were You Saying

The phrase "What were you saying?" signals conversational self-awareness about speaking equity and respect.

---

## When to Use

1. **When you interrupt someone** and they let you keep speaking — go back and give them the floor
2. **When you've gone on too long** in a rant or story — acknowledge it and redirect
3. **When the topic drifts** and you forget what they were saying — bring it back
4. **When topics overlap** in group conversations — spread the wealth

## Why It Works

- Tells the other person you're not full of yourself
- Makes you more likable because you give proper attention
- Signals humility and self-awareness
- Resets respect and ease in the conversation

## Variations

- "Sorry for interrupting, please go on"
- "Was that what you were going to say?"
- "I cut you off — what were you saying?"

## Key Insight

This one phrase is how King became a supervisor's favorite employee. It's simple, but it signals everything about your character.
