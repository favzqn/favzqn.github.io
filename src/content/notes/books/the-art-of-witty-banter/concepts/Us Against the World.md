# Us Against the World

Creating artificial closeness by positioning yourself and your conversation partner as the only sane people in a crazy environment.

---

## The Psychology

People have a universal desire to belong. By calling out a shared context or emotion, you create a perception of similarity — even if you two aren't really all that different from everyone else.

## Examples

- "Can you believe how aggressive everyone is here?"
- "Glad I'm not the only one here who..."
- "Yeah, it's exhausting in there, isn't it?"
- Locking eyes with a stranger after witnessing something odd

## How It Works

1. Recognize a shared environment or emotional state
2. Call it out as if only you two share it
3. This creates an artificial feeling of closeness and similarity
4. It also makes you look observant — you noticed what others missed

## Key Insight

You're creating a bond through shared observation of outsiders. The reality is you two probably aren't that different from everyone else, but the PERCEPTION of similarity drives likability.
