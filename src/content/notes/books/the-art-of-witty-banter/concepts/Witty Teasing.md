# Witty Teasing

Playful provocation that creates rapport — delivered with a deadpan face that slowly fades into a smile.

---

## The Rules

1. **Deadpan delivery**: completely serious, but exaggerated in seriousness to the point of absurdity
2. **The fade**: the deadpan face must slowly fade into a smile to let them feel safe
3. **Indication of kidding**: smirk, lean in, or use tone to signal you're joking

## Safe Topics to Tease About

- Acknowledged weaknesses ("You burnt the pancakes, what a shock!")
- Talents and positives ("I bet you were told you have too much money to shop there again")

## Never Tease About

- Aspects they cannot change or control
- Deeply personal choices
- Political or religious views
- Values they hold sacred

## Techniques

- **Misinterpret their words**: pretend they said something different ("I love cats" → "You eat cats?")
- **Exaggerated reaction**: "You like Nickelback? Okay, I am going to jump into an empty pool right now"

## Key Insight

Keep it lighthearted. Your overall objective is to create mutual comfort, not to prove how clever you are.
