# Derail Your Train of Thought

Parking your ego and yielding the conversational floor to the other person's topic — even when you have something better to say.

---

## The Problem

Most people fight against being derailed. They cling to their own topic because they feel they never get the spotlight. When both people fight for their own trains, nobody listens.

## The Solution

Shut up far more often than you think. When someone else has a topic, actively board their train and ride with it. Don't just tolerate it — engage with it.

## The Talk Show Host Model

Talk show hosts are the ultimate example. They CAN do everything their guest does AND better. So why doesn't the show become a monologue? Because they park their egos and let other people's trains run.

## Key Insight

If you're the only good conversationalist, yield your topic to theirs. If both people yield, you get the best conversation possible — two trains meeting in the middle.
