# Fallback Stories

Pre-prepared stories with four components that rescue dead conversations and create engaging discussion.

---

## The Four Components

1. **Bridging Sentence**: a short, plausible transition from the previous topic into your story ("I just heard about this the other day...")
2. **The Story**: a brief narrative — an interesting occurrence, moral dilemma, or hypothetical situation
3. **Your Position**: your opinion on the situation — people are more willing to share their position after you disclose yours first
4. **Questions**: specific questions about what THEY would do in the same situation

## Best Topics

- Hypothetical situations ("Would you do X if...")
- Moral dilemmas (not dark or depressing)
- Universal themes that ensure everyone has something to add
- Questions about what they would do in certain situations

## Example

"My friend spent $300 on a meal, mostly on wine, for no particular reason. I think it's excessive but also kind of admirable. What's the most you'd ever spend on a single meal?"

## Key Insight

Don't just tell a story — ask for their opinion on a specific aspect. "What would you do?" is too broad. Narrow it down.
