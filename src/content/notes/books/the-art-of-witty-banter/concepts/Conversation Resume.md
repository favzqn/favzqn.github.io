# Conversation Resume

A pre-prepared list of your best stories, talking points, achievements, and personal history — reviewed before social settings like a job resume before an interview.

---

## The Concept

People prepare extensively for job interviews — reviewing accomplishments, preparing stories, rehearsing answers. But in everyday conversations, they show up unprepared and get caught flat-footed.

A conversation resume changes that. It's a written document listing your best conversation material.

## Ingredients

- Notable achievements and personal history
- Matching stories for each item
- Talking points you want to position
- Lessons learned from experiences
- Funny or interesting anecdotes

## How to Use

1. Write it out and review regularly
2. Review before social settings
3. Think about how items fit into natural conversation
4. Position the persona you want to create in others' minds

## Key Insight

Knowing that if everything hits the fan, you'll still have material — that's confidence. Incremental victories build social confidence.
