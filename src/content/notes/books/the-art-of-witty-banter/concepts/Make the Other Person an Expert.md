# Make the Other Person an Expert

Turning your conversation partner into your de facto teacher by being so curious and interested that you insist on learning from them.

---

## The Concept

There are at least 100 topics the other person can teach you about. You don't know what they are, and they might not be important to you — but there's always room for facts you may have overlooked.

## How to Do It

1. Park your ego and assume the role of a student
2. Ask them to teach you about something they know
3. Put favorable labels on them: "Wow, you're really knowledgeable about this"
4. When they share a random fact, seize the opportunity: "Tell me more about that"

## Why It Works

- Inflates their confidence
- Makes them more comfortable with you
- Creates a dynamic where they want to share more
- You gain new perspectives and knowledge

## Key Insight

Curiosity is an approach to others that makes you more receptive. Taking it a step further and turning your partner into your teacher — that's an approach that makes them more receptive to YOU.
