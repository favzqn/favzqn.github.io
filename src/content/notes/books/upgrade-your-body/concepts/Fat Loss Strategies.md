# Fat Loss Strategies

Research-backed strategies for accelerating fat loss and boosting metabolism.

---

## Metabolism Boosters

1. **Ice water** — adding ice cubes boosts metabolism by 20%+; drink first thing in the morning
2. **Vinegar** — acetic acid prevents fat storage; add tbsp to water
3. **Mustard** — boosts metabolism by 20%
4. **Green tea** — boosts metabolism by up to 40% (Oxford Polytechnic)
5. **Cinnamon** — 1/4 tsp daily can help prevent diabetes and regulate blood sugar

## Training Strategies

6. **Cold exercise** — exercising in cold stimulates brown fat production, burning fat 60% faster
7. **Fasted training** — wait until after workout to eat; burns up to 30% more fat. Take BCAAs during workout.
8. **Sleep 8 hours** — doubles fat loss progress on a fitness routine
9. **Don't cut carbs completely** — single portion per day, ideally around workouts

## Nutrition

10. **Meal prep** — bulletproof your diet by preparing meals in advance
11. **Basil** — reduces cortisol (stress hormone that causes fat storage)
12. **Cayenne pepper** — reduces appetite and boosts metabolism

## Key Insight

> "Sleep doubles your fat loss progress when on a fitness routine."

Sleep is the most underrated fat loss tool. Cold exposure is the most overrated-but-effective one.
