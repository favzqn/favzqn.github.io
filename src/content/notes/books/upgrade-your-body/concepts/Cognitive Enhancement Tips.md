# Cognitive Enhancement Tips

Research-backed tips for improving concentration, memory, learning speed, and mental performance.

---

## The Tips

1. **Citrus smells** — increase concentration (Ohio State University). Use citrus candles or lemon oil before focused work.
2. **Yoga** — cuts headaches by up to 70% (Pittsburgh University)
3. **Zinc** — improves learning speed by regulating neuron communication
4. **Coffee + nap** — drink coffee, then take a 20-minute nap. Caffeine kicks in as you wake = maximum alertness
5. **Blueberries** — improve memory and cognitive functioning
6. **Omega-3 fatty acids** — salmon, walnuts, chia seeds improve learning and memory
7. **Positive thinking** — finding the silver lining extends lifespan
8. **Walking in nature** — greatly reduces stress levels
9. **Fake smiling** — even a forced smile boosts dopamine and reduces anger
10. **Rephrase problems out loud** — restating a problem triggers new neural pathways for solutions

## Key Insight

Cognitive enhancement doesn't require expensive supplements. Smells, walks, smiles, and berries do the job.
