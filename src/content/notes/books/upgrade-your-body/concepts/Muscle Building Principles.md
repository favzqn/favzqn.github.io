# Muscle Building Principles

Research-backed principles for building muscle and improving strength.

---

## Training Principles

1. **Go deep on squats** — going to full depth uses 7% more gluteus muscles
2. **Mix rep ranges** — switch between high and low reps to stimulate maximum growth
3. **Slow negatives** — focus on the lowering portion; increases muscle fatigue and growth
4. **Optimal workout time: 6PM** — body is warmest and most alert; improves performance
5. **Turkish get-ups** — torch body fat, build core strength, improve balance
6. **Vary exercises** — don't perform the same routine day-to-day

## Recovery & Nutrition

7. **Slow-release protein before bed** — cottage cheese or casein for overnight growth
8. **BCAAs intra-workout** — prevent fatigue and maintain energy during training
9. **L-glutamine** — 5g with breakfast speeds muscle cell growth
10. **Creatine** — proven to increase strength and power output

## Key Insight

Muscle is built during recovery, not during training. Sleep + protein timing + varied stimulus = growth.
