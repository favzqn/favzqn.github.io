# Sleep Optimization Tips

Actionable tips for improving sleep quality and duration.

---

## The Tips

1. **20-minute afternoon naps** — boost energy, focus, productivity, and improve nighttime sleep quality
2. **Wake during light sleep** — use Sleep Cycle app to avoid grogginess from waking during deep sleep
3. **Blue-light filtering** — f.lux for screens; blue light suppresses melatonin
4. **18°C / 65°F room temperature** — optimal for sleep onset and quality
5. **Sleep with socks on** — regulates blood temperature for faster sleep onset
6. **Tidy bedroom before bed** — releases endorphins and creates mental signal for sleep
7. **Placebo sleep** — tell yourself you got great sleep; your brain will partially believe it
8. **8 hours doubles fat loss** — sleep is the foundation of body composition

## Key Insight

Sleep is not just rest — it's the foundation for fat loss, cognitive function, mood regulation, and physical recovery. Every other optimization is built on top of good sleep.
