# Upgrade Your Body & Mind

*Upgrade Your Body & Mind: Simple Secrets & Strategies to Burn Fat, Build Muscle, Get Lean, Upgrade Your Health* by Peter Paulson (2015). A compact reference of research-backed health and fitness tips organized by category.

---

## Summary

A practical tip-book organized into 7 chapters covering sleep, vision, cognitive function, fat loss, heart health, muscle building, and nutrition. Each tip cites a research source. The book is designed as a go-to reference — pick a goal, flip to the chapter, implement the tips.

### Chapter 1: Improve Your Sleep (9 tips)

- 20-minute afternoon naps improve nighttime sleep quality
- Use apps like Sleep Cycle to wake during light sleep phases
- Blue-light filtering (f.lux) for evening screen use
- 65°F (18°C) is the optimal sleeping temperature
- Sleep with socks on — regulates blood temperature for faster sleep onset
- Tidy your bedroom before sleep — releases endorphins
- Tell yourself you got great sleep — placebo works

### Chapter 2: Upgrade Your Vision (3 tips)

- Eggs contain lutein — prevents vision loss
- Raw carrots provide beta-carotene for eye health
- Pumpkin seed oil adds additional eye protection

### Chapter 3: Upgrade Cognitive Functioning (18 tips)

- Citrus smells increase concentration (Ohio State University)
- Yoga cuts headaches by 70% (Pittsburgh University)
- Zinc improves learning speed by regulating neuron communication
- Coffee before a nap (20 min) = maximum alertness on waking
- Blueberries improve memory and cognitive functioning
- Omega-3s in fatty fish improve learning and memory
- Positive thinking extends lifespan
- Walking in nature reduces stress
- Even a fake smile boosts dopamine

### Chapter 4: Shred Fat & Upgrade Metabolism (18 tips)

- Ice water boosts metabolism by 20%+
- Vinegar (acetic acid) prevents fat storage
- Mustard boosts metabolism by 20%
- Green tea boosts metabolism by up to 40% (Oxford Polytechnic)
- Cold exposure stimulates brown fat — burns fat 60% faster
- Fasted training burns up to 30% more fat
- 8 hours of sleep doubles fat loss progress
- Don't cut carbs completely — single portion around workouts

### Chapter 5: Protect Your Heart (3 tips)

- Portobello mushrooms clear arteries (chitin)
- Daily mushrooms reduce Alzheimer's risk by 70%
- Mushrooms boost immune function

### Chapter 6: Add Muscle (12 tips)

- Go deep on squats — 7% more glute activation
- Mix rep ranges for maximum growth
- Slow negatives increase muscle fatigue and growth
- 6PM is the optimal workout time (body temperature peak)
- Consume slow-release protein before sleep for overnight growth
- BCAAs intra-workout prevent fatigue
- L-glutamine (5g with breakfast) speeds muscle cell growth

### Chapter 7: Eating to Become Superhuman (10 tips)

- Prawns beat chicken per calorie for vitamin D and protein
- Spinach + eggs = cognitive boost (tyrosine)
- Greek yogurt + blueberries = young-looking skin
- Turmeric (curcumin) fights dementia
- Maca boosts energy and mood

## Key Themes

1. **Small Changes, Big Impact** — most tips are single-ingredient or single-behavior changes
2. **Research-Backed** — every tip cites a university or journal source
3. **Stackable** — tips are designed to be combined
4. **Sleep Is the Foundation** — sleep improves fat loss, cognition, mood, and recovery
5. **Cold Is Your Friend** — cold water, cold exposure, cold exercise all boost metabolism
6. **Food Is Medicine** — specific foods for specific outcomes
