# Desire Is Suffering

Every desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until it is fulfilled.

---

## The Logic

- If you have 10 desires and you fulfill 7, you're still unhappy (3 unfulfilled)
- If you have 2 desires and you fulfill 1, you're mostly happy
- The fewer desires you have, the more you can accept the current state
- The less your mind moves (toward past or future), the happier you are

---

## Desire as a Contract

> "Every desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until it is fulfilled. If you want to be happy, reduce your desires." — Naval Ravikant

---

## The Solution

- Give up desire for external things
- Accept the current state of things
- Let go of trying to make temporary situations permanent
- Be present
