# Presence

The mind exists in motion toward the past or future. Being present is the key to happiness.

---

## How the Mind Works

- The mind is always moving: regretting the past or planning the future
- When you latch onto a feeling (even happiness), the mind starts moving
- "I'm happy now" → "I want to stay happy" → mind is now moving
- Internal silence = presence = contentment

---

## How to Be Present

- Meditation
- Cold showers (forces you into the present moment)
- Doing things you enjoy (flow state)
- Eliminating desires

---

## Quote

> "The more present I am, the happier and more content I will be."
