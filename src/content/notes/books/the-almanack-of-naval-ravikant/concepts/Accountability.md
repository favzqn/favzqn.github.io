# Accountability

Take business risks under your own name. Society will reward you with responsibility, equity, and leverage.

---

## Why It Matters

- Accountability means putting your reputation on the line
- When you are the agent (not the employee), you share in the upside
- Without accountability, you're just renting out your time
- It's the bridge between specific knowledge and leverage

---

## Key Insight

> "Embrace accountability, and take business risks under your own name. Society will reward you with responsibility, equity, and leverage."

If you're a trusted, reliable, high-integrity, long-term-thinking person, people will want to work with you, invest in you, and give you opportunities.
