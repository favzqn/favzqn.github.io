# Leverage

Fortunes require leverage. Business leverage comes from three forms.

---

## Three Types of Leverage

### 1. Labor (Permissioned)
- Most old-fashioned type
- Requires permission — people have to work for you
- Richest people in history used this

### 2. Capital (Permissioned)
- Requires permission — people have to give you money
- Trust and track record required
- "Give me a lever long enough and a place to stand, and I will move the earth" — Archimedes

### 3. Code + Media (Permissionless)
- New type of leverage
- Code: software, programs, AI
- Media: books, podcasts, videos, tweets
- No permission needed — you can create and deploy
- Marginal cost of reproduction is near zero
- The most equalizing form of leverage

---

## Key Insight

> The new rich are people who own code and media. They don't need anyone's permission to use their leverage.

Code and media create a disconnection between inputs and outputs — you can impact millions without spending time physically.
