# Philosophy

The real truths are heresies. They cannot be spoken. Only discovered, whispered, and perhaps read.

---

## The Meaning of Life — Three Answers

### Answer 1: It's Personal
- You have to find your own meaning
- Any wisdom others give you will sound like nonsense
- The important part is not the answer — it's the question

### Answer 2: There Is No Meaning
- You've been dead for 10 billion years and will be dead for 70 billion more
- Anything you do will fade
- No one will remember you past a certain number of generations
- You have to create your own meaning
- After death, it's like before you were born — you just don't care

### Answer 3: Entropy
- The arrow of time comes from entropy (second law of thermodynamics)
- Living systems locally reverse entropy
- We globally accelerate entropy until the heat death of the Universe
- Maybe we're all one thing — indistinguishable at the end

## Rational Buddhism

- Reconcile science and spirituality
- The mind creates suffering through desires and attachments
- Meditation helps you observe the mind
- Accept the current state — it's all temporary
