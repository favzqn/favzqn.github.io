# Meditation

Intermittent fasting for the mind. Time spent undistracted and alone resolves the unresolved.

---

## Naval's Methods

1. **Just sitting** — eyes closed, no thoughts, write down if anything comes up
2. **Walking meditation** — simply walk and observe
3. **Noting meditation** — observe and label what you notice

---

## Benefits

- Reduces mental fatigue
- Increases presence
- Calms the monkey mind
- Helps you see reality clearly
- Improves decision-making

---

## Quote

> "Time spent undistracted and alone, in self-examination, journaling, meditation, resolves the unresolved and takes us from mentally fat to fit."
