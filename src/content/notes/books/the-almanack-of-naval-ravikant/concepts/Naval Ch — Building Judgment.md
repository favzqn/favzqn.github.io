# Building Judgment

Wisdom applied to external problems is judgment. In an age of leverage, one correct decision can win everything.

---

## Key Ideas

- "Clear thinker" is a better compliment than "smart"
- Hard work matters, but judgment matters more
- Direction matters more than speed (especially with leverage)
- You don't get rich by spending time to save money — you save time to make money

---

## How to Think Clearly

- Real knowledge is built from the ground up (can't learn trigonometry without arithmetic)
- If you can't explain something to a child, you don't understand it
- Richard Feynman explained math in 3 pages from the number line to precalculus
- I'd rather understand the basics really well than memorize complicated concepts
- Clear thinkers appeal to their own authority, not others'

---

## Decision-Making

- Don't make decisions you'll be fundamentally unhappy about
- Be radically honest with yourself
- Study logic and math — they're foundational
- Read what you love until you love to read

---

## Learning to Read

- The genuine love for reading itself is a superpower
- "Read what you love until you love to read"
- Reread more than you read new books
- Build a foundation of reading — then you won't fear any book
- Give up reading for a while if you're overwhelmed — come back
