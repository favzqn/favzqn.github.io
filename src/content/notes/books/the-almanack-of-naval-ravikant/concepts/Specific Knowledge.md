# Specific Knowledge

Knowledge you cannot be trained for. If society can train you, it can train someone else and replace you.

---

## How to Find It

- Found by pursuing your genuine curiosity and passion — not what's "hot"
- Building it feels like play to you but looks like work to others
- Taught through apprenticeships, not schools
- Often highly technical or creative
- Cannot be outsourced or automated

---

## Examples

- Sales skills, coding ability, pattern recognition
- The ability to learn anything quickly
- Telling people what they don't want to hear
- Judging character, assessing risk

---

## Key Insight

Specific knowledge is your competitive advantage. It's the thing that makes you irreplaceable and creates wealth.
