# Freedom From

Naval's shift from "freedom to" to "freedom from" — internal freedom over external freedom.

---

## The Shift

| Old Definition | New Definition |
|---------------|----------------|
| "Freedom to" — do anything I want | "Freedom from" — freedom from reaction |
| "Freedom to" — whenever I feel like | "Freedom from" — freedom from anger, sadness |
| External freedom | Internal freedom |

---

## Freedom From

- Freedom from reaction
- Freedom from feeling angry
- Freedom from being sad
- Freedom from being forced to do things
- Freedom from other people's expectations
- Freedom from the need for approval

---

## Quote

> "My old definition was 'freedom to.' Freedom to do anything I want. Now, the freedom I'm looking for is internal freedom. It's 'freedom from.' Freedom from reaction. Freedom from feeling angry. Freedom from being sad. Freedom from being forced to do things."
