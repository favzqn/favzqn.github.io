# Meditation + Mental Strength

Meditation is intermittent fasting for the mind. Too many distractions lead to a heavy mind.

---

## Naval's Meditation Practice

- Tried at least four different types
- Settled on **just sitting** — eyes closed, no mantras, no rituals
- Sit for 60 minutes — if you think of something, write it down, then sit again
- If you can't meditate or fall asleep — victory either way
- The real meditation is recognizing how out of control your mind is

---

## Cold Showers

- Most suffering comes from avoidance
- The suffering from a cold shower is the tip-toeing in — once you're in, it's just cold
- Cold exposure activates the immune system (Wim Hof method)
- Hot showers are just one less thing to need out of life

---

## Key Insights

- Your breath is the gateway between autonomic and voluntary nervous systems
- Relaxed breathing tells your body you're safe
- Most of our suffering comes from avoidance
- You can retrain your mind to not need external comforts
