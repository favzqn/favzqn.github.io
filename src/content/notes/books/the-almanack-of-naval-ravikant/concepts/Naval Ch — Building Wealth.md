# Building Wealth

The core chapter on how wealth is actually created.

---

## Key Ideas

- Making money is not a thing you do — it's a skill you learn
- If you lost all your money, you could get it back in 5-10 years because it's a skillset
- Getting rich is about knowing what to do, who to do it with, and when to do it
- Hard work matters but must be directed in the right way

---

## How to Get Rich (Tweetstorm)

### 1. Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status
- Wealth = assets that earn while you sleep
- Money = how we transfer time and wealth
- Status = your place in the social hierarchy
- Ignore people playing status games

### 2. You Must Own Equity
- Renting out your time won't get you rich
- Own a piece of a business to gain financial freedom

### 3. Give Society What It Wants
- Provide what society wants but doesn't yet know how to get — at scale
- The internet has massively broadened the possible space of careers

### 4. Play Long-Term Games
- All returns in life come from compound interest
- Pick business partners with high intelligence, energy, and integrity
- Don't partner with cynics and pessimists

### 5. Learn to Sell and Build
- If you can do both, you will be unstoppable

---

## The Wealth Equation

> Specific Knowledge + Accountability + Leverage = Wealth

- **Specific Knowledge** — knowledge you can't be trained for
- **Accountability** — take business risks under your own name
- **Leverage** — labor, capital, code, media
