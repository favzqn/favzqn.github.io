# Happiness Is Learned

Happiness is not inherited or chosen — it's a skill that can be developed, like fitness or nutrition.

---

## Key Principles

- Happiness is the absence of desire, especially for external things
- When nothing is missing, the mind shuts down — internal silence
- Every positive thought contains a negative thought (duality)
- Greatness comes out of suffering
- Don't latch onto happiness — let it be temporary

---

## Habits That Build Happiness

- Exercise — makes you happy every time
- Meditation — lets your mind rest
- Spending time with happy people
- Eating well
- Avoiding news (negative, causes anxiety)
- Journaling

---

## The Default State

> We are highly judgmental survival-and-replication machines. We constantly walk around thinking, "I need this" or "I need that," trapped in the web of desires. Happiness is the state when nothing is missing.
