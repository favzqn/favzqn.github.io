# Judgment

Wisdom applied to external problems. Knowing the long-term consequences of your actions.

---

## The Formula

> Wisdom → Applied to External Problems → Judgment

In an age of leverage, one correct decision can win everything.

---

## Key Principles

1. Direction > Speed — especially with leverage
2. Hard work without judgment is wasted effort
3. "Clear thinker" is better than "smart"
4. Real knowledge is intrinsic — built from the ground up
5. Don't memorize — understand fundamentals

---

## Falsifiability

- Test your beliefs against reality
- If you can't be wrong, you're not thinking
- Change your mind when evidence demands it
