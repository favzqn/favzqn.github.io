# Learning Happiness

Happiness is not something you inherit or even choose — it's a highly personal skill that can be learned.

---

## Key Ideas

- Naval went from 2/10 to 9/10 in happiness
- Having money helps but it's a very small piece
- Happiness is a default state — it's there when you remove the sense of something missing
- Not about positive thoughts — it's about the absence of desire

---

## Naval's Definition of Happiness

> Happiness is the state when nothing is missing. When nothing is missing, your mind shuts down and stops running into the past or future to regret something or to plan something. In that absence, for a moment, you have internal silence.

---

## Happiness Is a Choice

- It's a skill you develop, like fitness or nutrition
- A rational person can find peace by cultivating indifference to things beyond their control
- Eliminate desires rather than fulfill them
- Anticipation of vices pulls us into the future — eliminate it to be present

---

## Envy Is the Enemy

- Envy is the only sin that's no fun at all
- Jealousy fades when you realize you can't have everything
- Stop comparing — it's a zero-sum game
