# Saving Yourself

The skills you need: reading, persuasion, and mathematics. Freedom from expectations, anger, and self-imposed limitations.

---

## Essential Skills

### Reading
- Related to the skill of self-education
- Read what you love until you love to read

### Persuasion
- If you can influence people, you can get a lot done
- It's a learnable skill

### Mathematics
- Mathematics is the language of nature
- You don't need a lot — basic statistics, arithmetic, probability
- Foundation for money, science, game theory, economics, computing

---

## Choosing Freedom

- "The hardest thing is not doing what you want — it's knowing what you want"
- Everyone makes it up as they go along — there are no "adults"
- "Freedom to" vs "Freedom from" — Naval's shift
- Old: freedom to do anything
- New: freedom from reaction, from being forced to do things

---

## Freedom From Expectations

- Other people's expectations are their problem, not yours
- Courage isn't charging into battle — it's not caring what people think
- Value your time — it's more important than money or friends
- If you're not doing what you want, earning, or learning — what are you doing?

---

## Freedom From Anger

- Anger is a way to force change on the world
- Anger is always a temporary solution
- Hold onto your anger and it'll hold onto you
- Realize you're just a monkey with a plan
