# Compound Interest

All the returns in life — whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge — come from compound interest.

---

## The Key Idea

- Play iterated games with long-term people
- Pick an industry where you can play long-term games
- Don't optimize for short-term gains at the expense of long-term relationships
- In long-term games, people cooperate because they know they'll see each other again

---

## Beyond Money

Compound interest applies to:
- **Wealth** — investments grow exponentially over time
- **Knowledge** — learning builds on itself
- **Relationships** — trust deepens over years
- **Skills** — practice compounds into mastery

---

## Quote

> "All the returns in life, whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge, come from compound interest."
