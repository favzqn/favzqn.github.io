# Naval Ravikant

Co-founder of AngelList. Philosopher-entrepreneur known for his wisdom on wealth, happiness, and life.

---

## Key Ideas

- **Wealth Equation:** Specific Knowledge × Accountability × Leverage = Wealth
- **Happiness Equation:** Happiness = Reality - Expectations
- **Reading:** "Read what you love until you love to read"
- **Desire:** Every desire is a contract to be unhappy until fulfilled
- **Judgment:** Knowing the long-term consequences of your actions

---

## Background

- Born in India, raised in the US
- Self-educated in philosophy, economics, and technology
- AngelList: platform for startup investing
- Famous tweetstorm: "How to Get Rich (Without Getting Lucky)"
- Hosts podcasts on wealth, health, and happiness
- Practices "Rational Buddhism" — science + spirituality

---

## His Philosophy

- Wealth is not about hard work — it's about judgment and leverage
- Happiness is a skill, not a trait
- Freedom from > Freedom to
- Read voraciously, think clearly, act decisively
- "The three big ones in life: wealth, health, happiness — in that order"
