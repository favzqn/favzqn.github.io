# Eric Jorgenson

Author of *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant*. Entrepreneur and writer who compiled Naval's wisdom into a book.

---

## Key Facts

- Compiled Naval Ravikant's tweets, podcasts, and interviews into a cohesive book
- Made the book free to read online
- Focused on distilling Naval's philosophy into actionable advice
- The book covers wealth creation and happiness — two areas Naval is known for

---

## His Approach

- Curated and organized Naval's scattered wisdom
- Added structure and context to Naval's tweets and quotes
- Created a practical guide that readers can apply to their lives
- Made the almanack freely available — embodying Naval's philosophy of giving
