## Key Concepts

### Wealth Building
- [[Specific Knowledge]] — knowledge you can't be trained for
- [[Leverage]] — code, media, capital, labor
- [[Accountability]] — take business risks under your own name
- [[Compound Interest]] — long-term games with long-term people
- [[Judgment]] — knowing long-term consequences of actions

### Happiness
- [[Happiness Is Learned]] — happiness is a default state when nothing is missing
- [[Desire Is Suffering]] — every desire is a contract to be unhappy until fulfilled
- [[Presence]] — the mind exists in motion toward past or future
- [[Meditation]] — intermittent fasting for the mind
- [[Freedom From]] — "freedom from" > "freedom to"

## The Famous Tweetstorm

"How to Get Rich (Without Getting Lucky)":
1. Seek wealth, not money or status
2. Understand ethical wealth creation is possible
3. You're not going to get rich renting out your time — you must own equity
4. Give society what it wants but does not yet know how to get — at scale
5. Play iterated games — compound interest
6. Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage
7. Learn to sell. Learn to build. If you can do both, you will be unstoppable.
