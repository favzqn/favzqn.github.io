# Wiki Overview

## What This Is

A personal knowledge base built from 36 non-fiction books, 6 novels, and 1 article, cross-referenced into a connected graph of ~551+ pages. The wiki follows the LLM Wiki pattern: immutable sources in `.raw/`, agent-maintained knowledge in `wiki/`.

## The Philosophical Thread

The books trace a single idea through different traditions:

**Solitude** (Rilke) → **Presence** (Naval) → **Effortless Action** (Lao Tzu) → **Flow** (Csikszentmihalyi) → **Deep Work** (Newport)

This isn't a reading list. It's a philosophy of life built across 25 books spanning Taoism, Islam, psychology, neuroscience, literature, business, and negotiation.

## Cross-Book Insights

The wiki's strongest feature is its cross-references. Key connections:

- **Flow ≈ Wu Wei ≈ Alpha State** — psychology, philosophy, and neuroscience describing the same peak experience
- **Desire = Suffering** — Naval (Buddhist), Dostoevsky (Raskolnikov's ego), Lao Tzu (the uncarved block)
- **Attention is the meta-skill** — Deep Work + Digital Minimalism + Anxious Generation + Why We Sleep all converge
- **Tactical empathy = Non-contention** — Chris Voss and Lao Tzu teach the same thing
- **Childhood needs slowness** — Rilke's patience + Haidt's free play + Csikszentmihalyi's autotelic self
- **Alienation is universal** — Yozo (No Longer Human) + Raskolnikov (Crime and Punishment) + Santiago (Old Man and the Sea) + Montag (Fahrenheit 451) + the Dreamer (White Nights) all face isolation, but respond differently
- **Influence is everywhere** — Cialdini's theory (Influence) + Carnegie's principles (How to Win Friends) + Voss's tactics (Never Split the Difference) + Jones's magic words (Exactly What to Say) + King's frameworks (Read People Like a Book, Persuasion Tactics, Art of Witty Banter, Conversationally Speaking) all describe the same skill from different angles
- **Patience is the meta-skill** — Graham's margin of safety + Lao Tzu's wu wei + Naval's "play long-term games" + Rilke's "live the questions" all converge on the same principle
- **The Influence Spectrum** — nine books on influence (Cialdini, Carnegie, Voss, Jones, King ×4) converge on: emotion before logic, listen more than talk, make it their idea
- **The Literature of Alienation** — five novels (Dazai, Dostoevsky ×2, Bradbury, Hemingway) present five responses to alienation: withdrawal, rebellion, awakening, fantasy, endurance
- **The Body as Battleground** — five books on body-mind (van der Kolk, Walker, Norton, Paulson, Pollan) establish: the body is the mind, sleep is the foundation, the food system is the enemy
- **The Architecture of Wealth** — four books on wealth (Graham, Kaufman, Knight, Naval) converge on: compound interest, temperament over intelligence, create don't compete
- **Learning How to Learn** — four books on learning (Oakley, Newport, Csikszentmihalyi, Silva) establish: the brain has two modes, both are needed, cycling between them is the key
- **Faith as Framework** — four spiritual books (Faris, Lao Tzu, Rilke, Silva) converge on: surrender produces power, rhythm is the foundation, stillness is productive
