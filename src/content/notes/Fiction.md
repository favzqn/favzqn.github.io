# Fiction

Novels as philosophical texts. The fiction in this garden is not entertainment — it is a lens for understanding the human condition. Each novel is treated as a source of ideas, not just a story.

---

## The Books

- [[No Longer Human]] — Osamu Dazai's masterpiece of alienation. Yozo's conviction that he is [[Disqualified from Being Human]] is the purest expression of existential isolation in literature.
- [[Crime and Punishment]] — Dostoevsky's exploration of the [[Extraordinary Man Theory]] and the psychology of guilt. Raskolnikov's crime is an intellectual experiment that destroys him.
- [[Fahrenheit 451]] — Bradbury's prediction of a world where books are burned and television replaces thought. [[Censorship by the People]], [[The Parlour Walls]], and [[Books as Living Memory]].
- [[The Old Man and the Sea]] — Hemingway's meditation on [[Dignity in Defeat]]. Santiago is "destroyed but not defeated."
- [[White Nights]] — Dostoevsky's dreamer — a man who lives entirely in fantasy. [[A Minute of Bliss]]: one moment of happiness justifies a lifetime.

---

## Key Concepts

- [[The Clown]] — Yozo's humor as defense mechanism; the mask that protects and imprisons
- [[Disqualified from Being Human]] — the conviction of being fundamentally other
- [[Fear of Human Beings]] — the terror that drives all relationships in No Longer Human
- [[Censorship by the People]] — Bradbury's insight: censorship comes from the people, not the government
- [[Books as Living Memory]] — knowledge preserved by becoming it; the book people of Fahrenheit 451
- [[Dignity in Defeat]] — Hemingway's philosophy: destruction is not defeat
- [[The Dreamer]] — White Nights' portrait of a man who prefers fantasy to reality

---

## The Literature of Alienation

The novels in this garden share a theme: **alienation from the human condition.** Yozo cannot connect. Raskolnikov cannot submit. Montag cannot conform. Santiago cannot accept defeat. The dreamer cannot engage with reality.

Each novel offers a different response to alienation: Yozo self-destructs. Raskolnikov confesses. Montag rebels. Santiago endures. The dreamer retreats.
