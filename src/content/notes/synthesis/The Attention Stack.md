# The Attention Stack

Attention is the meta-skill of the 21st century. Six books converge on the same conclusion: whoever controls their attention controls their life.

## The Stack (Bottom to Top)

### Layer 1: Sleep — The Foundation
[[Why We Sleep]] establishes that sleep is non-negotiable. The [[The Glymphatic System]] cleans the brain during sleep. [[Memory Consolidation]] happens during [[REM vs NREM]] cycles. Without 7-9 hours, every layer above collapses. [[The Sleep-Deprived Society]] documents how modern life systematically destroys this foundation.

### Layer 2: Digital Boundaries — Protecting the Foundation
[[Digital Minimalism Defined]] argues for intentional technology use. [[The Digital Declutter]] is the 30-day reset. [[Solitude]] — being free from input of other minds — is the prerequisite for deep thought. [[The Attention Economy]] explains why this is hard: trillion-dollar companies profit from your distraction.

### Layer 3: Childhood Development — Building the Capacity
[[The Anxious Generation]] shows that [[The Great Rewiring]] from play-based to phone-based childhood destroyed the ability to focus before it even formed. [[The Decline of Free Play]] meant children never developed [[Autotelic Self]] — the personality that finds flow naturally. [[Haidt's Four Norms]] propose structural fixes.

### Layer 4: Deep Work — The Skill
[[Deep Work]] is distraction-free concentration that pushes cognitive limits. The [[Deep Work Hypothesis]]: deep work is simultaneously rare and valuable. Those who cultivate it thrive. Four philosophies ([[Monastic Philosophy]], [[Bimodal Philosophy]], [[Rhythmic Philosophy]], [[Journalistic Philosophy]]) offer different approaches.

### Layer 5: Specific Knowledge — The Output
[[Specific Knowledge]] — knowledge you can't be trained for — is the product of deep work. [[Naval Ch — Building Wealth]] argues you won't get rich renting your time. You need [[Leverage]] (code, media, capital) which requires deep skills which require protected attention.

### Layer 6: Judgment — The Return
[[Judgment]] — knowing long-term consequences — is the highest use of focused attention. [[Compound Interest]] rewards those who apply judgment over decades, not days.

## The Paradox

The [[The Iron Law of the Market]] says demand must exist first. But the deepest demand — for attention, focus, and meaning — is exactly what the attention economy destroys. The stack is self-reinforcing: protect attention → develop deep skills → create specific knowledge → build leverage → exercise judgment → compound returns.

## The Destruction Path

When any layer breaks, the stack collapses:
- Sleep deprivation → reduced focus capacity
- Phone addiction → destroyed deep work capacity
- Childhood screen time → never developed autotelic self
- Distraction → no specific knowledge → no leverage → no wealth

## Connection to Other Threads

This stack connects to [[Desire Is Suffering]] — the attention economy amplifies desire to sell products. It connects to [[Wu Wei]] — true effortless action requires the focus that attention protection provides. It connects to [[The Flow State]] — flow requires the deep concentration that the stack enables.
