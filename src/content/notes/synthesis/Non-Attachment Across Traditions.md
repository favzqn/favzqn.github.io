# Non-Attachment Across Traditions

Five traditions — poetry, venture capital, Taoism, neuroscience, and Islam — teach the same practice: let go of what you're holding onto, and you'll find what you were looking for.

## The Five Paths

### Rilke: Solitude as Creation
[[The Inner Voice]] — "go into yourself and see how deep the place is from which your life arises." [[Solitude and Inner Growth]] — being alone is not loneliness; it's the precondition for creation. [[Living the Questions]] — "be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart." [[Patience and Slowness]] — great things take time. [[Art as Inner Necessity]] — create because you must, not for approval.

### Naval: Desire as the Enemy
[[Desire Is Suffering]] — every desire is a contract for unhappiness. [[Presence]] — the mind exists in motion, not in achievement. [[Meditation]] — "intermittent fasting for the mind." [[Freedom From]] — "freedom from" > "freedom to." [[Happiness Is Learned]] — the default state when nothing is missing.

### Lao Tzu: The Uncarved Block
[[The Uncarved Block]] — original nature before ego carves it. [[Wu Wei]] — action through non-action. [[Emptiness and Fullness]] — the usefulness of empty space. [[Non-Contention]] — winning without fighting. [[Softness Overcomes Hardness]] — water wins by yielding.

### Silva: The Alpha State
[[Alpha Meditation]] — entering relaxed, receptive brain states. [[Mental Screen]] — the internal visualization space. [[The Three Finger Technique]] — anchoring to alpha for instant access. [[Dream Control]] — programming the mind during sleep. [[Creative Problem Solving]] — insight comes when you stop trying.

### The Productive Muslim: Submission as Freedom
[[Productivity and Barakah]] — productivity infused with divine blessing. [[The Amanah Framework]] — time, body, and mind as trusts. [[Tazkiah and Time Management]] — self-purification as productivity. [[Fasting and Productivity]] — Ramadan as training in letting go. [[Productive Focus]] — deep work as worship.

## The Common Thread

Each tradition teaches releasing attachment:
- Rilke: release attachment to external approval
- Naval: release attachment to desire
- Lao Tzu: release attachment to ego's fixed forms
- Silva: release attachment to conscious control
- Islam: release attachment to self-will, submit to purpose

## The Healing Connection

[[The Body Keeps the Score]] shows that trauma is the body's inability to let go. [[Trauma]] creates [[Dissociation]] — the mind's escape when it can't release. [[Window of Tolerance]] narrows when the body holds on. [[Interoception]] — awareness of internal states — is the first step to release. [[Neuroplasticity]] means the brain can learn to let go. [[Yoga for Trauma Recovery]] and [[EMDR and Trauma Processing]] are physical practices of non-attachment.

## The Practice

Non-attachment is not passive. It's the active practice of:
1. Noticing what you're holding onto ([[Interoception]], [[Meditation]])
2. Naming it ([[Labeling]], [[The Power of Disclosure]])
3. Releasing it ([[Wu Wei]], [[Alpha Meditation]])
4. Acting from the cleared space ([[Deep Work]], [[The Flow State]])
