# Business

Wealth creation, entrepreneurship, negotiation, and the psychology of markets. This domain spans the practical (how to build a company) to the philosophical (what wealth is for).

---

## The Books

- [[The Intelligent Investor]] — Benjamin Graham's classic on value investing. The concept of [[Mr. Market]] and [[Margin of Safety]] are the foundation of rational investing.
- [[Shoe Dog]] — Phil Knight's memoir of building Nike. A masterclass in persistence, leverage, and the chaos of entrepreneurship.
- [[The Personal MBA]] — Josh Kaufman's framework for understanding business without an MBA. Covers [[The Iron Law of the Market]], value creation, and systems thinking.
- [[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant]] — Naval's philosophy of wealth: [[Specific Knowledge]], [[Leverage]], and the distinction between wealth and status.

---

## Key Concepts

- [[Margin of Safety]] — buy below intrinsic value; protect against downside
- [[Mr. Market]] — the allegorical market participant who offers prices, not value
- [[Specific Knowledge]] — knowledge you can't be trained for; your unfair advantage
- [[Leverage]] — code, media, capital, labor: the four forms of leverage
- [[The Iron Law of the Market]] — find demand before building supply
- [[Compound Interest]] — the eighth wonder; wealth grows exponentially over time

---

## The Convergence

The business books in this garden share a surprising insight: **the best business advice is philosophical.** Graham teaches emotional discipline. Knight teaches surrender to uncertainty. Naval teaches that wealth is a means, not an end. Kaufman teaches that understanding systems matters more than tactics.
