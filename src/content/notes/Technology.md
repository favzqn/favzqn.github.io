# Technology

The relationship between humans and their tools — from the smartphone to the AI agent. This domain explores how technology reshapes attention, work, and childhood.

---

## The Books

- [[Digital Minimalism]] — Cal Newport's philosophy of intentional technology use. The [[Digital Declutter]] as a 30-day reset. [[Solitude]] as freedom from input.
- [[The Anxious Generation]] — Jonathan Haidt's diagnosis of the phone-based childhood. [[The Great Rewiring]] — the shift from play-based to phone-based childhood has produced an epidemic of anxiety and depression.
- [[The Solo Climb]] — Ajey Gore's article on AI engineering harnesses. [[Internalised Harness]], [[Load-Bearing Harness]], and the [[Agent-of-Agent Review]] pattern.

## The Tension

The garden holds two truths about technology simultaneously:
1. **Technology is a tool** — digital minimalism is not about rejecting technology but about using it intentionally
2. **Technology is a threat** — the attention economy, social media, and AI can erode the very capacities (deep work, flow, solitude) that make us human

The resolution is not Luddism but discernment. Use technology to amplify your values, not replace them.
