# The Harness Question

The question that comes before the agent question and the headcount question: *if the senior engineer goes quiet for two weeks, what holds?*

---

## The Question

*If the senior engineer goes on holiday for two weeks and the agents keep shipping, do I trust what comes out the other side?*

## The Answers

- **Yes** → the harness is strong enough. Climb.
- **"Mostly, but..."** → the harness is not strong enough. The "but" is the gap that becomes the incident.
- **No** → the climb is not ready. Build the harness first.

## The Strategic Error

Most teams ask the agent question first ("which model should we use?") or the headcount question first ("how many engineers do we need?"). The harness question comes first because without it, the 100x shape is just a faster way to ship bugs.

## For Founders

Ask the harness question before the agent question. Not the headcount question. The harness question. *If the senior engineer goes quiet for two weeks, what holds?* If the answer is *nothing solid,* you don't have a team. You have a climber and a rope and a wish.

## For Senior Engineers

Check your gear before you leave the ground. The agents are not going to check it for you, and the team that used to is gone.

> The climb without the harness isn't ambition. It's a fall in slow motion.
