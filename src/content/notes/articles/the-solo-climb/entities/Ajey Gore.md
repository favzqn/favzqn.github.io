# Ajey Gore

Engineering leader and writer. Author of the "entangling exuberance" blog at ajeygore.in. Writes about AI-native engineering practices, org design, and the intersection of AI agents with software development.

---

## Key Ideas

- **The 100x shape**: solo senior engineer + AI agents doing the work of eight — real but requires a load-bearing harness
- **The harness question**: what holds if the senior engineer goes quiet for two weeks?
- **Internalised harness**: Honnold didn't remove the rope, he internalised it — the same applies to engineering safety systems
- **Harness as enabler**: the harness isn't the recovery plan; it's what makes speed possible
