# Cognitive Load Technique

A lie detection and behavioral analysis method based on a simple principle: lying is harder than telling the truth. Increase the mental burden on the person and watch for cracks.

**Note:** This page exists as a redirect/alias. The canonical note is [[Lie Detection (Cognitive Load)]].

---

## The Core Idea

Lying requires extra cognitive effort — maintaining the false story, monitoring your own body language, reading the listener's reactions, and suppressing the truth. The cognitive load technique exploits this by increasing the mental burden until the liar's performance degrades.

See [[Lie Detection (Cognitive Load)]] for the full treatment, including:
- Establishing a baseline
- Increasing cognitive load through unexpected questions
- Watching for incongruence and timing mismatches
- The practical applications

---

## Beyond Lie Detection

Cognitive load is also relevant in:
- **Education**: Overloading students with too much information at once impairs learning ([[A Mind for Numbers]])
- **UX design**: Too many choices produce decision fatigue
- **Negotiation**: [[Never Split the Difference]] — calibrated questions increase the other party's cognitive load
- **[[The Pomodoro Technique]]**: Works partly by limiting cognitive load to manageable 25-minute bursts
